## Competitor Teardowns (5–7)
1. **Ritual (Synbiotic+)**
   - *Block Order:* Hero (Product) -> Logo farm -> 3-in-1 formula benefits -> Transparency/Tracing -> Reviews -> CTA
   - *Gaps:* Lacks detailed scientific explanations of *why* the 3-in-1 works for specific conditions. No interactive tools for symptom checking.
2. **Seed (DS-01 Daily Synbiotic)**
   - *Block Order:* Hero (Science focus) -> Viability/Survivability -> Microbiome benefits -> Subscription model -> FAQ
   - *Gaps:* Overly technical for beginners. Missing practical lifestyle advice alongside the supplement.
3. **Athletic Greens (AG1)**
   - *Block Order:* Hero (All-in-one) -> Foundational health -> Ingredient breakdown -> Testimonials/Celebrities -> Guarantee -> CTA
   - *Gaps:* Jack-of-all-trades, not specifically focused on digestive health. Very high price point.
4. **Hum Nutrition (Flatter Me)**
   - *Block Order:* Hero (Quiz CTA) -> Product features -> How it works (Enzymes) -> Ingredients -> Reviews -> Cross-sell
   - *Gaps:* Heavily focused on aesthetics ("flatter stomach") rather than actual digestive health and science.
5. **Thorne (Gut Health)**
   - *Block Order:* Hero -> Category grid -> Educational content -> Top products -> Quality guarantee
   - *Gaps:* Clinical and sterile. Overwhelming choice. No clear starting point for someone with general symptoms.
6. **Zoe (Personalized Nutrition)**
   - *Block Order:* Hero -> How it works (testing) -> Science/Studies -> App features -> Pricing -> Reviews
   - *Gaps:* High barrier to entry (requires testing). Focuses on diet rather than simple supplementation.

## Positioning
- **Selected Positioning:** "Science-backed, zero-fluff digestive solutions for everyday balance." We focus on root causes (motility, acid, microbiome) rather than just "de-bloating" aesthetics, providing clear, sourced information and targeted tools.
- **Rejected 1:** "The only supplement you'll ever need." (Too scammy, overpromising).
- **Rejected 2:** "Get a flatter tummy in 7 days." (Too aesthetic-focused, ignores real health issues).
- **Rejected 3:** "Clinical-grade interventions for severe GI distress." (Too medical, alienates general audience).
- **Rejected 4:** "Personalized microbiome sequencing." (Too high barrier to entry, complicated).
