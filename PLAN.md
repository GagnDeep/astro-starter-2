## 2. Competitor Teardown
1. **Monash University FODMAP Diet** - The gold standard for food data, but terrible as a daily tracker. Clunky UI. Missing gap: No real predictive symptom analysis.
2. **Cara Care** - Beautiful UI, focuses on GI issues, but too medicalised. Missing gap: Lacks specific actionable guidance on FODMAP stacking.
3. **Bowelle** - Simple, Apple Health integration, but very basic. Missing gap: Doesn't link the food eaten directly to the FODMAP groups.
4. **MySymptoms** - Incredibly detailed but overwhelming. Looks like an Excel spreadsheet. Missing gap: User experience is hostile to people in a flare-up.
5. **Nerva** - Hypnotherapy focused, completely ignores the diet side. Missing gap: No food tracking at all.
6. **Bearable** - Great general tracker, but not specific enough for IBS/FODMAPs. Missing gap: Doesn't calculate FODMAP load.
7. **Fast FODMAP** - Just a lookup tool, no tracking. Missing gap: Doesn't track symptoms over time.
