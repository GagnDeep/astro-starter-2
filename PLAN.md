# Property Investing Education Hub Plan

1. "How do I start investing in property with little money?"
2. "What's the best way to calculate rental yield?"
3. "Is it better to buy a buy-to-let in my own name or a limited company?"
4. "How much deposit do I actually need for an investment property?"
5. "Can I use equity in my current home to buy a second property?"
6. "What are the hidden costs of being a landlord?"
7. "How do I find below market value (BMV) properties?"
8. "What happens if my tenant stops paying rent?"
9. "How do section 24 tax changes affect landlords?"
10. "Should I invest for capital growth or rental yield?"
11. "What is an HMO and is it worth the hassle?"
12. "How do I flip a house successfully?"
13. "Where are the best property hotspots right now?"
14. "What do I need to know before going to a property auction?"
15. "How do I manage a property myself vs using a letting agent?"
16. "Is commercial property better than residential?"
17. "What is the BRRRR method and does it work?"
18. "How do I get a buy-to-let mortgage if I'm self-employed?"

20. "How to deal with rogue tenants?"

**Jargon they never use:**
- Cap rate (they use yield)
- Amortization (they say mortgage term/repayment)
- CRE (they say commercial property)

## Competitor Teardown
1. **Property Hub**
   - Homepage: Hero, Podcast, Free Courses, Paid Courses, Community, Footer.
   - Tone: Friendly, accessible, slightly informal.
   - Biggest Gap: Deeply technical interactive tools are missing; overly reliant on forum/podcast for answers.
2. **BiggerPockets (US but relevant)**
   - Homepage: Hero, Calculator CTA, Forums, Books/Pro, Footer.
   - Tone: Community-driven, intense, slightly sales-heavy.
   - Biggest Gap: UK specific tax and legal nuances are absent.
3. **Property Tribes**
   - Homepage: Forum list, Recent topics, Ads, Footer.
   - Tone: Raw, unfiltered, sometimes cynical.
   - Biggest Gap: Very poor UX/UI, no structured educational pathways.
4. **Property Geek**
   - Homepage: Hero, Latest posts, Email capture, Footer.
   - Tone: Analytical, personal, calm.
   - Biggest Gap: Infrequent updates, lacks broad reference material.
5. **Progressive Property**
   - Homepage: Hero video, Event signup, Courses, Testimonials, Footer.
   - Tone: High-energy, hype-driven, wealth-focused.
   - Biggest Gap: Feels like a hard sell; lacks objective, calm analytical tools.

## Positioning & Voice
**Chosen Positioning:** The analytical, calm, and mathematically sound approach to UK property investment. We focus on numbers, legal realities, and long-term wealth preservation, not "get rich quick" hype.

**Rejected Alternatives:**
1. The "Hustle" approach (too high-energy, associated with scams).
2. The Community-first approach (requires massive moderation, hard to bootstrap).
3. The purely academic approach (too dry, doesn't convert to agents/courses).
4. The news-aggregator (low value-add, hard to monetize).

**Voice Rules:**
- Be precise, neutral, and authoritative.
- Use bullet points and tables for clarity.
- **Banned Words:** Hustle, guarantee, easy money, hack, secret, explode, overnight, guru.

## Capture Strategy
- **Hero:** Waitlist for the advanced course.
- **Tools:** Results sent via email (Lead gen for agents).
- **Reference/Blog:** Inline contextual guides (e.g., "Download the limited company checklist").
- **Footer:** General newsletter.

## Homepage Block Order
1. **Hero:** "Data-Driven Property Investment Education." + Waitlist Capture.
2. **The Problem:** "Why most landlords lose money." (Data points on hidden costs).
3. **The Solution (Tools):** "Do the math before you buy." (Links to calculators).
4. **Structured Learning:** "Start with the foundations." (Links to reference library).
5. **The Blog:** "Latest analysis."
6. **Capture:** "Get the weekly market breakdown."
7. **Footer.**

(This differs from competitors by putting the problem and tools before the courses/content).
