## Competitor Teardowns (5-7)

### 1. The Spruce Eats
- **Homepage Block Order:** Featured Articles -> Trending -> Seasonal Recipes -> Editors' Picks -> Newsletter.
- **Gaps:** Lacks deep comparison of online cooking courses. Tools are basic. Too recipe-focused, not enough structured learning or technical reviews.

### 2. Serious Eats
- **Homepage Block Order:** Latest -> Deep Dives -> Techniques -> Equipment Reviews -> Recipes.
- **Gaps:** Excellent deep dives but lacks structured curriculums or course reviews. Equipment reviews are great but missing interactive comparison tools for readers to input their budget/needs.

### 3. America's Test Kitchen
- **Homepage Block Order:** Paywall CTA -> Recent Reviews -> Popular Recipes -> TV Show episodes.
- **Gaps:** Highly gated content. Complex navigation. We can beat them on free accessibility, interactive calculators, and transparent methodology.

### 4. Epicurious
- **Homepage Block Order:** Seasonal -> Recipes -> Ingredients -> Buying Guides.
- **Gaps:** Pivot to recipes has left their technical guides behind. Equipment reviews are often shallow roundups. No course reviews.

### 5. Food52
- **Homepage Block Order:** Shop CTA -> Articles -> Recipes -> Community.
- **Gaps:** E-commerce driven. Biased towards their own shop. Content is lifestyle-heavy, lacking rigorous technical testing and course reviews.

### 6. Wirecutter (Kitchen Section)
- **Homepage Block Order:** Top Picks -> Categories -> Recent Reviews.
- **Gaps:** Excellent testing, but generalist. Doesn't tie equipment back to specific culinary techniques or courses.

## Positioning
- **Chosen:** The analytical, rigorous guide for serious home cooks bridging the gap to professional techniques. We review courses and gear based on curriculum value and technical performance.
- **Rejected 1:** General recipe site for weeknight dinners. (Too crowded)
- **Rejected 2:** Pure affiliate roundups of Amazon kitchen gadgets. (Low quality)
- **Rejected 3:** Luxury lifestyle food blog. (Lacks technical depth)
- **Rejected 4:** Pure B2B restaurant equipment review. (Wrong audience)

## Voice & Banned Words
- **Voice:** Direct, technical, encouraging, objective, precise.
- **Banned Words:** "Delicious," "yummy," "mouth-watering," "must-have," "game-changer," "whip up," "simply." (We use precise culinary terms and objective measurements instead.)
