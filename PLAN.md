## 2. Competitor Teardowns
1. **NerdWallet**: Homepage: Hero (search) -> Top picks (credit cards, brokers) -> Articles. Gaps: Heavy focus on credit cards; investing feels buried.
2. **Investopedia**: Homepage: Hero (news) -> Dictionary terms -> Basic guides. Gaps: Too academic, overwhelming navigation, intimidating for absolute beginners.
3. **The Motley Fool**: Homepage: Hero (stock picks) -> News -> Premium upsell. Gaps: Extremely sales-heavy, aggressive stock-picking bias, low focus on indexing for beginners.
4. **Bankrate**: Homepage: Hero (rates) -> Calculators -> Comparisons. Gaps: Investing is a secondary category to mortgages and savings accounts.
5. **Morningstar**: Homepage: Hero (market commentary) -> Fund ratings -> Articles. Gaps: Designed for professionals/advanced retail investors; terminology is too complex.

## 4. Positioning & Voice
**Positioning:** The calm, objective classroom for the first-time investor. We don't sell stock picks; we sell financial literacy and structural understanding.
**Rejected Positioning:**
- The aggressive stock picker (too risky).
- The institutional quant (too complex).
- The anti-establishment crypto bro (wrong audience).
- The generic personal finance blog (too broad).

**Voice:** Calm, authoritative, patient, and precise. No hype.
**Banned Words:** "To the moon", "guaranteed", "get rich quick", "bet", "play the market", "hacks", "secrets".
