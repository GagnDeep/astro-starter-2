# Plan

## Research & Positioning
**Problem phrasing:**
- "How much does it cost to build a fitness app?"
- "I need a developer for my gym app."
- "Custom workout tracker app development."
- "Integrate Apple Health into my React Native app."
- "Hire a team to build a fitness community app."

**Competitor Teardown:**
1. *Mindbody (B2B SaaS)* - Focuses on studio management. High cost, rigid structure. Missing: Custom app feel.
2. *Trainerize* - Good for personal trainers, templated apps. Missing: Full bespoke features (wearables, custom AI).
3. *Appinventiv* - Agency. Good case studies, but generic "we build apps" tone. Missing: Specific fitness domain expertise highlighted in pricing.
4. *Cleveroad* - Agency. Comprehensive guides. Missing: Interactive calculators, real transparent pricing on the homepage.
5. *TopTal* - Freelance network. High quality. Missing: Cohesive product team for fitness specifically.

**Positioning:**
We are the premier boutique development agency specifically for fitness and health tech. We don't just "build apps", we understand Apple HealthKit, Google Fit, wearable integrations, and HIPAA compliance for health data.
*Rejected alternatives:*
- Cheap templated app builder (too low margin)
- Generalist software agency (no differentiation)
- B2B SaaS for gyms (different business model)
- Freelance marketplace (lack of cohesion)

**Voice Rules:**
- Expert, direct, transparent, authoritative.
- Banned words: "Synergy", "Ninja", "Guru", "Cheap", "Easy", "Quick fix".
