# Plan: FoodLogTrack.com

## Positioning
A simple, fast, and highly educational food logging and diet tool ecosystem. Unlike bloated apps (MyFitnessPal) that gate calculators behind paywalls, FoodLogTrack provides elite-tier, primary-sourced calculators and reference guides for free, monetizing via high-quality display ads and contextual affiliate integrations (supplements, meal prep delivery).

Rejected positioning:
1. Hardcore bodybuilder tracker (too niche).
2. Paid subscription app (competes with established giants).
3. "Magic" AI diet generator (violates no-bs, primary sourced constraint).
4. Medical/clinical nutrition tracker (too much liability).

## Voice
Direct, educational, authoritative, and encouraging. Irreverent when discussing fad diets, but strictly serious and zero-humor on tax dates, numerical calculations, warnings, and legal disclaimers.
Banned words: "leverage", "utilize", "synergy", "hack", "miracle", "cleanse".

## Competitor Teardowns
1. **MyFitnessPal**: Heavy on subscription upsells, slow load times, gated macro breakdowns. *Gap*: Our tools are 100% free, faster, and instantly accessible without login.
2. **Cronometer**: Extremely detailed micronutrient data, but complex for beginners. *Gap*: We focus on macros and calories first with an intuitive UI.
3. **FatSecret**: Completely free, but outdated UI and community-focused. *Gap*: Modern, mobile-first design with high-quality editorial content.
4. **Lose It!**: Gamified, but restricts basic logging features in the free tier. *Gap*: All core logging and calculations are free.
5. **YAZIO**: Aggressive onboarding quiz before showing any value. *Gap*: Show calculators immediately, ask for email/capture after providing value.
6. **TDEE Calculator.net**: Great tool, but visually dated and lacks a full logging ecosystem. *Gap*: Integrated experience from calculating TDEE to logging food.
