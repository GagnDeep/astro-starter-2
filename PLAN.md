## 4. Figures & Primary Sources
- **Capital Gains Tax Discount:** 50% for individuals holding >12 months (Source: ATO).
- **Average Rental Yield (Gross):** ~3-5% for capital city houses (Source: CoreLogic).
- **LMI:** Applies when LVR > 80% (Source: APRA).
- **Depreciation:** 2.5% over 40 years for properties built post-1987 (Source: ATO).
