# PLAN

## Positioning
**Target Audience**: Health-conscious individuals, athletes, seniors, and office workers seeking a simple, reliable way to track hydration, receive reminders, and understand their fluid needs.
**Positioning**: A no-nonsense, science-backed hydration tracking web app that works offline, requires no downloads, and explains *why* you need water, not just *how much*.
**Rejected Positioning**:
1. Gamified water tracker with avatars (too distracting).
2. Subscription-based tracking app (we are free/freemium web-first).
3. Medical diagnostic tool (too much liability).
4. Social network for water drinking (unnecessary).

## Voice & Tone
**Voice**: Encouraging, clear, authoritative, scientific yet accessible.
**Banned words**: "Just drink more water", "toxins", "detox", "miracle", "hack", "flush", "secret".

## Competitor Teardowns
1. **Waterllama**:
   - *Homepage*: Hero (App Store links) -> Features (gamification) -> Testimonials -> Footer.
   - *Gaps*: Lacks detailed educational content on *why* hydration matters based on age/weight/activity. Missing robust web version.
2. **Plant Nanny**:
   - *Homepage*: Hero (Cute graphics) -> How it works -> Download -> Footer.
   - *Gaps*: Very gamified, low scientific education. No tools for calculating specific sweat loss.
3. **Hydro Coach**:
   - *Homepage*: Hero -> Benefits -> Features -> Download -> Footer.
   - *Gaps*: Heavy Android focus, limited web presence. Calculators are hidden inside the app.
4. **MyWater**:
   - *Homepage*: Hero -> Stats/Awards -> Features -> Download.
   - *Gaps*: Pushy subscription model, articles are generic.
5. **Drink Water Reminder**:
   - *Homepage*: Minimal, just download links.
   - *Gaps*: Missing entirely on the educational front (reference library, glossary, posts).
