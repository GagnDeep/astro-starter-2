## Strategy
A simple online document viewer for shared files. Focuses on speed, privacy, and ease of use. Target audience: professionals, students, freelancers who need to quickly view documents without downloading heavy software.

## Competitor Teardown
1. **Google Docs/Drive Viewer**: Requires login for best experience. Slow load for large files.
   - *Gaps*: Forced ecosystem, privacy concerns.
2. **Microsoft 365 Online**: Clunky, requires MS account.
   - *Gaps*: Account wall, slow.
3. **Smallpdf / iLovePDF**: Ad-heavy, limited free usage, focuses on conversion.
   - *Gaps*: Upsell heavy, UI clutter.
4. **DocHub**: Geared towards signing, not just viewing.
   - *Gaps*: Too complex for simple viewing.
5. **Zoho Viewer**: Good, but often overlooked.
   - *Gaps*: Brand recognition.

## Positioning
**Chosen Positioning:** The fastest, most secure way to view any document in your browser. No sign-ups, no tracking, just your file.
**Rejected Positioning:**
1. Document management system.
2. PDF editor and converter tool.
3. E-signature platform.
4. Collaboration workspace.
