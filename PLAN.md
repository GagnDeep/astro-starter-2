**Competitor Teardown:**
1.  **Cayman Compass:** News-heavy, local focus, cluttered homepage. Weak on evergreen expat finance tools.
2.  **Cayman News Service:** Raw news, political focus, basic design.
3.  **Loop Cayman:** General Caribbean news, very broad, ad-heavy.
4.  **Mondaq/Lexology (Cayman sections):** Too legalistic, hard to read, written by lawyers for lawyers.

**Biggest Gap:** A specialized, authoritative, clean platform focused *exclusively* on business, finance, and expat practicalities, without the distraction of local crime or general Caribbean news. Deep tools and verified evergreen guides.

## 2. Positioning & Voice

**Positioning:** The definitive, no-nonsense business and financial journal for the Cayman Islands expat and corporate community. We provide clarity on complex regulations, actionable insights for businesses, and authoritative guides for professionals relocating to or living in Cayman.

**Rejected Alternatives:**
1.  *General local news site:* Too much competition, low CPM, dilutes the high-value business focus.
2.  *Pure legal blog:* Too dry, misses the expat lifestyle/finance angle.
3.  *Tourist/Travel guide:* Completely wrong audience (we want residents and investors, not cruisers).
4.  *Crypto-only hub:* Too narrow, misses traditional finance, real estate, and general business.

**Voice Rules:**
*   Authoritative, precise, objective, professional.
*   **Banned Words:** "Tax haven", "loophole", "shady", "secretive", "paradise" (too cliché), "cheap".

    *   Footer: Simple newsletter form everywhere.

## 5. Homepage Block Order

1.  **Hero:** Value proposition ("Clarity for Cayman Business & Finance") + Newsletter Capture.
2.  **Market Data / Tools Strip:** Quick links to the Salary Calculator, PR Points Calculator, Stamp Duty Calculator.
3.  **Deep Dive Guides:** Featured 3 reference articles (e.g., Economic Substance, Company Formation).
4.  **Latest Analysis (Blog):** Recent business news and regulatory updates.

6.  **Footer:** Navigation, Legal, simple capture.
(This order differs from competitors who usually put breaking news/crime at the very top).
