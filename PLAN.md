# Plan for Institute for Tax

Target audience: CPAs, EAs, and independent tax preparers looking to scale their practice.
How they actually phrase the problem (Verbatim & Themes):

2. "How do I find clients with complex returns who are willing to pay $500+?"

4. "Shared leads from Bark or Thumbtack are a race to the bottom."
5. "I bought tax resolution leads but they were all tire kickers who couldn't afford my retainer."
6. "How many leads do I need to close one new tax prep client?"
7. "I want predictable growth during tax season without learning Facebook ads."
8. "What is the average cost per acquisition for a CPA client?"
9. "SEO takes too long, I need clients before April 15th."
10. "Can I just buy exclusive tax leads?"
11. "Where do successful EAs get their clients?"
12. "Are live transfer tax leads worth the cost?"
13. "I need high-income individuals with multiple K-1s."
14. "My referral pipeline dried up this year."
15. "Should I hire a marketing agency for my accounting firm or buy leads?"
16. "What's the best way to get virtual tax preparation clients?"
17. "I want to sell my practice in 5 years, I need to grow my book of business now."
18. "Do inbound phone calls convert better than form fills for tax prep?"
19. "How do I filter out low-income tax shoppers?"

*Jargon they never use*: "Omnichannel synergistic lead acquisition", "Growth hacking my tax firm", "B2C accounting funnels".
*Jargon they do use*: Book of business, realization rate, 1040s, K-1s, 1120S, EA, PTIN, tax resolution, OIC (Offer in Compromise).

## Competitor Teardown
1. **Thumbtack / Bark**
   - *Homepage*: Search bar for local services.
   - *Pricing*: Pay per lead (credits), cheap ($15-$40) but shared with 4+ others.
   - *Tone*: Consumer-first, commoditized.
   - *Biggest gap*: Leads are hyper price-sensitive. Tax pros hate the "race to the bottom" bidding war.
2. **TaxProMarketer**
   - *Homepage*: Heavy focus on coaching, SEO, and full agency services.
   - *Pricing*: High monthly retainer ($1,000+).
   - *Tone*: Educational, authoritative, agency.
   - *Biggest gap*: They sell the shovel (marketing), not the gold (leads). It takes 6+ months to see ROI.
3. **GoodAccountants**
   - *Homepage*: Matchmaking service for business owners.
   - *Pricing*: High upfront fee / percentage of billing.
   - *Tone*: Exclusive network.
   - *Biggest gap*: Very expensive, long sales cycle, not suited for high-volume tax prep growth.
4. **CountingWorks PRO**
   - *Homepage*: Focus on website building and software.
   - *Pricing*: SaaS subscription.
   - *Tone*: Tech-forward, modernizing the firm.
   - *Biggest gap*: Lead gen is a secondary byproduct of having a good website, not a direct spigot you can turn on.
5. **LeadGenius / Broad B2B Providers**
   - *Homepage*: Enterprise data solutions.
   - *Pricing*: Custom, enterprise.
   - *Tone*: Corporate, abstract.
   - *Biggest gap*: Not tailored to the specific nuances of tax resolution or tax prep.

## Positioning & Rejected Alternatives
**Positioning**: Institute for Tax is the direct pipeline to high-value tax clients for CPAs and EAs. We don't sell coaching, we don't sell websites, we don't sell retainers. We sell exclusive, pre-qualified tax preparation and resolution leads. Stop marketing; start closing.
*Rejected Alternatives*:
1. Full-service marketing agency for accountants (Too crowded, long time-to-value).
2. A matching directory for consumers (Hard to build the two-sided marketplace from scratch).
3. A CRM/Software tool for tax pros (We want to sell leads, not software).
4. Shared lead provider (CPAs hate shared leads; leads to low retention).

## Voice Rules
- **Direct, professional, ROI-focused.** Speak peer-to-peer to an educated professional.
- **Banned Words**: "Growth hack", "synergy", "ninja", "guru", "cheap", "free leads".
- Emphasize "Exclusive", "High-Intent", "Pre-qualified".

## Capture Strategy
- **Hero**: Direct Waitlist/Quote form ("Get your first 5 exclusive leads").
- **Reference/Glossary Pages**: Sticky sidebar or bottom inline capture ("Download the Tax Firm Growth Guide").
- **Blog Posts**: End of article newsletter/guide capture ("Join 5,000+ CPAs getting weekly growth tips").
- **Tools**: Results sent via email (e.g., "ROI Calculator -> Enter email to see full breakdown").
- **Forms configured via AGENTS.md contract**: `waitlist`, `quote`, `newsletter`, `guide`.

## Homepage Structure
Differing from competitors (who usually start with "Grow your firm" generic fluff):
1. **Hero**: Headline: "Stop Competing on Price. Buy Exclusive Tax Leads That Actually Close." Sub: For CPAs and EAs. Form: Waitlist/Quote.
2. **The "Anti-Shared" Block**: A direct hit on Thumbtack/Bark. "Why shared leads are killing your realization rate."
3. **How It Works (The Filter)**: Show our strict pre-qualification criteria (Income >$100k, Complex Returns, No price shoppers).
4. **Calculator Teaser**: "Calculate your true Cost Per Acquisition".
5. **Testimonials/Data**: Average close rate, average first-year value.
6. **Final CTA**: "Ready for tax season?"
