## Competitor Teardowns
1. **PrivacyGuides**
   - *Block Order:* Header, Table of Contents, OS Recommendations, Hardware Recommendations, Footer.
   - *Gap:* Very dry, hard to know what to actually buy for non-technical users. No price tracking or easy comparisons.
2. **Restore Privacy**
   - *Block Order:* Hero, "Best Of" List, Detailed Reviews, FAQ, Sidebar Ads.
   - *Gap:* Too much affiliate filler, UI is cluttered, lacks interactive tools like a threat-model quiz.
3. **TechRadar (Privacy Phone Guide)**
   - *Block Order:* Hero, Quick Summary, Individual Reviews, Buying Advice, Price Widgets.
   - *Gap:* Recommends mainstream phones (like iPhone) as "private", misses the truly secure OS options, purely commercial intent.
4. **GrapheneOS Official Site**
   - *Block Order:* Features, Technical Overview, Supported Devices, Install Guide.
   - *Gap:* Highly technical, intimidating for average buyers, zero hardware comparison outside Pixels.
5. **The Privacy Dad**
   - *Block Order:* Blog feed, Newsletter signup, Recent Reviews, Footer.
   - *Gap:* Poor navigation, missing a structured reference library.
6. **Rob Braxman Tech Store**
   - *Block Order:* Featured Products, Video embeds, Testimonials.
   - *Gap:* Primarily a store selling pre-flashed devices, highly opinionated, not a neutral review site.
7. **Wired (Secure Phone Article)**
   - *Block Order:* Editorial Hero, Introduction, Product 1, Product 2, Ads.
   - *Gap:* Shallow research, out of date quickly, no deep-dive tools.
