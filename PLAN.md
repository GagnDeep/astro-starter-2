1. **MasterTheHandpan.com**
   - *Focus:* Courses and lessons.

   - *Gap:* Very course-heavy, less objective buying advice for the instrument itself.
2. **Pantheon Steel (Halo)**
   - *Focus:* Direct manufacturer.
   - *Homepage:* Hero (latest model) -> Featured scales -> About the maker -> Newsletter.
   - *Gap:* Assumes you already know what a handpan is and want their brand.
3. **NovaPans.com**
   - *Focus:* Budget/accessible pans.
   - *Homepage:* Hero (sale/discount) -> Product grid -> Reviews -> FAQ.
   - *Gap:* Tone can feel like dropshipping; less educational depth on *why* to choose certain scales.
4. **SarazHandpans.com**
   - *Focus:* Premium maker.
   - *Homepage:* Hero (artisan vibe) -> Available instruments -> Custom orders -> Blog.
   - *Gap:* Extremely high end, intimidating for absolute beginners.
5. **HandpanCentral.com**
   - *Focus:* General info blog.
   - *Homepage:* Hero (blog list) -> Latest posts -> Categories.
   - *Gap:* Weak structure, poor capture strategy, no interactive tools.
6. **HangBlog.org / Handpan.org (Forums)**
   - *Focus:* Community discussion.
   - *Homepage:* Forum index.
   - *Gap:* Impossible to navigate for structured, step-by-step buying advice.
7. **DiscoveringTheHandpan (similar to MTH)**
   - *Focus:* Lessons.
   - *Gap:* Limited buyer's guide depth.

## Positioning & Rejected Alternatives
**Chosen Positioning:** The definitive, objective, zero-BS buyer's guide and reference library for absolute beginners to intermediate players. We don't sell pans; we help you buy the right one and learn to play it.

**Rejected Alternatives:**
1. *The Artisan Reviewer:* Only reviewing $3000+ custom pans. (Alienates 90% of search traffic).
2. *The Cheap Deals Aggregator:* Pushing Amazon affiliate links for $200 tongue drums. (Ruins trust).
3. *The Course Seller:* Locking all advice behind an email wall. (Hurts organic SEO).
4. *The Mystical Sound Healer:* Overly spiritual tone focusing on chakras. (Alienates secular musicians).

## Voice Rules & Banned Words
- **Voice:** Direct, encouraging, objective, slightly technical but plain-English.
- **Banned Words:** "Hang drum" (except when correcting it to Handpan), "magical", "mystical", "vibe", "cheap" (use "budget-friendly" or "entry-level"), "perfect" (everything is a tradeoff).

## Fact & Figure Verification
- **First Handpan (Hang):** Created in 2000 by Felix Rohner and Sabina Schärer of PANArt in Bern, Switzerland. [Source: PANArt documentation]
- **Cost Range:** Quality entry-level starts around $1,000 - $1,500. High-end custom can exceed $3,000. Amazon $200-$400 ones are often factory stamped and poorly tuned.
- **Frequency:** A=440Hz is standard international pitch. A=432Hz is alternative.
- **Materials:** Nitrided steel (warm, ceramic, shorter sustain, needs rust oil), Stainless steel (bright, long sustain, highly rust resistant), Ember Steel (hybrid properties).
