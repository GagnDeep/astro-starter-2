## Competitor Teardown
1. **Seed (seed.com)**
   - *Homepage Block Order:* Hero (Product) -> Scientific validation -> What's inside (Pre/Pro) -> Survivability -> Sustainability -> Reviews -> CTA.
   - *Pricing:* $49.99/mo subscription.
   - *Tone:* Highly clinical, academic, authoritative.
   - *Biggest Gap:* Too scientific for the average consumer who just wants to stop bloating. Overwhelming terminology.
2. **Ritual (Synbiotic+) (ritual.com)**
   - *Homepage Block Order:* Hero (Benefit focused) -> Transparency/Ingredients -> 3-in-1 explanation -> Clinical trial results -> Reviews.
   - *Pricing:* $39/mo subscription.
   - *Tone:* Clean, minimalist, lifestyle-focused, transparent.
   - *Biggest Gap:* The product is generalized; it lacks specific guidance for severe issues or daily variations.
3. **Athletic Greens (AG1) (drinkag1.com)**
   - *Homepage Block Order:* Hero (All-in-one) -> Social proof/Athletes -> 75 ingredients list -> Habit building -> Guarantee/Pricing.
   - *Pricing:* $79/mo subscription.
   - *Tone:* High-performance, energetic, habit-driven.
   - *Biggest Gap:* Overwhelming "kitchen sink" approach; expensive, and not specifically optimized *just* for gut health (it's a multivitamin hybrid).
4. **Zoe (zoe.com - testing/nutrition)**
   - *Homepage Block Order:* Hero (Personalized) -> How it works -> Test kit details -> Science/Founders -> Success stories.
   - *Pricing:* Expensive upfront test ($294) + membership.
   - *Tone:* Scientific yet accessible, highly personalized.
   - *Biggest Gap:* Huge friction to start. Requires testing, blood sugar monitoring, and waiting weeks for results.
5. **Love Wellness (Sparkle Fiber/Gut Feelings) (lovewellness.com)**
   - *Homepage Block Order:* Hero -> Problem/Solution (Bloating) -> Product carousel -> Ingredients -> Reviews -> Education/Blog.
   - *Pricing:* ~$25-$30/bottle.
   - *Tone:* Playful, approachable, female-focused, conversational.
   - *Biggest Gap:* Can feel a bit too informal/influencer-driven, potentially lacking scientific gravity for skeptics.
6. **Hum Nutrition (Flatter Me/Gut Instinct) (humnutrition.com)**
   - *Homepage Block Order:* Hero (Quiz CTA) -> Product Grid -> Expert validation (RDs) -> Reviews -> Quiz CTA.
   - *Pricing:* ~$26/bottle.
   - *Tone:* Millennial pink, quiz-driven, beauty-focused.
   - *Biggest Gap:* Focuses heavily on the aesthetic side of digestion (flat stomach, clear skin) rather than holistic internal health.

## Data & Constants for Tools (Verified & Sourced)
- **Dietary Fiber Intake:** Men under 50: 38g/day. Women under 50: 25g/day. Men over 50: 30g/day. Women over 50: 21g/day. (Source: Institute of Medicine, 2005. Verified 2024-05).
- **Water Intake:** Roughly 3.7 liters for men, 2.7 liters for women total water, minus ~20% from food = ~3L for men, ~2.2L for women. (Source: U.S. National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine. Verified 2024-05).
- **Normal Transit Time:** 24 to 72 hours. Stomach emptying (2-5 hours), small intestine (2-6 hours), colon (10-59 hours). (Source: Mayo Clinic. Verified 2024-05).
- **Bristol Stool Scale:** Types 1-2 (Constipation), Types 3-4 (Ideal), Types 5-7 (Diarrhea). (Source: Bristol Royal Infirmary, 1997).
