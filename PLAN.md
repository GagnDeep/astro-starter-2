# PLAN.md

## Positioning
dogsnacker.com - The healthy, single-ingredient treat subscription for dogs. No junk, no filler, just high-value snacks that dogs actually want, delivered monthly.

**Rejected Alternatives:**
1. Toy and treat mixed box (too crowded by BarkBox, SuperChewer)
2. Custom meal subscription (too complex logistics, high CAC like Farmer's Dog)
3. Cheap bulk treats (bad brand perception, low margins)
4. Boutique artisanal treats only (too niche, low frequency)

- "monthly dog treat box"
- "dog treats subscription no toys"

**Jargon Banned:** "Human-grade" (overused marketing), "Pup-tastic" (banned humor), "Superfood for dogs" (cringe).

## Teardown (BarkBox, PupBox, SuperChewer, BullyMake, The Farmer's Dog)
- BarkBox: heavily focuses on toys and themes. Gap: treats are an afterthought.
- PupBox: age-specific, but high toy-to-treat ratio.
- BullyMake: durability focused. Gap: not for soft chewers or treat-motivated dogs.
- Farmer's Dog: food, not treats. Gap: too expensive for casual treat buyers.
- SuperChewer: durable toys, okay treats.

**Our Gap:** 100% focused on premium, single-ingredient treats. No toys to clutter the house.
