# Battery Product Passport Planning

## Positioning

**Chosen Positioning:** The definitive, engineering-grade compliance authority for EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542) implementation. We provide deterministic guides, calculators, and a verified provider directory for technical teams and compliance officers who need to build or buy a passport solution without marketing fluff.

**Rejected Positioning:**
1. *The "Easy" Solution:* Rejected because compliance is fundamentally complex; oversimplifying loses trust with engineers.
2. *The ESG Marketing Platform:* Rejected because our audience needs technical and legal facts, not greenwashing tools.
3. *A Generic Digital Product Passport (DPP) Wiki:* Rejected because the battery regulation is the first and most stringent; generic DPP advice is too broad and often wrong for batteries.
4. *Software Vendor Content Marketing:* Rejected because we need to remain objective and directory-focused, not a funnel for a specific SaaS tool.

## Voice and Tone

**Voice:** Authoritative, precise, irreverent but strictly factual.
**Tone constraints:** Irreverence is allowed in general copy, but humor is strictly banned on tax dates, numerical calculations, warnings, and legal disclaimers.
**Banned words:** Synergize, seamless, easy, revolutionary, blockchain (unless technically relevant to a provider's architecture), future-proof.

## Competitor Teardowns (5-7)
1. **Circulor**
   - *Homepage Order:* Hero (Sustainability) -> Logos -> Explainer video -> Solutions -> Case Studies -> CTA.
   - *Gaps:* Heavy on high-level sustainability, light on exact technical requirements. Hard to find the actual regulation text. Focuses entirely on their own platform.
2. **Optel Group**
   - *Homepage Order:* Hero (Traceability) -> Industries -> Platform Features -> Webinars -> Contact.
   - *Gaps:* Generic traceability platform. Not battery-specific on the homepage. Lacks practical, free calculators or reference material.
3. **Minespider**
   - *Homepage Order:* Hero (Blockchain DPP) -> How it works -> Battery Passport specific section -> Team -> Newsletter.
   - *Gaps:* Over-indexes on blockchain architecture. Missing a comprehensive directory of alternatives. Articles are sometimes light on legal citations.
4. **Global Battery Alliance (GBA)**
   - *Homepage Order:* Hero (Mission) -> News -> Battery Passport Pilot -> Members -> Reports.
   - *Gaps:* It's an NGO/alliance, so it's very report-heavy. Hard to navigate for a manufacturer looking for a simple compliance checklist or tool. Not actionable.
5. **RCS Global / SLR**
   - *Homepage Order:* Hero (Consulting) -> Services -> News -> About -> Contact.
   - *Gaps:* Traditional consulting firm layout. No self-serve tools or transparent pricing for implementation.
6. **BatteryPassport.org (Systemiq)**
   - *Homepage Order:* Hero (Info) -> Framework -> Reports -> Consortium -> Contact.
   - *Gaps:* Very academic and policy-focused. Lacks a vendor directory or practical software implementation guides.
