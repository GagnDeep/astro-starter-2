## Competitor Teardowns (5-7 named)
1. **DocuSign**:
   - *Order*: Hero (Enterprise focused) > Social Proof (Logos) > Features (Complex) > Use Cases > CTA.
   - *Gaps*: Too expensive for solo, complex UI, no instant free tool without signup.
2. **Adobe Acrobat Sign**:
   - *Order*: Hero (Bundled with Adobe CC) > Integration Logos > Feature grid > Plans > CTA.
   - *Gaps*: Pushes you to Adobe ecosystem, heavy, over-engineered for simple needs.
3. **PandaDoc**:
   - *Order*: Hero (Proposals focus) > Templates > CRM integrations > Social proof > CTA.
   - *Gaps*: Focuses heavily on proposals/sales, not simple document signing.
4. **SignNow**:
   - *Order*: Hero (API/Workflows) > Features > G2 Badges > API focus > CTA.
   - *Gaps*: Poor mobile experience for signees, complex pricing.
5. **Smallpdf (eSign tool)**:
   - *Order*: Hero (Tool interface) > How-to steps > Features > FAQ > SEO text.
   - *Gaps*: Good SEO, but feels like a generic utility, weak audit trails, annoying daily limits.
6. **HelloSign (Dropbox Sign)**:
   - *Order*: Hero (Dropbox integration) > API > Features > Trust > CTA.
   - *Gaps*: Requires Dropbox ecosystem buy-in, expensive tiers.

## Positioning
- **Winning Positioning**: The frictionless, instantly usable e-signature tool that just works for small businesses. Zero signup for your clients, zero enterprise bloat.
- **Rejected 1**: The cheapest DocuSign clone (Race to the bottom).
- **Rejected 2**: The all-in-one document workspace (Scope creep, confusing).
- **Rejected 3**: The API-first signature platform (Wrong audience, too technical).
- **Rejected 4**: The proposal and CPQ engine (PandaDoc's space, too sales-focused).

## Voice & Banned Words
- **Voice**: Direct, reassuring, professional but not stiff. Empathetic to the stress of getting paid/closing a deal.
- **Banned Words**: "Synergy", "Enterprise-grade", "Digital Transformation", "Paradigm Shift", "Revolutionary", "Click here" (accessibility), "Leverage".
