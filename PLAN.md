**Competitor Teardown:**

1. DeepLearning.AI: Math-heavy, academic. Tone: Scholarly. Gap: Immediate practical application for non-ML engineers.
2. Fast.ai: Practical but heavily focused on PyTorch and coding from scratch. Gap: Low-code/No-code tools and higher-level abstractions.
3. DataCamp: Focuses on broad data science. Tone: Professional. Gap: Specialized purely in GenAI and modern LLMs.
4. Coursera (Google/IBM): Slow to update, enterprise focus. Tone: Corporate. Gap: Cutting-edge tools like LangChain, open-weights models.
5. Udacity: Expensive bootcamps. Tone: Salesy. Gap: Accessible, bite-sized reference material and tools.
6. Codecademy: Syntax-focused. Tone: Beginner. Gap: System design and real-world AI architecture.
7. Maven (various AI courses): Fragmented, inconsistent quality. Tone: Creator-driven. Gap: A cohesive, standardized curriculum.
