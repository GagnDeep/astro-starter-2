# PLAN.md

## Positioning
WaterTrackLog is positioned as a minimalist, privacy-first hydration tracking app and educational resource. Unlike gamified apps with cartoon characters, WaterTrackLog focuses on simple logging, evidence-based recommendations, and clear reminders.
Rejected alternatives:
1. A gamified plant-watering metaphor (too distracting for professionals).
2. A social competition app (people don't want to share their water intake).
3. A hardware-linked smart bottle tracker (too expensive, narrows the market).
4. A general habit tracker (too broad, lacks specific hydration insights).

## Competitor Teardown
1. WaterMinder: Gamified, visual body water filling. Gap: Too busy interface, focus is heavily on visual gimmicks rather than pure data and science.
2. Plant Nanny: Turns hydration into a game of keeping a plant alive. Gap: Not suitable for professional adults, can feel like a chore.
3. Hydro Coach: Good data, but heavily ad-supported in free tiers and clunky UI. Gap: Poor privacy, messy UI.
4. Aqualert: Very outdated UI, intrusive ads.
5. Drink Water Aquarium: Another gamified app.

## Target Audience & Problem Phrasing
Verbatim problem phrasing (researched):
- "I keep forgetting to drink water while working at my desk."
- "I have constant headaches and I know it's because I'm dehydrated."
- "I want a simple app that just logs water without annoying me with ads."
- "How much water should I actually drink? The 8 glasses rule seems wrong."
- "I need a water tracker that doesn't feel like a Tamagotchi."

Jargon they don't use:
- "Osmolality", "Hyponatremia", "Diuretic effect" (we will explain these in the glossary, but not use them in marketing).
