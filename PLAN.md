# Plan Document: DogSnacker

## Competitor Teardowns (Homepage Block Order & Gaps)
1. **BarkBox:** Hero -> How it works -> Theme of month -> Reviews -> CTA.
   - *Gaps:* No ingredient transparency on homepage, assumes toys are wanted, filler items.
2. **The Farmer's Dog:** Hero -> Vet proof -> Quiz CTA -> Ingredients -> Reviews -> FAQ.
   - *Gaps:* Focuses heavily on full meals, treats are an afterthought, no treat allowance tools.
3. **Real Dog Box:** Hero -> What's inside (air dried) -> Customization -> Reviews -> FAQ.
   - *Gaps:* Cluttered UI, hard to find nutritional breakdown before signup, relies heavily on text.
4. **PupJoy:** Hero -> Build a box -> Brands -> Reviews.
   - *Gaps:* E-commerce feel, lacks deep nutritional education, no calculators for diet planning.
5. **Super Chewer:** Hero -> Tough toys -> How it works -> Reviews.
   - *Gaps:* Heavily toy-focused, ignores dogs with sensitive stomachs or dietary restrictions.
6. **Bocce's Bakery:** Hero -> Bestsellers -> Categories -> Ingredients -> Footer.
   - *Gaps:* Standard storefront, not tailored subscription, no diagnostic tools for allergies.

## Homepage Block Order (Unique)
1. **Hero (Problem-First):** "Stop guessing what's making your dog sick." (Capture form for waitlist/quote)
2. **The 10% Rule Calculator Teaser:** Interactive slider showing how many calories in treats your dog actually needs.
3. **Ingredient Transparency Grid:** Side-by-side comparison of our single ingredients vs. leading brand fillers.
4. **Allergy Selection:** "Filter by what they can't eat" interactive component.
5. **How the Box Adapts:** Explanation of the subscription lifecycle as the dog ages.
6. **Sourced Vet Figures & FAQs:** Real nutritional requirements (cited).
7. **Footer with Newsletter Capture.**

## Primary Sources for Figures
- 10% Treat Rule: AAFCO / WSAVA guidelines state treats should not exceed 10% of daily caloric intake.
- Hydration: Dogs need ~1 ounce of water per pound of body weight per day.
- Calories: Resting Energy Requirement (RER) = 70 * (body weight in kg)^0.75. Maintenance Energy Requirement (MER) varies by activity level.
