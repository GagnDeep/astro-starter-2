## Positioning

*Selected:* A free, web-based, science-backed breathwork timer and reference library. No app downloads required.
*Rejected 1:* A paid subscription app for guided breathwork. (App market is saturated).
*Rejected 2:* A mystical/spiritual meditation hub. (Focusing on physiology and science builds wider trust).
*Rejected 3:* A hardware companion site. (No physical products to sell).
*Rejected 4:* A purely clinical medical site. (Needs to be accessible to the general public).

## Voice & Banned Words

*Voice:* Clear, scientific, practical, calming, and authoritative.
*Banned Words:* innovative, synergy, disruptive, click here, read more. (As per memory rules).
