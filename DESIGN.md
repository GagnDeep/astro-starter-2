# Design: uconvertfile.com

## Palette & Fonts
- **Palette (No Hex Codes allowed in raw usage):**
  - Background: `var(--color-bg)` (Soft off-white)
  - Text: `var(--color-text)` (Deep slate/charcoal)
  - Primary: `var(--color-primary)` (Forest Green - avoiding fintech indigo)
  - Accent: `var(--color-accent)` (Warm Orange)
  - Muted: `var(--color-muted)` (Light Gray)
- **Fonts:**
  - Display (Banned: Inter): `Merriweather`, serif.
  - Body: `Roboto`, sans-serif.
  - Mono: `Fira Code`, monospace (for all numbers, financial figures, and rates).

## Restrictions
- No stock photos.
- No emoji icons.
- No glassmorphism.
- No gradients.
- All numbers use tabular monospace typography.

## UI Components
- **Buttons:** Solid primary or outlined.
- **Forms:** Clean borders, clear accessible labels, honeypots.
- **Cards:** Simple 1px borders, no heavy shadows.
