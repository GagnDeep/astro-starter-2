# Design Standards

- **Art Direction:** Editorial/Swiss
- **Industry Materials & Palette:** Based on wealth management aesthetics (e.g., leather folios, dark mahogany desks, crisp white paper).
- **Colors (OKLCH):**
  - Brand hue: A deep, authoritative blue-slate (`oklch(0.25 0.05 250)`).
  - Supporting hue: A warm, muted gold or amber (`oklch(0.65 0.12 70)`).
  - Neutral ramp: Chroma restricted to 0.01-0.03 (never pure grey).
- **Typography:**
  - Two self-hosted families (not Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Open Sans, or Lato).
  - Font 1: A clean, authoritative sans-serif for headings (e.g., Helvetica Now, Neue Haas Grotesk).
  - Font 2: A highly legible serif for body text (e.g., Charter, Merriweather, or Georgia if system fallback).
