# Design Direction

**Art Direction:** Technical-brutalist
This direction suits the highly technical and unembellished nature of a B2B aerospace directory and data platform, reinforcing its non-journalistic, pure-data positioning.

**Materials & OKLCH Palette:**
1. **Titanium (Neutral ramp):** Used for surfaces and text. Low chroma. Example: `oklch(0.95 0.02 260)`
2. **Anodized Aluminum / Warning Orange (Brand hue):** Used for primary actions, accents, and the brand color. Applied sparingly (max 10% of painted pixels). Example: `oklch(0.65 0.2 45)`
3. **Aviation Blue / Carbon Fiber (Supporting hue):** Used for secondary accents and depth, positioned ~30-60 degrees away from the brand hue. Example: `oklch(0.4 0.1 240)`

**Typography (Self-hosted, not banned):**
1. **Display:** Clash Display (or equivalent brutalist/technical sans). Line-height 0.95-1.05, tracking -0.02 to -0.035em.
2. **Body:** IBM Plex Sans (or equivalent legible technical sans). Line-height 1.6, measure capped near 65ch.
