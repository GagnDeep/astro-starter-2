# DESIGN

## Art Direction
Technical-brutalist (to fit the regulatory, high-precision nature of battery passports and climate disclosure regulations).

## Industry Materials and Palette (OKLCH)
- **Brand Hue:** Lithium Silver (oklch(0.85 0.05 250))
- **Supporting Hue:** Copper (oklch(0.65 0.15 45))
- **Neutral Ramp:** Graphite (chroma 0.01-0.03, never pure grey) - Example: oklch(0.3 0.02 250)

## Font Families
- Space Grotesk (display)
- IBM Plex Sans (body)
