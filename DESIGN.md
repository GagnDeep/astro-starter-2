# Design Specifications

**Art Direction:** Editorial/Swiss. High founder credibility and clarity.

**Materials for Palette:**
1. Leather Folios (Brand Hue: Deep authoritative blue-slate)
2. Warm Mahogany/Amber (Supporting Hue: Warm, muted gold/amber)
3. Crisp White Paper (Neutral Ramp)

**OKLCH Hues:**
- Brand: `oklch(0.25 0.05 250)` (Deep Blue-Slate)
- Supporting: `oklch(0.65 0.12 70)` (Warm Amber)
- Neutrals: Chroma restricted to 0.01-0.03.

**Fonts:**
- Headings (Display): Helvetica Now, Neue Haas Grotesk, or similarly clean, authoritative sans-serif. (Fallback to local sans-serif).
- Body: Charter, Merriweather, Georgia (fallback).
