# Design Direction

**Art Direction:** Editorial/Swiss
To convey high founder credibility and clarity, the design uses an Editorial/Swiss aesthetic.

**Industry Materials:**
- Leather folios
- Dark mahogany desks
- Crisp white paper

**OKLCH Palette:**
- **Brand Hue:** A deep, authoritative blue-slate (`oklch(0.25 0.05 250)`).
- **Supporting Hue:** A warm, muted gold or amber (`oklch(0.65 0.12 70)`).
- **Neutral Ramp:** Chroma is restricted to `0.01-0.03` (never pure grey).

**Typography:**
- **Headings (Display):** A clean, authoritative sans-serif (e.g., Helvetica Now, Neue Haas Grotesk). `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`, `line-height 0.95-1.05`, `tracking -0.02 to -0.035em`.
- **Body:** A highly legible serif (e.g., Charter, Merriweather). `17-19px`, `line-height 1.6`, measure capped near `65ch`.
