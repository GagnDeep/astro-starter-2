# Design Specifications

**Art Direction:** Technical-brutalist (clean, authoritative, structured)

**Palette Inspiration:** (Industry materials: Recycled paper, raw steel, carbon fiber)

- **Brand Hue:** Dark slate/carbon (OKLCH: 30% lightness, 0.05 chroma, 180 hue)
- **Supporting Hue:** Pale recycled sage (OKLCH: 85% lightness, 0.08 chroma, 140 hue) - approx 40 degrees away from the brand hue.
- **Neutral Ramp:** Chroma 0.01-0.02 (cool grays), avoiding pure grayscale to give a clinical yet distinct technical feel.

**Typography:**

- **Display:** (To be chosen, self-hosted, e.g., a strong serif or technical sans not in the banned list)
- **Body:** (To be chosen, self-hosted, structured and highly legible)

**Additional Rules:**

- Avoid uniform 8px radius. Use sharp corners or very tight radii for a technical feel.
- Dark mode is designed with brand hues at low lightness (never #000).
- Motion is strictly CSS, one-shot scroll reveals (max 20px, opacity, 300-500ms), fast hover states (≤ 200ms).
