# Design Direction

**Art Direction**: Editorial/Swiss

**Justification against Editorial/Swiss**: As an independent document assistance service, trust, clarity, and authority are paramount. Editorial/Swiss prioritizes clean typography, asymmetric layouts, and an absence of superficial decoration (e.g., no glassmorphism, gradient blobs, or heavy shadows). This strictly distinguishes the site from typical flashy start-ups and emphasizes serious, rigorous document checking.

**Palette (OKLCH)**:
- **Materials**: Official Application Forms (ink on white paper), Consular Stamp (deep navy ink), Secure Passports (dark blue textured covers).
- **Brand Hue**: Dark Navy Blue (Consular Stamp/Passport). OKLCH ~ `l: 0.3, c: 0.1, h: 260`.
- **Supporting Hue**: Warm Crimson (Official Wax Seal/Stamp highlight, 30-60 degrees away). OKLCH ~ `l: 0.5, c: 0.15, h: 20`.
- **Neutral Ramp**: Chroma 0.01-0.03, starting from a warm, paper-like off-white (`l: 0.98`) down to deep text (`l: 0.15`).

**Fonts**:
- Self-hosted. (No Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato).
- **Display**: Libre Baskerville or similar high-quality serif to echo official document typography and editorial authority.
- **Body**: Public Sans or similar clean neo-grotesque to handle dense, technical instructions legibly, matching the Swiss aesthetic for structural text.
