## Competitor Teardowns (5-7)
1. **LanguageAcademy**: High volume content, but cluttered homepage. Gap: Clear, immediate value prop for AI scoring.
2. **APEUni**: Feature-rich, overwhelming for beginners. Gap: Simple onboarding, better explanations of AI scores.
3. **PTE Success**: Good mobile app, weak web experience. Gap: Mobile-first web app with seamless capture.
4. **E2Language**: Course-focused, less self-serve AI. Gap: Instant feedback tools without talking to sales.
5. **AlfaPTE**: Cheap, but inaccurate AI. Gap: Trustworthy AI with sourced explanations and edge cases.
6. **PTE Tools**: Very basic UI. Gap: Modern, accessible design with zero CLS.
