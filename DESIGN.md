# Art Direction & Design Law

**Art Direction:** Technical-brutalist (stark, utilitarian, code-first).

**Palette (Three Industry Materials):**
- **Brand Hue:** Warning Orange / Polyimide Tape (Accent color for interactions)
- **Supporting Hue:** Industrial Blue / ESD Matting (30-60 degrees away)
- **Neutral Ramp:** Anodized Aluminum (Chroma 0.01-0.03, never pure grey)

**Typography (Two font families):**
- **Display:** Clash Display (technical, strong)
- **Body:** IBM Plex Sans (highly legible, structural)
*(Note: Excludes banned fonts like Inter, Poppins, Roboto, etc.)*

**Design Rules:**
- The accent covers at most a tenth of painted pixels.
- Dark mode is designed: brand hue at low lightness for surfaces, never #000, 1px top highlight instead of shadows.
- CSS-only motion: one-shot scroll reveal, max one ambient element, respects prefers-reduced-motion.
- Banned: Purple/indigo gradients, glassmorphism, emoji as icons, uniform 8px radius, gradient blobs, AI-generated 3D renders.
