# Design Law

## Art Direction
**Technical-brutalist**
*Justification:* This direction aligns with the no-nonsense, practical, data-driven nature of commercial and specialty underwriting. It avoids the fluff of standard corporate sites, fitting the persona of a trade publication that doesn't pretend to be a professional body.

## Palette
**Industry Materials:**
1. Manilla folder (warm paper)
2. Ink (deep navy/black)
3. Highlighters (sharp neon accent)

**OKLCH Hues:**
- **Brand Hue (Ink):** oklch(25% 0.05 250) - A deep, slightly desaturated navy.
- **Supporting Hue (Manilla):** oklch(90% 0.04 80) - A warm, off-white/beige tone.
- **Neutral Ramp:** Chroma 0.01-0.03, derived from a cool blue-grey (e.g., oklch(x% 0.02 250)). Never pure grey.

*Note:* The accent covers at most a tenth of painted pixels. Dark mode surfaces use the brand hue at low lightness, never pure #000.

## Typography
**Two Self-Hosted Font Families:**
- **Display:** Space Grotesk (A structured, slightly technical sans-serif).
- **Body:** Lora (A highly legible serif suitable for long-form technical reading).

*(Neither Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Open Sans nor Lato are used.)*
