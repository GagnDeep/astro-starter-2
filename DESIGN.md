# Design System

## Art Direction
- **Chosen Direction:** Editorial/Swiss
- **Justification:** Conveys authoritative reference material without feeling like a startup; emphasizes readability, clear hierarchy, and trustworthy institutional structure suited for government contracting topics.

## Palette & Materials
- **Materials (Federal contracting references):**
  - Navy blue suit (Institutional trust)
  - Manila folder (Documentation, filings)
  - Red ink (Approval/correction)
- **OKLCH Hues:**
  - **Brand Hue:** Institutional Navy (`oklch(28% 0.05 255)`)
  - **Supporting Hue:** Deep Red (`oklch(45% 0.15 25)`)
  - **Neutral Ramp:** Chroma 0.01-0.03 (e.g., `oklch(15% 0.01 255)` for text, never pure grey).

## Typography
- **Families:**
  - `Source Serif 4` (or similar self-hosted serif) for Display/Headings (adds editorial/archival authority).
  - `Public Sans` (or similar self-hosted sans) for Body/UI.
- **Sizing:**
  - Display: `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`, line-height 0.95-1.05, tracking -0.02 to -0.035em.
  - Body: 17-19px, line-height 1.6, measure capped near 65ch.
