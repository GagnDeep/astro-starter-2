# Design Standards

## Art Direction
**Technical-brutalist**
We chose a Technical-brutalist art direction because Fish Scanner is a highly technical tool that prioritizes factual compatibility and scientific literature over decorative visuals. It should feel robust, data-centric, and utility-first, reflecting its purpose as a specialized calculator.

## Color Palette (OKLCH)
Derived from three industry materials:
1. **Brand Hue:** `oklch(55% 0.15 250)` - Oceanic Blue (inspired by actinic reef lighting).
2. **Supporting Hue:** `oklch(60% 0.12 210)` - Coral Cyan (inspired by synthetic sea salt mixes), exactly 40 degrees away.
3. **Neutral Ramp:** `oklch(L 0.02 260)` (chroma 0.02, never pure grey) - Slate/Steel (inspired by titanium aquarium heaters and stainless aquascaping tools).

## Typography
1. **Display Family:** `Space Grotesk` (or similar geometric, non-standard sans). Clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem), line-height 1, tracking -0.03em.
2. **Body Family:** `IBM Plex Sans` (or similar technical sans). 18px at line-height 1.6, measure capped at ~65ch.
*(Note: Neither is Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Open Sans, or Lato)*
