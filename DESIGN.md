# Design Specifications

- **Art Direction**: Editorial/Swiss. We focus on structured, highly legible presentation of dense information and comparisons.
- **Palette & Materials**:
  - *Passport (Navy/Blue)*: Brand Hue OKLCH(0.4 0.1 260)
  - *Customs Stamp (Purple)*: Supporting Hue OKLCH(0.5 0.15 300)
  - *Archival Paper (Neutral)*: Neutral Ramp at chroma 0.01-0.03 OKLCH(x 0.02 90)
- **Fonts**:
  - *Display*: Playfair Display (Serif for authoritative headers)
  - *Body*: Source Sans 3 (Highly legible sans-serif for dense data and prose)
