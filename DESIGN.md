# Design Direction

**Art Direction**: Utility-monospace
**Reasoning**: Target audience is operations/business owners, not consumers seeking glossy photography. The design should convey technical rigor, business logic, and practicality.

**Palette (OKLCH)**:
- **Materials**: Stainless steel (neutral/base), Copper (brand/accent), White ceramic (background/contrast).
- **Brand Hue (Copper)**: `oklch(60% 0.15 45)` - Used for primary accents.
- **Supporting Hue (Steel Blue)**: `oklch(55% 0.10 75)` - Used for secondary elements/data points.
- **Neutral Ramp**: Chroma 0.01-0.03 for backgrounds and surfaces.

**Typography**:
- **Display**: A structured monospace or highly technical sans-serif. E.g., `IBM Plex Mono` or `Space Mono`.
- **Body**: A readable, dense geometric sans. E.g., `IBM Plex Sans` or `Work Sans`.
