# Design Direction

**Art Direction:** Editorial/Swiss
*Chosen for its authoritative, structured, and professional feel, fitting a B2B media site for financial principals and compliance leads.*

**Palette:**
Derived from three industry materials (Bank vault steel, executive leather, and crisp parchment paper).
- **Brand Hue (Steel Blue):** OKLCH(0.25 0.05 250) - used for primary accents, links, and high-emphasis elements.
- **Supporting Hue (Leather Brown):** OKLCH(0.40 0.10 40) - used for secondary accents, 30-60 degrees away in hue.
- **Neutral Ramp (Parchment):** Neutral shades built at chroma 0.01-0.03 (never pure grey), providing a crisp, legible background.

**Typography:**
Two self-hosted families (excluding Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato):
- **Display:** *Bricolage Grotesque* (or similar non-banned geometric/grotesque sans)
- **Body:** *Lora* (or similar non-banned serif for editorial readability)

**Banned Elements:**
- Purple or indigo gradients.
- Centered heroes with two centered buttons.
- Glassmorphism.
- Emoji as icons.
- Uniform 8px radius everywhere.
- Gradient blobs.
- Fake avatars.
- "Get Started" as the only CTA.
- AI-generated 3D renders.
