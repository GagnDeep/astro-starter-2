# Design Law

**Art Direction:** Editorial/Swiss

**Industry Materials for Palette:**
- Stainless Steel (Kitchen Prep) -> Neutral ramp at chroma 0.01-0.03
- Terracotta/Clay (Brick Oven) -> Brand hue
- Butcher Block/Wood (Dining Tables) -> Supporting hue 30-60 degrees away

**OKLCH Hues:**
- Brand hue (Terracotta): `oklch(60% 0.15 40)`
- Supporting hue (Wood): `oklch(65% 0.12 70)`
- Neutral ramp (Stainless Steel): `oklch(40% 0.02 260)` to `oklch(95% 0.01 260)`

**Font Families:**
- Display: 'Playfair Display', serif (Self-hosted)
- Body: 'Work Sans', sans-serif (Self-hosted)
