# Design Law

## Art Direction
**Editorial/Swiss** — The design will prioritize clarity, structure, and readability, avoiding embellishments. High contrast and robust grid systems reflect the seriousness of federal contracting while keeping it accessible.

## Palette (Industry Materials)
We derive our palette from the physical and digital materials of federal contracting:
1. **Navy Blueprint** (Brand hue) - OKLCH: 35% 0.1 260. Defines trust and authority.
2. **Manila Folder** (Supporting hue) - OKLCH: 85% 0.1 80 (approx. 50 degrees away from blue/neutral). Used for subtle highlights and secondary elements.
3. **Government Forms/Paper** (Neutral ramp) - OKLCH: Chroma limited to 0.01 - 0.03. Never pure grey. Gives a warm, paper-like feeling.

## Typography
1. **Lora** (Serif) - Used for headings. Provides an editorial, authoritative feel without being overly dense.
2. **Work Sans** (Sans-serif) - Used for body text. Legible, clean, and straightforward.

*(Both are self-hosted and conform to the rule against Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Open Sans, or Lato).*
