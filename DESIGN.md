# Design Law

## Art Direction
**Technical-brutalist** - Chosen to reflect the privacy-focused, evidence-based, and technical nature of the site. It favors function and clarity over ornamentation.

## Palette
**Industry Materials & Hues:**
- **Ledger Paper / E-ink:** (Neutral/Base) `oklch(0.98 0.02 95)`
- **Faraday Bag / Shielding Mesh:** (Brand Hue/Dark) `oklch(0.20 0.05 260)`
- **Copper Trace / Circuitry:** (Accent Hue) `oklch(0.65 0.15 45)` (approx 30-60 degrees offset depending on base definition, used sparingly).
- *Note: A neutral ramp is maintained at chroma 0.01-0.03, avoiding pure grey.*

## Typography
**Two Self-Hosted Families:**
- **Display:** Space Grotesk (or similar stark, technical sans-serif) - Used for headings with clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem), line-height 0.95-1.05, tracking -0.02 to -0.035em.
- **Body:** IBM Plex Mono (or similar readable monospace/technical font) - Used for body text at 17-19px, line-height 1.6, measure capped near 65ch.
*(Note: Excludes Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Open Sans, Lato)*
