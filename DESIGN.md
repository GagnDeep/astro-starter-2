# Design Direction

## Art Direction: Technical-brutalist
- A utilitarian, structured, and function-first design that aligns with the serious nature of exam preparation and visa points calculation.
- Content is arranged with clear boundaries, strong typography, and a low-chrome aesthetic.

## Palette
- **Materials**: Graphite (pencil), Paper, Blueprint.
- **Colors (OKLCH)**:
  - **Brand**: `oklch(0.55 0.15 250)` (a technical blueprint blue)
  - **Supporting**: `oklch(0.60 0.10 200)` (cyan/teal offset, 50 degrees away)
  - **Neutral**: `oklch(0.95 0.01 250)` to `oklch(0.15 0.01 250)` (cool grays, avoiding pure neutral, chroma 0.01)

## Typography
- **Primary / Display**: `Bricolage Grotesque` (or a similar utilitarian sans-serif, not Inter/Roboto/Open Sans). Used at `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)` with a tight line-height (0.95-1.05) and negative tracking (-0.02 to -0.035em).
- **Body**: `Newsreader` (or a high-legibility secondary font, not Lato/Poppins). Sized at 17-19px, line-height 1.6, with a maximum measure capped near 65ch.
