# Aerospace UAE Sitemap & Information Architecture

This sitemap outlines 16 URLs organized in a hub-and-spoke model, designed to capture the long-tail search intent for the UAE's Tier 2/3 aerospace and MRO supply chain.

## Hubs

### 1. Home
- **URL:** `/`
- **Target Query:** Aerospace UAE directory, UAE aerospace MRO jobs
- **Intent:** High-level navigation and brand discovery. Buyers looking for aerospace suppliers; engineers looking for specialized aerospace jobs in the UAE.
- **Page Type:** Homepage
- **Unique Value:** The definitive entry point that clearly separates the buyer journey (directory) from the candidate journey (jobs), setting the independent, technical-brutalist tone of the site.
- **Inbound Links:** Every page on the site (via global navigation).
- **Outbound Links:** `/directory/`, `/jobs/`, `/about/`, `/pricing/`, `/contact/`

### 2. Supplier Directory Hub
- **URL:** `/directory/`
- **Target Query:** UAE aerospace suppliers, UAE aviation MRO companies
- **Intent:** Procurement officers and primes searching for a vetted list of UAE-based Tier 2/3 subcontractors.
- **Page Type:** Hub Page (Directory)
- **Unique Value:** A comprehensive, non-editorialized list of verified UAE aerospace suppliers, categorized by capability.
- **Inbound Links:** `/`, `/pricing/`, `/about/`, and all Directory Spokes.
- **Outbound Links:** `/directory/cnc-machining/`, `/directory/composite-manufacturing/`, `/directory/ndt-services/`, `/directory/surface-treatment/`, `/directory/aviation-logistics/`

### 3. Hiring Board Hub
- **URL:** `/jobs/`
- **Target Query:** Aerospace jobs UAE, aviation engineering jobs Dubai Abu Dhabi
- **Intent:** Specialized engineers and technicians looking for high-quality MRO and aerospace manufacturing roles.
- **Page Type:** Hub Page (Jobs Board)
- **Unique Value:** Strictly focused on aerospace engineering and MRO roles, cutting through generic job board noise.
- **Inbound Links:** `/`, `/pricing/`, `/about/`, and all Jobs Spokes.
- **Outbound Links:** `/jobs/b1-b2-licensed-engineers/`, `/jobs/structures-technicians/`, `/jobs/avionics-specialists/`, `/jobs/quality-inspectors/`, `/jobs/cnc-programmers/`
