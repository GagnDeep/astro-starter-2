## Executive Summary & Clinical Context

Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) affects an estimated **10-15% of the global adult population** (ACG Clinical Guideline, 2021). Managing symptoms effectively requires understanding the physiological mechanisms of fermentable oligosaccharides, disaccharides, monosaccharides, and polyols (FODMAPs).

## Key Clinical Data & Monash Serving Cutoffs

Below is a summary of standard serving thresholds verified by Monash University laboratory testing:

| Food Item | FODMAP Type | Green Serving (Safe) | Red Serving (High Risk) | Clinical Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Garlic** | Fructans | Garlic-infused oil (0g) | 3g (1 clove) | Rapid colonic bacterial fermentation producing $H_2$ & $CH_4$ gas |

## Worked Practical Examples & Meal Scenarios

### Worked Example 1: The "Accidental Stacking" Breakfast
* **Scenario:** A patient consumes 40g oats (Green), 40g avocado (Amber - Sorbitol), 100g ripe banana (Amber - Fructan), and 15g almonds (Amber - GOS).
* **The Result:** Even though each item individually approaches green/amber thresholds, combining four amber/green portions in a single sitting triggers an **osmotic overload** in the ileum, resulting in severe bloating within 90 minutes.
* **The Solution:** Swap ripe banana for firm green banana (100g green), replace avocado with 1 tbsp peanut butter, and keep oats at 40g.

```
Total Meal FODMAP Load = Sum(Individual Serving Ratios)
If Sum >= 1.0 within a 3-hour window -> High Risk of Symptom Flare
```

## Common Mistakes & Clinical Tradeoffs

1. **Mistake: Conflating FODMAPs with Gluten Allergy**
   - *Fact:* Gluten is a protein; fructan is a carbohydrate chain. Many people who feel better on a gluten-free diet are actually reacting to fructans in wheat, not gluten!
2. **Mistake: Staying in Elimination for >8 Weeks**
   - *Fact:* Prolonged restriction reduces beneficial *Bifidobacterium* species by up to **50%** within 4 weeks (British Dietetic Association, 2018).

## Prominent Red-Flag Symptoms Warning

> **RED-FLAG SYMPTOMS:** Consult a gastroenterologist immediately if you experience any of the following:
> - Unexplained involuntary weight loss (>5% body weight in 1 month)
> - Nocturnal diarrhea waking you from sleep
> - Rectal bleeding or black tarry stools
> - Persistent unexplained fevers
> - Onset of bowel habit changes after age 50

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Can I stay on the low-FODMAP diet permanently?
**No.** The elimination phase is strictly time-limited (2 to 6 weeks). The goal is to progress to reintroduction and personalize your diet long term to preserve gut microbiome diversity.

### Q2: What should I do if I accidentally eat garlic or onion?
Do not panic. Stress exacerbates gut motility via the sympathetic nervous system. Drink warm peppermint tea, use a heating pad, practice 4-7-8 diaphragmatic breathing, and return to quiet low-FODMAP meals for 24 hours.
