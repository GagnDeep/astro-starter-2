## The Financing Hurdle

One of the most common questions we receive from investors in Dubai is: *"Can I get a mortgage in the US if I don't have a green card, a social security number, or a US credit score?"*

**The answer is definitively Yes.**

While you cannot walk into a major US retail bank (like Chase or Bank of America) and get a conventional loan, a robust shadow banking and private lending market exists specifically to cater to foreign investors. These are known as **Foreign National Mortgages** or **DSCR Loans**.

## What is a DSCR Loan?

DSCR stands for **Debt Service Coverage Ratio**.

In a conventional mortgage, the lender looks at *your* personal income (W2 salary, tax returns) and *your* personal debts to determine if you can afford the monthly payment.

In a DSCR loan, the lender doesn't care about your personal income in Dubai. They care entirely about the **income of the property you are buying**. If the rent the property generates is high enough to cover the mortgage payment, taxes, and insurance, the loan is approved.

### The Calculation
- **DSCR = Net Operating Income / Total Debt Service**
- A DSCR of 1.0 means the rent exactly covers the mortgage.
- Most lenders require a DSCR of **1.20 or higher** (meaning the property generates 20% more income than the debt costs).

## Typical Terms for UAE Residents

Because the lender cannot easily pursue your assets in the UAE if you default, these loans carry slightly higher risk for the bank. Consequently, the terms are different from a standard US mortgage:

1. **Higher Down Payment:** Expect to put down **30% to 40%** in cash. Lenders want you to have significant "skin in the game" so you don't walk away from the property.
2. **Interest Rates:** Rates are typically 1.0% to 2.0% higher than conventional rates.
3. **Reserves:** Lenders usually want to see 6 to 12 months of mortgage payments sitting in a US bank account as a liquid reserve.
4. **LLC Requirement:** Most DSCR lenders *require* you to close the loan in the name of a US [LLC](/library/structuring-us-property-purchases-llc/), not your personal name. This aligns perfectly with our tax and liability structuring advice.

## The Process for Gulf Investors

1. **Setup the Entity:** Form your US LLC (e.g., Wyoming) and obtain the EIN.
2. **Open a US Bank Account:** You must have a US account to fund the down payment and pay the mortgage. We assist clients in opening these remotely.
3. **Source the Funds:** Move your down payment and reserves (in USD) into the US bank account. Lenders will trace the origin of the funds (AML checks).
4. **Appraisal & Rent Schedule:** The lender will order an appraisal to confirm the value of the property and a "Rent Schedule" to confirm the market rent for the DSCR calculation.
5. **Close:** Sign documents remotely via the US Embassy in Abu Dhabi/Dubai or a remote online notary (RON).

## Conclusion

Leverage is the key to maximizing returns in real estate. By utilizing non-recourse DSCR loans, UAE residents can safely scale a US property portfolio without tying up 100% cash in every deal.
