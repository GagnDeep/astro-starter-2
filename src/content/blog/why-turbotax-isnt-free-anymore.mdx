# Why TurboTax Isn't Free Anymore (And What To Use Instead)

Every January, the ads start: *Free, Free, Free.* But if you've ever tried to file your taxes using a heavily advertised "Free Edition," you probably reached the end only to see a $40, $80, or even $120 bill.

This isn't an accident. It's a calculated strategy known as "bait and switch." Here is exactly why you got charged, and how to avoid it this year.

## What Kicks You Out of the "Free" Tier?

For the 2024 tax season, commercial "Free Editions" only cover what they call "simple tax returns." If you have any of the following, you will be forced to upgrade:

| Tax Situation | Form Required | Forces Upgrade? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student Loan Interest | 1098-E | Yes (usually to Deluxe) |
| Freelance / Gig Income (Uber, DoorDash) | 1099-NEC / Schedule C | Yes (usually to Premium) |
| Sold Crypto or Stocks | 1099-B / Schedule D | Yes (usually to Premium) |
| Unemployment Income | 1099-G | Yes (varies by provider) |
| Health Savings Account (HSA) | 1099-SA | Yes |
| Claiming Dependents | Child Tax Credit | Sometimes (varies by year/provider) |

If you just have a single W-2 and take the standard deduction, you might slip through. But almost *any* other financial activity triggers an upsell.

## The Actually Free Alternatives for 2024

If you don't want to pay, you don't have to. Here are the services that won't bait-and-switch you:

1. **FreeTaxUSA:** Federal filing is 100% free for everyone, including all major forms (1099s, Schedule C, Crypto). State filing is $15. Read our [TurboTax vs FreeTaxUSA](/comparisons/turbotax-vs-freetaxusa) guide for a full breakdown.
2. **Cash App Taxes:** 100% free for both Federal and State, including most major forms. However, you must file on a mobile device and download their app. Read our [Cash App vs FreeTaxUSA](/comparisons/cash-app-vs-freetaxusa) guide.
3. **IRS Free File:** If your Adjusted Gross Income (AGI) is $79,000 or less (for 2023 taxes filed in 2024), you qualify for partner software that is legally bound to be free.
4. **VITA Programs:** Volunteer Income Tax Assistance offers free in-person help if you make under $64,000. Learn more in our [VITA Sites vs Software](/blog/vita-sites-vs-software) guide.

## Common Mistakes

- **Mistake 1:** Assuming "Free to Start" means "Free to File." Almost all software is free to *start*. They wait until you've spent an hour entering data before hitting you with the paywall, banking on the sunk-cost fallacy.
- **Mistake 2:** Paying for State Filing when Federal was free. Providers often offer free Federal filing but charge $40+ for the State return. Always check the total price before starting.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I deduct the cost of tax prep software?**
A: Under current tax law (through 2025), you generally **cannot** deduct tax preparation fees if you take the standard deduction. If you are self-employed, you can deduct a portion of the fee as a business expense on Schedule C.

**Q: Is FreeTaxUSA a scam? Why is it free?**
A: It is not a scam; they are an authorized IRS e-file provider. They make money by charging $15 for state returns and offering optional add-ons like audit defense.

**Q: What if I already started my return in TurboTax?**
A: You can absolutely stop, leave the site, and start over in a free service. You don't owe them anything until you actually hit "File" or provide your credit card.
