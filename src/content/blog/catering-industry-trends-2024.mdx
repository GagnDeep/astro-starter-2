As we move through 2024, the catering industry continues to adapt to post-pandemic realities and shifting corporate budgets. Operators are focusing on streamlined drop-off menus and highly localized sourcing.

## The Rise of Hybrid Corporate Catering

With hybrid work models solidifying, companies are relying on "anchor days" to bring staff together. This has created a surge in high-value, individually boxed lunches that accommodate massive arrays of dietary restrictions. The focus is on safety, presentation, and zero cross-contact.

Read more about structuring these accounts in our [Corporate Catering Pricing](/pricing/corporate/) guide, or revisit the basics on the [homepage](/).

## Tech-Enabled Proposal Generation

Operators are moving away from manual Excel spreadsheets in favor of integrated proposal software that recalculates margins in real time. This ensures that every quote is profitable before it goes out the door.
