When you run a small business, tax season can feel overwhelming. The tax software industry preys on this anxiety, pushing expensive "Self-Employed" or "Small Business" tiers.

## Do You Really Need Paid Software?
If you operate as a sole proprietor or a single-member LLC, your business income is reported on Schedule C of your personal Form 1040.

### The Upsell
Commercial "Free Editions" will block you as soon as you say you have business income. They claim you need specialized guidance for [Itemized Deductions](/guides/itemized-deductions/) or depreciating assets.
- If your business is relatively simple (e.g., freelance writing, driving for a gig app), you likely do not need this.
- As long as your AGI is under the threshold, [IRS Free File](/guides/irs-free-file/) partners will let you file Schedule C and Schedule SE for free.

### Alternatives
If you are over the AGI limit, consider FreeTaxUSA, which charges $0 for federal filing, even for complex Schedule C returns. (Read our full [FreeTaxUSA Review](/blog/freetaxusa-review)).
