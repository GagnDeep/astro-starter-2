Grading digital assignments shouldn't require an IT degree. When students submit essays or worksheets as PDFs, teachers need a fast, reliable way to add grades, checkmarks, and feedback without navigating a maze of menus.

## The Problem with Enterprise Software

Schools often provide licenses for heavy enterprise PDF suites. While powerful, these tools suffer from:
1. **Feature Bloat:** You need a red pen and a text box, not digital certificate management.
2. **Slow Load Times:** Opening 30 student assignments takes forever when the software is sluggish.
3. **Compatibility Issues:** Some students submit [Linearized PDFs](/glossary/term-2), others submit massive, unoptimized scans (check the sizes with our [Calculator](/calculators/calc-1)).

## A Streamlined Workflow for Grading

A [simple online annotator](/tools/annotate-pdf) solves this by focusing entirely on the reading and markup experience.

### How to grade a batch of PDFs:

1. Download the student submissions to a local folder.
2. Open your browser and navigate to your markup tool.
3. Drag the first assignment into the browser.
4. Use the **Freehand Draw** tool (set to red) to add checkmarks or circle errors.
5. Use the **Text Tool** to add a final grade and summary feedback at the top.
6. Hit Save (it downloads instantly to your downloads folder).
7. Drag the next assignment in.

Because the processing happens locally in your browser, there is zero upload/download latency. The transition between papers is instantaneous. Furthermore, because no data is sent to a server, student privacy is maintained (complying with FERPA and other regulations).
