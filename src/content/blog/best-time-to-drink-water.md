It is often said that hydration is the key to life, and understanding **best time to drink water** is a major part of that equation. In this post, we dive deep into the science, debunk common myths, and provide actionable advice.

## The Science

Studies show that even a 2% drop in body water can lead to noticeable cognitive decline and physical fatigue.

| Condition | Water Needs |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sedentary | Baseline (Half body weight in oz) |
| Active | Baseline + 12oz per 30 mins |
| Extreme Heat | Baseline + 20% |

### Common Mistakes

1. **Ignoring Thirst:** Your [Thirst Mechanism](/glossary/thirst-mechanism) is highly tuned, but waiting until you are extremely thirsty means you are already dehydrated.
2. **Overhydration:** Yes, you can drink too much water, leading to [Hyponatremia](/glossary/hyponatremia).
3. **Forgetting Electrolytes:** Water alone isn't always enough, especially for athletes.

### FAQ
