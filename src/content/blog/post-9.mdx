## The Basics of Treating

This is guide number 9. Always ensure you are feeding treats that make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily diet.

## Common Mistakes
- **Assuming all treats are equal:** They aren't.
- **Ignoring Calories:** Treats have calories. Don't feed them daily if your dog is prone to weight gain.

## What to Do Next
If you've been feeding rawhide, throw it out. It's simply not worth the risk of a blockage.
