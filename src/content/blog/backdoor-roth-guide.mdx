## The Tradeoffs

The Backdoor Roth IRA allows high earners to bypass income limits. Tradeoff: The pro-rata rule. If you have existing pre-tax IRA balances, the conversion becomes partially taxable. It only makes sense if you can isolate post-tax contributions.

## Common Mistakes

1. Failing to file Form 8606 to report the non-deductible contribution, leading to double taxation.
2. Failing to account for inflation in long-term projections.
3. Ignoring fee drag, which can consume up to 30% of a portfolio's growth over a 30-year period.
