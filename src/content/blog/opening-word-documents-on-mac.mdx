# Opening Word Documents on Mac Without Office

Received a .docx file on your Mac but don't want to pay for a Microsoft 365 subscription? You have options.

## 1. Apple Pages
Pages is included free with macOS and can open most Word documents. However, complex formatting (like specific table styles or advanced macros) might not translate perfectly.

## 2. Google Docs
You can upload the file to Google Drive and open it in Google Docs. This works well but requires a Google account and involves uploading your file to their servers.
