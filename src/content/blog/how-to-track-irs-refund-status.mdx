Once you hit submit on your truly free tax return, the waiting game begins. Here is how to track your IRS refund status effectively.

## The Timeline
The IRS typically issues 9 out of 10 refunds in less than 21 days for returns filed electronically with direct deposit.

### Using "Where's My Refund?"
The best way to track your status is via the official IRS tool.
- You need your Social Security Number, filing status, and exact whole dollar amount of your refund.
- You can get this estimated amount using our [Tax Refund Calculator](/tools/tax-refund-calculator/).

### Why is my refund delayed?
Several factors can delay processing:
- You claimed the [Earned Income Tax Credit](/guides/earned-income-tax-credit/) or the [Child Tax Credit](/guides/child-tax-credit/). By law, the IRS cannot issue these refunds before mid-February.
- Your return requires manual review due to errors, such as a mismatch on your [W-2](/guides/w2-form-guide/).

### The "Refund Advance" Trap
Many paid tax software companies offer a "Refund Advance" or "Refund Transfer." **Avoid these.** They often come with high hidden fees or require you to pay for their premium software tier. Filing electronically via [IRS Free File](/guides/irs-free-file/) and using direct deposit is the fastest, completely free way to get your money.
