Most small businesses view paper, ink, and printer maintenance as a "cost of doing business." It's just a line item on the budget that rarely gets scrutinized.

However, when you actually run the numbers, the financial and environmental cost of printing documents—specifically for the purpose of getting them signed—is staggering.

In this guide, we'll break down the hidden costs of the "print, sign, scan" workflow and how switching to [electronic signatures](/glossary#electronic-signature) can immediately boost your margins.

## The Hidden Costs of Printing

Let's assume your business processes 100 contracts, NDAs, or [W-9 forms](/templates/w9) per month.

Using our free [Paper Cost Calculator](/tools/paper-cost-calculator), we can break down the math:

| Item | Avg Cost per Page | Cost per 100 Docs (5 pages each) | Annual Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Paper | $0.05 | $25.00 | $300.00 |
| Ink / Toner | $0.07 | $35.00 | $420.00 |
| **Direct Materials**| | **$60.00** | **$720.00** |

That's **$720 a year** just in direct materials to print standard documents.

But that doesn't account for the biggest cost: **Time.**

## Calculating the ROI of Your Time

If it takes you or an admin 15 minutes to print a document, put it in a folder, wait for the client to arrive and sign it (or mail it to them), and then scan it back into the digital archive, you are bleeding time.

According to our [Time Savings Calculator (ROI)](/tools/roi-calculator), processing 100 documents a month at 15 minutes each equals **25 hours a month**.

If your hourly rate (or your admin's fully loaded rate) is $50/hr, that's **$1,250 a month** in lost labor.

### The Total Cost of "Print, Sign, Scan"

- **Materials:** $720 / year
- **Labor:** $15,000 / year
- **Total:** **$15,720 / year**

## The Digital Solution

Switching to a digital workflow eliminates the material cost entirely and reduces the labor time from 15 minutes per document to roughly 1 minute per document.

### Steps to eliminate paper costs:

1. **Stop printing contracts:** If you generate a contract in Word or Google Docs, export it as a PDF. Do not hit print.
2. **Use frictionless e-signatures:** Upload that PDF to [usignpdf](/). Drop the signature blocks where they need to go, and email the link to the client.
3. **Store digitally:** Once signed, the platform automatically generates a [Certificate of Completion](/glossary#certificate-of-completion) and stores the document securely using [AES-256 encryption](/security/encryption). You can download it directly to your Google Drive or Dropbox.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Paying for Enterprise bloat:** Don't replace your $720 paper cost with a $1,200 annual contract for a tool like DocuSign or [PandaDoc](/compare/pandadoc) if you don't need their advanced CRM integrations. See our [DocuSign alternative guide](/blog/docusign-alternatives).
2. **Ignoring Legality:** Ensure the digital tool you use is compliant with the [ESIGN Act](/legality/us-esign-act) (in the US) or [eIDAS](/legality/eu-eidas) (in the EU). A simple image pasted on a PDF does not hold up in court.
