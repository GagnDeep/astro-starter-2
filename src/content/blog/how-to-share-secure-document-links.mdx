# How to Share Secure Document Links

When sharing sensitive information, a simple email attachment is often too risky. Here is how to use secure document links.

## Why Attachments Fail
- **Lack of Control:** Once sent, you cannot unsend an attachment.
- **Version Control:** If you find a typo, you have to email a "V2" file.
- **Size Limits:** Most email providers cap attachments at 25MB.

## Benefits of Link Sharing
By sharing a link instead of a file, you maintain control. You can update the underlying document, track when it was opened, and revoke access at any time.

## Using a Secure Viewer
For ultimate security, share links that open in a zero-footprint viewer. This prevents the recipient from downloading or printing the document, restricting them to a read-only view. Learn more in our guide to [Zero Footprint Security](/reference/security/zero-footprint/).
