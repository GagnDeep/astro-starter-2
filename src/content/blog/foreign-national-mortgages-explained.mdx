## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Buying in your personal name:** Exposes you to massive estate tax liabilities.
2. **Failing to file US taxes:** Even if you owe nothing due to depreciation, failing to file means you cannot claim those deductions, leading to penalties and full withholding.
3. **Ignoring state taxes:** While the UAE has no personal income tax, states like California or New York will tax your rental income aggressively. We often recommend zero-income-tax states like Texas, Florida, or Nevada.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do I need a US bank account?**
A: Yes. Most US property managers require a US bank account to deposit rents. We assist our clients in opening these remotely.

**Q: Can I get a mortgage in the US without a credit score?**
A: Yes. Foreign National Mortgages (or DSCR loans) underwrite the property's income rather than your personal US credit history. Expect to put 30-40% down.
