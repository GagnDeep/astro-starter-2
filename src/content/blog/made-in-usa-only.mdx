# The Ultimate Guide to Made In Usa Only



### Section 1: The Nuances of Made In Usa Only
When considering made in usa only, it is critical to look at the sourcing. Understanding your dog's diet is the foundation of a healthy life. The Association of American Feed Control Officials (AAFCO) sets baseline nutritional standards, but these are minimums, not ideals. Many commercial treats use fillers like corn, wheat, or soy, which offer little nutritional value and can exacerbate allergies. Instead, focusing on single-ingredient treats allows for better control over your dog's diet. For instance, a 50lb dog has a Resting Energy Requirement (RER) of roughly 725 kcal/day. If moderately active, their maintenance need is ~1160 kcal/day, leaving a maximum treat allowance of 116 kcal/day. When a single commercial biscuit contains 40 kcal of pure filler, you can see how quickly this limit is reached without providing any real nutrition. Therefore, selecting nutrient-dense options is paramount.

Furthermore, the processing method matters deeply. Dehydration gently removes moisture at low heat, preserving enzymes and vitamins. Freeze-drying involves rapid freezing and vacuuming, preserving the structure and nutrients perfectly. In contrast, extrusion (commonly used for kibble and cheap biscuits) involves high heat and pressure, destroying many natural vitamins and requiring synthetic spray-ons later. This fundamental difference in manufacturing is why we advocate for minimally processed treats.
