For decades, Switzerland and London were the undisputed hubs for Single Family Offices (SFOs). However, over the past five years, the Cayman Islands has seen an explosive surge in ultra-high-net-worth (UHNW) families choosing the jurisdiction as the base for their wealth management operations.

## Why Cayman?

The shift is driven by a combination of sophisticated structuring options, regulatory clarity, and lifestyle appeal.

1.  **The Regulatory Exemption:** Under the Securities Investment Business Act (SIBA), a Single Family Office that only manages the wealth of a single family (and does not offer services to the public) is generally treated as a "Non-Registrable Person." This means the SFO can operate without the heavy burden of obtaining a full CIMA license, providing immense operational privacy and agility.
2.  **Advanced Structuring Vehicles:** Cayman offers structures perfectly tailored to generational wealth transfer. The **Foundation Company** has become immensely popular for families from civil law jurisdictions who are uncomfortable with the common law concept of trusts. It allows the family to retain control via a Board of Directors while securing the assets in an ownerless, limited-liability vehicle.
3.  **Tax Neutrality:** The absence of corporate, income, and capital gains taxes allows the SFO to compound its investments efficiently before distributing them to family members in their respective tax-resident jurisdictions.

## The Lifestyle Component

Unlike traditional offshore financial centers that were essentially just booking locations, Cayman has actively developed its physical infrastructure to attract the principals of these SFOs to live on the island.
*   **Safety and Infrastructure:** Low crime rates, a highly developed medical system (e.g., Health City), and world-class private schools (e.g., Cayman International School) make it an attractive relocation destination.
*   **Residency for Wealthy Retirees:** The government offers specific, fast-track residency pathways for wealthy individuals. The "Certificate of Permanent Residence for Persons of Independent Means" grants a 25-year residency (and the right to work) in exchange for a CI$2 million real estate investment and proof of independent wealth.

## The Institutionalization of the SFO

We are seeing Cayman SFOs operate increasingly like institutional hedge funds. Rather than just passively holding assets, they are actively engaging in direct private equity investments, venture capital funding, and sophisticated hedging strategies, pulling top-tier talent directly from local Big Four accounting and Magic Circle law firms to run their operations.
