# Mapping Your Supply Chain for Due Diligence

By 2027, the battery industry will undergo a massive shift. This post covers the critical aspects of mapping your supply chain for due diligence.

## The Problem

Most operators underestimate the complexity of data gathering. The primary source (EU Regulation 2023/1542) mandates 90+ data points.

## Common Mistakes

- Ignoring the 2025 deadlines for carbon footprint.
- Assuming existing ERP systems are sufficient.
- Failing to secure supplier data early.

## Solution

1. Audit your current data.
2. Select a traceability vendor.
3. Run a pilot on a single battery line.

### Edge Cases

What if a supplier refuses to provide data? You must prove due diligence and use secondary data as permitted by the methodology.
