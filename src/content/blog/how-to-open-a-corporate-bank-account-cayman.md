One of the most frequent complaints from entrepreneurs establishing a Cayman Islands Exempted Company is the extreme difficulty of opening a corporate bank account on the island. Many founders incorporate their entity only to spend the next six months unable to process payments.

## The Disconnect: Local vs. Offshore

The root of the problem is a misunderstanding of the Cayman banking sector.
*   **The Local Retail Banks:** Banks like Butterfield or Cayman National exist primarily to service the domestic economy (local businesses and residents). They have very little appetite for the AML risk associated with a Cayman Exempted Company whose directors live in London, whose operations are in Asia, and whose clients are global.
*   **The Institutional Banks:** The massive offshore banks in Cayman service multi-billion dollar hedge funds and trusts. They will not open a checking account for a tech startup or a holding company with a few million dollars in seed capital.

## The AML Burden

Local retail banks operate under the strict supervision of CIMA. If they open an account for an offshore entity, they must conduct exhaustive Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD). They must identify the Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs), verify their source of wealth, and constantly monitor international wire transfers.

For a retail bank, the compliance cost of monitoring a foreign-operating startup vastly exceeds the revenue they might generate from account fees. Consequently, they often simply refuse to open the account, deeming it outside their risk appetite.

## The Solutions

So, how do Cayman Exempted Companies actually bank?

1.  **Onshore Banking (The Standard Route):** A Cayman company is not required to bank in the Cayman Islands. Most Cayman tech startups, Web3 projects, and holding companies open their corporate bank accounts onshore—typically in the United States (e.g., Mercury, Brex, or specialized crypto-friendly banks) or in Switzerland/Liechtenstein.
2.  **Digital EMIs:** Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) like Payoneer or specialized offshore corporate payment providers are increasingly filling the void left by traditional banks, offering multi-currency accounts with faster onboarding.
3.  **Fund Administrators:** For investment funds, the banking issue is solved by the Fund Administrator. The administrator opens accounts in the name of the fund with their institutional banking partners (e.g., State Street or Northern Trust) as part of their service package.

Founders should secure their banking relationships *before* finalizing their Cayman incorporation, ensuring their chosen bank is willing to onboard a Cayman entity.
