The UAE's aerospace manufacturing sector is undergoing a rapid maturation process. Primes are increasingly demanding localized supply chains capable of complex work, rather than relying entirely on importing finished structural components.

This shift directly impacts [CNC machining subcontractors](/directory/cnc-machining/) and [composite manufacturing](/directory/composite-manufacturing/) facilities, who must now demonstrate rigorous process controls and AS9100 compliance to win contracts.
