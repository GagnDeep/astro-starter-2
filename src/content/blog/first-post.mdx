The restaurant industry is evolving rapidly, and POS systems are at the heart of this transformation.

## What is the biggest shift in POS technology today?

The biggest shift in POS technology today is the move toward cloud-native platforms that natively integrate with third-party delivery services, payroll software, and automated marketing tools. This eliminates the need for expensive middle-ware solutions. By consolidating these functions, restaurants can significantly reduce their software stack complexity and cut down on monthly subscription costs while simultaneously improving data accuracy across their entire operation.

## How are hardware costs changing for operators?

Hardware costs for operators are changing as vendors shift away from proprietary terminals toward consumer-grade tablets and handhelds. This transition drastically lowers upfront capital expenditures. While consumer tablets may not be as rugged as purpose-built terminals, their low replacement cost and immediate availability make them an attractive option for both new concepts and established operators looking to refresh their technology stack without breaking the bank.
