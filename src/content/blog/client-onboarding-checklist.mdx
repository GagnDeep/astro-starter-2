Client onboarding is the most critical phase of the agency-client relationship. It sets the tone for everything that follows.

If your onboarding process is chaotic, the client will assume your work will be chaotic. If your onboarding is smooth, professional, and frictionless, they will trust you implicitly.

In this guide, we provide a step-by-step checklist for [agencies](/use-cases/agencies) to onboard clients perfectly, every time.

## Phase 1: The Contract and Payment

The moment a client says "Yes" on a sales call, the clock starts. The longer it takes to get the contract signed and the deposit paid, the higher the chance of buyer's remorse.

### The Frictionless Contract
Do not send a PDF attachment and ask them to "print, sign, and scan it back."

Instead, use a tool like [usignpdf](/) to send an [electronic signature](/glossary#electronic-signature) request.

- **Why it matters:** It takes them 30 seconds to sign on their phone. It creates a legally binding [Audit Trail](/security/audit-trails) compliant with the [ESIGN Act](/legality/us-esign-act).
- **The Alternative:** If you use an enterprise tool like [DocuSign](/compare/docusign), they might be forced to create an account or navigate a complex UI, adding unnecessary friction.

### The Initial Invoice
Send the invoice *immediately* after the contract is signed (or concurrently). Do not schedule the kickoff call until the deposit clears.

## Phase 2: The Welcome Packet & Intake

Once the money is in the bank, send a Welcome Packet.

### What to include:
1. **Welcome Letter:** A warm, enthusiastic message from the account manager.
2. **Communication Guidelines:** How and when to contact you (e.g., "We use Slack for quick questions, email for approvals. We respond between 9 AM and 5 PM EST").
3. **The Intake Form:** A secure form (using a tool like Typeform or our own capture API) to gather necessary assets:
   - Brand guidelines
   - Logos (vector format)
   - Login credentials (use a secure tool like 1Password, *never* email passwords)
   - Billing contact details

## Phase 3: The Kickoff Call

The kickoff call is not a sales call. It is a working session.

### The Kickoff Agenda:
| Time | Topic | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 mins | Introductions | Build rapport across teams. |
| 10 mins | Review SOW | Ensure everyone is aligned on the [freelance contract](/templates/freelance-contract) deliverables. |
| 15 mins | Deep Dive | Review the intake form answers and ask clarifying questions. |
| 10 mins | Timeline & Milestones | Set expectations for exactly when the first deliverable will be ready. |
| 5 mins | Q&A | Clear up any lingering doubts. |

## Phase 4: The Internal Handoff

The client shouldn't see this part, but it's crucial. The salesperson/account manager must hand the project over to the execution team.

1. Create the project in your project management tool (Asana, ClickUp, Jira).
2. Upload the signed contract to the project files (usignpdf ensures [document integrity](/glossary#document-integrity) with AES-256 [encryption](/security/encryption)).
3. Brief the team on the client's goals and quirks.

## Common Mistakes in Onboarding

1. **Overwhelming the client:** Don't send 14 different emails with 14 different requests. Consolidate.
2. **Starting work without a signature:** Never, ever start work before the contract is signed. (See our guide on [why freelancers need contracts](/blog/how-to-write-freelance-contract)).
3. **Being vague about timelines:** "We'll get back to you soon" is bad. "You will have the first draft by Tuesday at 3 PM" is good.
