# I am always hungry on a diet what should I eat?

When optimizing macros, one of the most common questions is about balancing protein and fiber.

## The Core Problem
Many people struggle with satiety on a caloric deficit. According to [NEEDS CONFIRMATION: 2024 satiety study], protein increases thermogenesis by 20-30%, whereas fiber delays gastric emptying.

### Table: Protein vs Fiber Satiety Tradeoffs

| Nutrient | Satiety Mechanism | Calories/gram | Common Mistake |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protein | Thermic effect, Peptides | 4 | Ignoring fiber, causing constipation. |
| Fiber | Physical bulk, SCFA | ~2 | Increasing too fast, causing bloating. |

## Worked Example
If you eat a meal with 30g of protein and 10g of fiber, your satiety response will likely last 4-5 hours.
