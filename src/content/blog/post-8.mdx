# The Difference Between Digital and Electronic Signatures

If you are running a small business, getting contracts signed shouldn't be a bottleneck. The era of print, sign, scan, and email is over.

## Section 1: The Nuances of The

When considering e-signatures, small business owners often overlook the hidden costs associated with traditional paper processes. Not only are you paying for paper, toner, and postage, but the sheer administrative burden of tracking physical documents can consume hours of valuable time each week. An electronic signature solution eliminates this friction entirely.

## Section 2: The Nuances of The

Furthermore, the legal landscape surrounding digital agreements is robust. As long as you maintain a clear audit trail that logs timestamps, IP addresses, and identity verification, these documents hold up in court just as well—if not better—than their physical counterparts. This is especially critical for [freelancers](/industries/freelancers) and [agencies](/industries/agencies) who operate asynchronously across different states or countries.
