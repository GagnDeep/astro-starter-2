## Introduction

The best templates to maximize your oral fluency and pronunciation scores in Describe Image.

Many test-takers struggle with achieving a 79+ score because they don't fully understand the Pearson scoring algorithm. In this post, we will cover the key strategies and common mistakes to avoid.

### Common Mistakes

1. **Speaking too fast:** Rushing through Read Aloud does not increase your oral fluency score.
2. **Ignoring formatting in writing:** The Write Essay task heavily penalizes poor structure and word count deviations.
3. **Pausing randomly:** Unnatural hesitations are heavily penalized by the AI.

### Worked Example

Let's look at a sample Describe Image task.

| Element | Description | Score Impact |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Introduction | "The graph illustrates..." | High |
| Body | Identifying maxima and minima | High |
| Conclusion | "In conclusion, it is evident that..." | Medium |

### FAQ

**Does microphone position matter?**
Yes. Placing it too close can cause breathing noises to be recorded, which negatively impacts pronunciation scores.

**How is partial credit awarded?**
For tasks like Multiple Choice (Multiple Answers), you lose points for incorrect selections, but the minimum score for a task is zero.
