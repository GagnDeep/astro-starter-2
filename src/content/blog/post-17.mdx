If you are a student or researcher, you likely spend hours [highlighting PDFs](/tools/highlight-pdf). But once you've marked up a 100-page document, how do you get those highlights out into a Word document or note-taking app without retyping them?

## Why Extraction is Difficult

Because of how [PDF Content Streams](/reference/topic-17) work, text and highlights are stored separately. A highlight is just a colored rectangle floating above the text; it does not inherently "know" what text it is covering.

To extract the text, software must calculate the mathematical overlap between the highlight annotation and the text stream underneath it.

## Best Tools for the Job

While simple web viewers don't offer extraction, dedicated citation managers (like Zotero or Mendeley) or specialized Mac tools (like Highlights app) do this calculation automatically.

If you just need to share the visual pages containing highlights (rather than the raw text), you can extract those specific pages following our [Extraction Guide](/guides/guide-2), which significantly reduces the [file size](/calculators/calc-1) for email sharing.
