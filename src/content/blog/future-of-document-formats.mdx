# The Future of Document Formats: Beyond the PDF

The PDF has ruled the digital document world for decades. But is its reign coming to an end?

## The Mobile Problem
PDFs are built on the concept of a fixed, printable page. This is terrible for mobile devices, requiring constant pinching and zooming.

## The Rise of Reflowable Content
Formats like EPUB, and web-native formats (HTML/CSS), are gaining traction because they adapt to the screen size. The "document" is no longer a static piece of digital paper, but a responsive container of information.

While the PDF isn't going anywhere soon, expect to see a shift towards more dynamic formats in the coming years. Explore all the formats we support in our [Reference Library](/reference/).
