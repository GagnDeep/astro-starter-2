Underpayment penalties can be confusing and costly. In this post, we break down how to calculate them and how to avoid them entirely using free tools.

## Understanding the Tax Penalty
The IRS operates on a "pay-as-you-go" system. If you owe more than $1,000 when you file, and you didn't pay enough estimated taxes during the year, you might face a penalty.

### Safe Harbor Rules
To avoid the penalty, you must meet one of the safe harbors:
1. Owe less than $1,000.
2. Pay at least 90% of the tax for the current year.
3. Pay 100% of the tax shown on your prior year's return (110% if your AGI was over $150k).

### Common Mistakes
- **Ignoring Q4 Payments:** [Freelancers](/types/freelancers/) often forget that the Q4 estimated payment is due in mid-January, not April 15th.
- **Not using free tools:** If you meet the safe harbor, you can file the forms for free via [IRS Free File](/guides/irs-free-file/).
