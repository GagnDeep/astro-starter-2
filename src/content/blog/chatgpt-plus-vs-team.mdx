If you are a professional using ChatGPT for work, the free version is no longer sufficient. You need access to the most capable models (GPT-4o), Advanced Data Analysis, and Custom GPTs.

This leaves two primary options for individuals and small groups: **ChatGPT Plus** and **ChatGPT Team**.

Choosing the wrong one can either result in wasted money or, much worse, a massive data privacy violation.

## ChatGPT Plus: The Individual Power User

ChatGPT Plus costs **$20/month per user**.

**Who it's for:** Freelancers, solopreneurs, and individuals using AI for personal productivity where no proprietary company data is involved.

### Pros:
- Access to GPT-4o, DALL-E 3, and Advanced Data Analysis.
- Ability to create and use Custom GPTs.
- Early access to new features (like advanced voice mode).

### Cons:
- **Data Privacy:** By default, OpenAI **can and will use your chats to train their future models.** You can opt-out by turning off "Chat History & Training" in settings, but doing so means you lose your chat history entirely across devices.
- **No Admin Controls:** If you buy 5 Plus accounts for your team, you manage 5 separate logins, 5 separate credit card charges, and have zero oversight into how the tools are being used.
- **Lower Limits:** Usage caps on GPT-4o are lower than the Team tier.

## ChatGPT Team: The Secure Business Standard

ChatGPT Team costs **$25/month per user** (billed annually) or $30/month (billed monthly). It requires a minimum of 2 users.

**Who it's for:** Any business, agency, or corporate team that plans to upload internal documents, write code, or process client data.

### Pros:
- **Absolute Data Privacy:** OpenAI explicitly states they **do not train on your business data or conversations.** You get complete privacy while keeping your chat history intact.
- **Higher Usage Limits:** Team users get higher message caps for GPT-4o and advanced tools, meaning fewer "You've reached your limit" interruptions during deep work.
- **Shared Workspace:** You can create Custom GPTs (like a "Brand Voice Copywriter" or a "Legal Contract Analyzer") and publish them securely *only* to your team workspace.
- **Admin Console:** Centralized billing, user management, and the ability to revoke access when an employee leaves the company.

### Cons:
- **Cost:** It is slightly more expensive and requires a 2-user minimum, making the entry price $50/month (annual) or $60/month (monthly).

## The Verdict

If you are using AI to summarize competitor websites or draft generic emails, ChatGPT Plus is fine.

If you are uploading financial spreadsheets, pasting in source code, or drafting confidential memos, **you must use ChatGPT Team (or Enterprise).** The $5/month difference is the cheapest cybersecurity insurance your company will ever buy.
