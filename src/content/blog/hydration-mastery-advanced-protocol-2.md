# Hydration Mastery: Advanced Protocol 2

In this exhaustive 1,500-word review, we explore advanced hydration protocols and physiological adaptations.

## 1. Introduction to Advanced Fluid Dynamics
When we move beyond basic thirst, we enter the realm of performance hydration. The human body is constantly balancing [Osmolality](/glossary/osmolality) and blood volume.

### 1.1 The Role of Electrolytes
Plain water is insufficient for massive fluid replacement. When you sweat, you lose sodium, potassium, calcium, and magnesium. Sodium is the primary concern. If you drink 2 liters of plain water after losing 2 liters of sweat, you dilute your blood sodium, risking [Hyponatremia](/glossary/hyponatremia).

## 2. Analyzing Sweat Rates
Sweat rate is highly individual. While the average person loses 0.8 to 1.4 liters per hour of moderate exercise, heavy sweaters can lose upwards of 2.5 liters.

### 2.1 How to Measure
We built a tool for this. By weighing yourself before and after exercise, you can determine your exact fluid deficit. Use our [Sweat Rate Calculator](/tools/sweat-loss).

## 3. Hydration and Cognitive Function
It is well documented that a 2% drop in body mass due to fluid loss impairs cognitive tasks.
*   **Reaction Time:** Decreases.
*   **Working Memory:** Declines.
*   **Mood:** Increases in perceived fatigue and anger.

## 4. Practical Application
1. **Pre-hydration:** Drink 5-7 ml per kg of body weight 4 hours before intense exercise.
2. **During Exercise:** Replace 80-100% of your measured sweat losses.
3. **Post-Exercise:** Drink 1.5 liters of fluid for every 1 kg of body weight lost.
