The Cayman Islands real estate market continues to demonstrate resilience despite global interest rate pressures. Q1 2024 data indicates a plateau in the rapid price acceleration seen during the pandemic, shifting toward a more stabilized, inventory-constrained market.

## Seven Mile Beach Continues to Command Premiums

The ultra-luxury condo market along the Seven Mile Beach (SMB) corridor remains insulated from traditional mortgage rate shocks, as transactions in this tier are predominantly all-cash.
*   **Average Price per Sq Ft:** SMB properties are currently trading between US$1,500 and US$2,500 per square foot, depending on age and amenities.
*   **New Developments:** Pre-construction sales for upcoming luxury developments (such as Lacovia and Watermark) have set new pricing benchmarks, with penthouses commanding north of US$20 million.

## Inland Migration and the Rise of South Sound

As SMB prices become prohibitive for many resident professionals, development has aggressively pushed eastward and southward.
*   **South Sound:** Continues to be the premier family neighborhood. Townhomes that sold for CI$600k in 2019 are now trading closer to CI$900k.
*   **Eastern Districts:** Areas like Savannah and Bodden Town are seeing a surge in mid-market residential developments aimed at local buyers and mid-level expats, with 3-bedroom homes starting around CI$550,000.

## Impact of Stamp Duty

Buyers must factor in the 7.5% one-time stamp duty on real estate purchases. While the government offers concessions for first-time Caymanian buyers, expat investors pay the full 7.5% on the market value of the property.
