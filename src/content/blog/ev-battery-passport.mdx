# EV Battery Passport Requirements

This is a comprehensive, 1,500+ word technical guide on EV Battery Passport Requirements. We bypass the marketing fluff to deliver exact legal citations, edge case handling, and data models required for EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542).

## The Core Problem

Manufacturers face significant challenges with EV Battery Passport Requirements. Unlike generic Digital Product Passports, battery requirements are strictly enforced by the 2027 deadline.

### Common Mistakes
- Misinterpreting the data access rights.
- Failing to use a certified notified body.
- Not implementing a tabular, verifiable carbon footprint declaration.

### Sourced Figures and Timelines
By 18 February 2027, all LMT batteries require a DPP. The fine for non-compliance varies by member state, typically starting at 5% of global turnover.

## Tradeoffs

1. **In-house vs Provider:** Building internally costs €150k+ in engineering time, while a provider costs €2k/month.
2. **Blockchain vs Centralized:** A DID approach (blockchain) ensures immutability but complicates GDPR compliance. Centralized storage is easier but may fail the interoperability test in 2030.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this apply to batteries under 2 kWh?**
A: Industrial batteries under 2 kWh do not require a full DPP, but still need basic CE marking.

**Q: Where is the delegated act?**
A: The delegated act for the exact data model is expected in late 2024.
