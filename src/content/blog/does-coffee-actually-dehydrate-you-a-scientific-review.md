# Does Coffee Actually Dehydrate You? A Scientific Review

The internet is rife with conflicting advice about water intake. For decades, coffee and tea were branded as diuretics that resulted in a net loss of fluid.

## The Science of Caffeine
While caffeine does have a mild [Diuretic](/glossary/diuretic) effect, the water in a cup of coffee or tea more than makes up for it. A 2014 study published in the *PLOS ONE* journal found no significant difference in hydration status between men who drank coffee and those who drank an equal volume of water over a three-day period.

## Worked Example: The Office Worker
Consider Jane, an accountant. She drinks three cups of coffee. She is still well within the healthy [Euhydration](/glossary/euhydration) range.
