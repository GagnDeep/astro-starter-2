Sending a contract into the void is stressful. You're left wondering: did it go to spam? Are they reviewing it? Or did they just forget?

## The Problem with Email Read Receipts

Traditional email read receipts are unreliable. Most modern email clients block them by default to protect user privacy.

## The Solution: E-Signature Tracking

When you use a platform like [usignpdf](/), tracking is built into the workflow automatically.

Because the client has to click a unique, secure link to view the document on our servers, we can log exactly when that event happens.

### What you can track:
1. **Delivered:** The email successfully reached their inbox.
2. **Viewed:** The client clicked the link and the document rendered on their screen.
3. **Signed:** The client completed the action.

All of these events are permanently logged in the document's [Audit Trail](/security/audit-trails), providing both peace of mind for you and legal non-repudiation in the future.
