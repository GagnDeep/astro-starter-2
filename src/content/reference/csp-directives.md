      <h1>Content Security Policy</h1>
      <p class="text-xl text-text/80">CSP directives for iframes.</p>

      <div class="mt-8">
        To protect your site from XSS, use the `frame-src` CSP directive to strictly control which domains can be embedded. To protect your own documents from being embedded elsewhere, use the `X-Frame-Options` or `frame-ancestors` directive.
