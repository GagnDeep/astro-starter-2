      <h1>ARIA Labels</h1>
      <p class="text-xl text-text/80">Accessibility for embedded content.</p>

      <div class="mt-8">
        Embedded documents are often a black box to screen readers. Always provide a descriptive `title` attribute on your `&lt;iframe&gt;`. Furthermore, provide a plain-text fallback link so visually impaired users can download the document directly.
