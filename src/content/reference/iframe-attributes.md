      <h1>Iframe Attributes</h1>
      <p class="text-xl text-text/80">Standard attributes for document viewers.</p>

      <div class="mt-8">
        Modern iframes support powerful attributes. `sandbox` restricts what the embedded document can do (e.g., `allow-scripts`, `allow-same-origin`). `loading="lazy"` defers loading until the iframe is near the viewport. `title` is essential for accessibility.
