      <h1>Object vs Embed</h1>
      <p class="text-xl text-text/80">Which tag should you use?</p>

      <div class="mt-8">
        The `&lt;object&gt;` tag allows for fallback content (e.g., `<object data="file.pdf">Please download the PDF</object>`). `&lt;embed&gt;` does not. However, both are generally considered legacy compared to `&lt;iframe&gt;` or dedicated canvas-based viewers.
      </div>
