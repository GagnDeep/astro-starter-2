## How to Interpret Your Results

The numbers provided by this calculator represent a mathematically optimal starting point based on the provided inputs. However, the human body is not a closed system.

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- **Chasing exact numbers:** Do not stress over hitting these numbers to the exact gram or calorie. Aim for consistency within a +/- 5% range.
- **Ignoring biofeedback:** If the calculator suggests a certain caloric intake, but you are constantly lethargic or losing strength in the gym, you need to adjust your intake upward regardless of what the math says.
- **Static targets:** Your targets must adapt as your body changes. Recalculate every 4-6 weeks or after a significant weight change.
