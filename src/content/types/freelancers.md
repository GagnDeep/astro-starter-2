# Tax Guide for Freelancers

Filing taxes as part of the Freelancers group comes with specific challenges and opportunities for free filing.

## What You Need to Know
If you are in this category, your income reporting requirements differ from others. For instance, you may receive different forms and qualify for different deductions.

## Top Tips for Freelancers
1. **Gather Documentation:** Always have your paperwork ready before April 15th.
2. **Use Free Tools:** Many Freelancers assume they need paid software. Check our [TurboTax vs H&R Block](/blog/turbotax-vs-hr-block) comparison to see why free often wins.
3. **Understand Deductions:** Ensure you are claiming everything you're entitled to.
