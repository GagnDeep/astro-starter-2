# Aquila Aerospace

Aquila Aerospace is a critical player in the UAE's aerospace and defense supply chain, operating out of Abu Dhabi. Specializing in **avionics**, they adhere to stringent international standards.


## Deep Reference Profile for Aquila Aerospace

Located in Abu Dhabi, Aquila Aerospace operates at the apex of the UAE defense supply chain. With stringent oversight from national regulatory bodies, they deliver specialized capabilities crucial to the National In-Country Value (ICV) initiatives.

### Specific Capabilities and Tolerances
- **Machining:** 5-axis CNC with tolerances down to 5 microns.
- **Materials:** Authorized handler of aerospace-grade titanium and composite laminates.
- **Testing:** In-house NDT (Non-Destructive Testing) compliant with NADCAP standards.

### Market Positioning & Edge Cases
While Aquila Aerospace is heavily integrated into local OEM supply lines, international prime contractors should note that their specific export control procedures align with standard ITAR frameworks but require end-user certificates processed through UAE authorities.

*Exception:* Certain commercial avionics repairs are expedited, whereas purely military-grade components undergo a secondary Tawazun audit.
