The Cayman Islands offer an exceptional standard of living, zero income tax, and a vibrant expat community. However, it is also one of the most expensive places in the world to live. Almost all goods are imported and subject to import duty (typically 22% or higher), which is reflected in consumer prices.

## Housing and Real Estate

Housing will be your largest expense. Rents vary significantly by district.

*   **Seven Mile Beach (SMB) / Camana Bay:** Premium locations. A modern 1-bedroom apartment typically ranges from CI$3,000 to CI$4,500+ per month.
*   **South Sound:** Popular with families and professionals. 2-3 bedroom townhomes rent for CI$4,000 to CI$6,500.
*   **George Town / Red Bay / Prospect:** More affordable options. A 1-bedroom can be found for CI$1,500 to CI$2,500.

*Note: 1 CI$ = 1.20 US$ (fixed peg).*

## Utilities and Connectivity

*   **Electricity (CUC):** High due to air conditioning. Expect CI$250 - CI$500+ per month depending on usage and property size.
*   **Water (Water Authority or Cayman Water):** CI$60 - CI$150 per month.
*   **Internet:** Fiber optic broadband (e.g., Logic, C3, Flow) costs between CI$90 and CI$150 per month for standard speeds (100Mbps+).
*   **Mobile Plan:** CI$60 - CI$100 per month for a post-paid plan with data.

## Groceries and Dining

*   **Groceries:** Expect your grocery bill to be 30-50% higher than in the US or UK. A weekly shop for a couple easily exceeds CI$200.
*   **Dining Out:**
    *   Casual lunch: CI$15 - CI$25
    *   Mid-range dinner for two (with drinks): CI$100 - CI$150
    *   Fine dining (e.g., Ritz-Carlton, Kimpton): CI$200+ for two

## Transportation

*   **Buying a Car:** Vehicles are expensive due to shipping and import duties (29.5% for standard cars, up to 42% for luxury vehicles). A reliable second-hand Japanese import usually starts around CI$6,000 - CI$10,000.
*   **Gasoline:** Approximately CI$5.50 - CI$6.00 per Imperial Gallon.

## Summary: What Salary Do You Need?

To live comfortably (renting a decent 1-bedroom, running a car, socializing, and saving a little), a single professional should aim for a minimum salary of US$75,000 - US$90,000. For families (requiring larger housing and factoring in school fees of CI$10k-CI$20k per child), a combined household income of US$150,000+ is generally necessary.
