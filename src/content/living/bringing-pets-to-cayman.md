The Cayman Islands is rabies-free. To protect this status, the Department of Agriculture (DoA) enforces extremely strict regulations regarding the importation of animals. Relocating a pet requires meticulous planning; a single missed deadline or missing document can result in your pet being denied entry or euthanized.

## The Rabies Requirement

The process depends entirely on where your pet is coming from.
*   **Rabies-Free Countries (e.g., UK, Australia, Jamaica):** The process is relatively straightforward. Pets need a microchip, an official health certificate, and an import permit.
*   **Rabies-Controlled Countries (e.g., USA, Canada):** The vast majority of expats come from here. The process is lengthy and requires a specific sequence of rabies vaccinations and blood tests.
*   **Rabies-Endemic Countries:** Pets generally cannot be imported directly and must spend six months in a rabies-free or rabies-controlled country first.

## The Process for US/Canada Imports (Rabies-Controlled)

If bringing a dog or cat from the US or Canada, you must start the process **at least 6 months before your intended travel date**.

1.  **Microchip:** The pet must be microchipped (ISO compliant) *before* the rabies vaccine is given.
2.  **Rabies Vaccination:** Must be administered after the microchip.
3.  **FAVN Titer Test:** Wait at least 30 days after the rabies vaccination, then have a vet draw blood for a Rabies Titer Test (FAVN). The blood must be sent to an approved laboratory (e.g., Kansas State University).
4.  **The Wait:** If the test result is successful (showing adequate rabies antibodies), the pet must wait **two to three months** (depending on the exact country status) from the date the blood was drawn before they can enter Cayman.
5.  **Tick and Tapeworm Treatment:** Within 14 days of travel, a vet must administer specific tick and tapeworm treatments.
6.  **Official Health Certificate:** Within 14 days of travel, an official government vet (e.g., USDA in the US) must endorse the health certificate.
7.  **Import Permit:** Submit all documentation to the Cayman DoA to receive the Import Permit. This must be presented to the airline before they let the pet board.

## Banned Breeds

The Cayman Islands strictly prohibits the importation of certain dog breeds, including:
*   Pit Bull Terriers
*   Dogo Argentino
*   Fila Argentino
*   Japanese Tosa
*   Mastiff (all breeds)

## Traveling with Pets

*   **In-Cabin:** Small pets can sometimes fly in the cabin on commercial airlines, depending on the airline's rules (e.g., Cayman Airways allows a limited number of small pets in the cabin).
*   **Cargo/Manifest:** Larger dogs must fly as cargo. Note that commercial airlines have "heat embargoes"—they will not fly pets as cargo during the hot summer months.
*   **Private Charter:** Because of the stress of cargo flying and the heat embargoes, many expats use pet relocation companies or pool resources to share a private charter flight from Miami or Tampa for their pets.

*Always consult the official Cayman Islands Department of Agriculture website for the most up-to-date and legally binding requirements.*
