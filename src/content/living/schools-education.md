Moving to the Cayman Islands with children requires careful planning regarding education. The most important rule for expatriates is this: **Expat children are generally required to attend private schools.** Public (government) schools are reserved for Caymanian citizens and those with Permanent Residency, though exceptions are sometimes made if space permits.

## The Private School Landscape

Grand Cayman has several excellent private schools offering different international curricula. Due to high demand, **waitlists are common**, and you should apply to schools before you even secure your work permit.

### British Curriculum (IGCSE / A-Levels)
*   **Cayman Prep and High School:** Highly regarded, academically rigorous, and historically the most difficult to get into.
*   **St. Ignatius Catholic School:** Strong academic reputation, follows the UK curriculum, requires adherence to Catholic ethos but accepts students of all faiths.

### American Curriculum
*   **Cayman International School (CIS):** Located in Camana Bay, CIS offers the International Baccalaureate (IB) diploma alongside a US high school diploma. It boasts modern facilities and is very popular with corporate expats.
*   **Triple C School:** A Christian-based school offering an American curriculum.

### Montessori and Alternative
*   **Montessori By The Sea / Cayman Montessori School:** Excellent options for primary education.
*   **Hope Academy:** Known for smaller class sizes and supporting diverse learning needs.

## Typical Costs and Fees

Private education in Cayman is a significant financial commitment. Fees generally increase as the child moves from primary to high school.

*   **Primary School (Years 1-6):** Expect to pay between CI$10,000 and CI$16,000 per year.
*   **High School (Years 7-13):** Expect to pay between CI$14,000 and CI$22,000+ per year.
*   **Additional Costs:** You must also factor in non-refundable application fees (CI$200-CI$300), seat holding fees/capital fees upon acceptance (often CI$1,000-CI$2,000), uniforms, and after-school care.

*Note: 1 CI$ = 1.20 US$.*

## The Application Process

1.  **Apply Early:** The school year starts in late August/early September. You should ideally be applying by January for the following academic year.
2.  **Assessments:** Most schools require an entrance assessment or an interview, even for early years.
3.  **Documentation:** You will need previous school reports, immunization records, and a copy of the child's birth certificate and passport.

## A Note on Work Permits

When applying for a work permit, the Department of WORC will scrutinize the parent's salary to ensure they can afford private school fees for dependent children. If your salary does not comfortably cover the CI$15,000+ per child cost of education alongside rent and living expenses, the dependent permit may be refused.
