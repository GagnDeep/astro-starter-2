The Cayman Islands boasts world-class healthcare facilities, but the system is heavily privatized and relies strictly on health insurance. Under the Health Insurance Act, it is mandatory for every person residing in the Cayman Islands to have health insurance.

## The Mandatory Requirement (SHIC)

By law, every employer must provide health insurance for their employees (and their unemployed spouses and children). The legal minimum level of cover is known as the **Standard Health Insurance Contract (SHIC)**.

### Who Pays?
*   **The Premium:** The employer is legally required to pay at least 50% of the premium for the employee's SHIC coverage. However, in professional sectors (finance, legal, tech), it is standard market practice for the employer to pay 100% of the employee's premium, and sometimes the dependents' premiums as well.
*   **Dependents:** Employers are required to *arrange* coverage for dependents, but they are not legally required to pay for it. If they choose not to, the cost is deducted from the employee's salary.

### The Limits of SHIC
It is vital to understand that a basic SHIC plan is highly restrictive.
*   **Maximum Lifetime Benefit:** Often capped around CI$100,000 (which can be exhausted rapidly in a major medical emergency).
*   **Outpatient Limits:** Very low annual limits for doctor visits, prescriptions, and diagnostics (e.g., CI$400 a year for outpatient visits).
*   **Recommendation:** Most expats opt to upgrade their insurance to a "comprehensive" plan (often called enhanced plans) to ensure adequate coverage for overseas care, maternity, and major medical events.

## The Healthcare Infrastructure

The island is serviced by three main hospitals and numerous private clinics.

1.  **Health Services Authority (HSA) - Cayman Islands Hospital:** Located in George Town, this is the main public hospital. It handles the majority of trauma cases and emergency room visits.
2.  **Health City Cayman Islands (HCCI):** Located in East End (with a new facility opening in Camana Bay), this is a JCI-accredited tertiary care hospital specializing in cardiology, orthopedics, and oncology.
3.  **Doctors Hospital (formerly CTMH):** A private hospital in George Town offering surgical services, maternity, and emergency care.

## Common Expat Questions

**What happens if I need specialist care not available on-island?**
Comprehensive insurance plans usually include "air ambulance" or medical evacuation coverage. In severe cases, patients are typically flown to Miami, Florida (a 75-minute flight) for specialized treatment.

**Is dental and vision included?**
Basic SHIC does not cover dental or vision. These are usually optional add-ons to enhanced corporate plans. Expect to pay entirely out of pocket for dentistry if you only have basic cover.

**How much does premium insurance cost?**
If you are paying out of pocket (e.g., for dependents), an enhanced plan can cost between CI$300 to CI$800+ per month per person, depending on age and the level of coverage (especially maternity and US-coverage riders).
