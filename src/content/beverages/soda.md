## Hydration Impact of Soda

When tracking your daily water intake, a common question is whether soda "counts."

### The Beverage Hydration Index (BHI)

The BHI compares the hydrating effect of various fluids to plain water (which has an index of 100). With a score of **80**, soda is a specific choice for your hydration strategy.

- **Pros:** Often provides necessary fluids and sometimes nutrients or electrolytes.
- **Cons:** May contain sugars, caffeine, or alcohol which can act as a [Diuretic](/glossary/diuretic) or add empty calories.
