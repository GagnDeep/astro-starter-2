      ## AOG Desk and Expedited Handling

      ### What defines a dedicated AOG logistics provider?
      A dedicated AOG logistics provider is defined by their operational capacity to respond to time-critical aircraft grounding events 24/7/365. We verify that listed forwarders maintain staffed AOG desks, established next-flight-out (NFO) protocols, and direct access to airside facilities for immediate part dispatch.

      ### How are sensitive aerospace components handled?
      Sensitive components, such as engines or calibrated avionics, require specific handling procedures to prevent shock or environmental damage. Verified providers must demonstrate capabilities for specialized packaging, temperature-controlled transit, and secure chain-of-custody tracking throughout the delivery process.
