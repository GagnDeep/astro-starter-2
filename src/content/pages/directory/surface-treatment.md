      ## Chemical Processing Standards

      ### How do you separate commercial platers from aerospace vendors?
      We separate commercial platers from aerospace vendors by strictly requiring documented prime approvals or Nadcap accreditation for chemical processing. This ensures the facility maintains the exact bath chemistries, temperature controls, and traceability required for critical aviation components.

      ### What types of surface treatments are covered?
      The directory covers specialized treatments such as Chromic Acid Anodizing (CAA), Tartaric Sulfuric Anodizing (TSA), passivation, and application of solid film lubricants. These processes are essential for providing corrosion resistance and fatigue performance on machined or cast aerospace parts.
