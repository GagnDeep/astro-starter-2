      ## Nadcap Accreditation and Testing Methods

      ### Why is Nadcap accreditation required for NDT listings?
      Nadcap accreditation is required because it serves as the global cooperative accreditation program for aerospace engineering, defense, and related industries. This rigorous auditing process ensures that the testing laboratory strictly adheres to standardized inspection procedures, providing primes with reliable defect detection data.

      ### What testing methods are verified on this platform?
      We verify facilities that offer core aerospace inspection methods, including Fluorescent Penetrant Inspection (FPI), Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI), Ultrasonic Testing (UT), and Radiographic Testing (X-ray). Each facility must demonstrate the active maintenance and calibration of their specific inspection equipment.
