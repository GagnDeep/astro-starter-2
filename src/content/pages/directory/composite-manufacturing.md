      ## Controlled Environment Verification

      ### What are the clean room requirements for listed facilities?
      All verified composite manufacturers must maintain certified clean rooms, typically ISO Class 7 or better. This controlled environment is strictly required to prevent contamination during the hand layup or automated tape laying of pre-preg carbon fiber materials before they enter the curing cycle.

      ### Do these facilities operate industrial autoclaves?
      Yes. We verify that each listed facility operates fully calibrated industrial autoclaves capable of providing the necessary heat and pressure profiles. This ensures that the final composite structures achieve the required void fractions and mechanical properties demanded by aerospace primes.
