      ## Sourcing Capabilities and Verification

      ### What are the verification protocols for Tier 2 and Tier 3 suppliers?
      We require direct evidence of capabilities from all suppliers listed on our platform. This includes verifiable AS9100, Nadcap, or EASA Part-145 certificates. We do not rely on self-reported data; instead, our Data Team cross-references submitted credentials directly with the issuing regulatory or accreditation bodies to guarantee the supplier is fully compliant and actively certified.

      ### How does In-Country Value (ICV) registration impact local sourcing?
      In-Country Value (ICV) is a primary consideration for primes operating within the UAE. We explicitly mark suppliers who possess active ICV certification, allowing procurement managers to meet local content requirements efficiently. This direct verification eliminates the ambiguity often associated with broad supplier databases that do not track local economic contribution metrics.

      ### Why is this directory distinct from generic B2B marketplaces?
      Generic B2B marketplaces aggregate unverified vendor claims across multiple industries, creating significant noise for aerospace procurement. We strictly focus on aerospace and MRO, meaning our taxonomy aligns with aviation standards. If you are looking for 5-axis milling of titanium, our directory filters specifically for that capability, backed by AS9100 documentation, rather than returning general machine shops.

      ### How are new suppliers added to the directory?
      Suppliers must submit a formal listing request containing their operational capabilities and supporting documentation. Our Data Team reviews the application, verifies the provided certifications, and confirms the physical presence of the claimed equipment within the UAE. Only after this rigorous vetting process is the supplier profile activated on the platform.

      ### How do we handle discrepancies or expired certifications?
      Our system is built for accuracy and reliability. If a supplier's certification expires, or if a discrepancy is identified during routine audits, the supplier's listing is immediately updated or temporarily suspended until renewed documentation is provided. This ensures that procurement professionals are always working with current, valid compliance data.

      ### What is the process for Primes looking to connect with local subcontractors?
      Primes can freely browse the directory and access the verified capabilities of our listed subcontractors. For specialized sourcing needs, primes can utilize our subcontractor-matching newsletter, which highlights verified vendors capable of fulfilling specific manufacturing or MRO requirements, streamlining the vendor discovery process.

      ### Is there a guarantee regarding supplier performance?
      We verify capabilities, certifications, and equipment lists to ensure suppliers possess the technical requirements needed for aerospace work. However, we do not act as an intermediary or guarantor for commercial transactions. Primes are expected to conduct their own commercial due diligence and quality audits before finalizing any contracts or purchase orders.

      ### Can international suppliers be listed?
      Our primary focus is mapping the UAE's aerospace manufacturing and MRO supply chain to support local sourcing initiatives and ICV goals. Therefore, the directory exclusively features suppliers with established, verified operational facilities located within the United Arab Emirates.

      ### How frequently is the directory data updated?
      Data integrity is central to our platform. Listings are actively monitored, and suppliers are required to provide updated documentation upon the renewal of their certifications. Our Data Team also conducts periodic reviews to ensure all listed capabilities remain accurate and reflect the current operational status of the facility.

      ### What role does the hiring board play alongside the directory?
      The hiring board complements the directory by providing a specialized talent acquisition platform for the aerospace sector. While the directory helps primes find capable organizations, the jobs board helps those organizations find the highly skilled, licensed engineers and technicians required to execute the work, creating a comprehensive ecosystem for the UAE aerospace industry.

      ### What are the operational requirements for Nadcap NDT facilities?
      Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) is a critical component of the aerospace manufacturing process. For a facility to be listed under our NDT services category, they must demonstrate active Nadcap accreditation for the specific testing methods they claim, such as Fluorescent Penetrant Inspection (FPI), Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI), or Ultrasonic Testing (UT). Our verification process ensures that these facilities maintain the stringent environmental and procedural controls mandated by Nadcap, providing primes with the assurance that critical components are evaluated to the highest aerospace standards.

      ### How does the directory support AOG logistics?
      Aircraft on Ground (AOG) situations require immediate, specialized logistical support. Our aviation logistics category exclusively features freight forwarders and logistics providers that maintain dedicated 24/7 AOG desks. These providers must demonstrate their capability to handle sensitive aerospace components, navigate complex customs regulations efficiently, and coordinate rapid transport to minimize aircraft downtime. We verify their operational infrastructure to ensure they can deliver the specialized service required in critical AOG scenarios.
