Sourcing these components requires vetting suppliers who are experienced with thick-gauge, shielded cabling handling 400V to over 1000V. \n\n### What specific equipment is needed for 400V+ assembly?\n\nHigh-voltage assemblies require specialized tooling such as ultrasonic welding stations for large cross-section copper or aluminum cables, and automated stripping equipment capable of safely removing multi-layer shielding without damaging the core conductor. We ensure matched suppliers possess this capital equipment in-house.\n\n### How is isolation resistance validated?\n\nIsolation resistance is validated using hipot (high potential) testing, where a voltage significantly higher than operating levels is applied to ensure the insulation system's integrity. Suppliers must demonstrate active calibration logs for their testing rigs to ensure every shipped harness meets safety margins.\n\n### Why does ultrasonic welding matter for high-current cables?\n\nUltrasonic welding provides a low-resistance, solid-state metallurgical bond that minimizes voltage drop and localized heating at the connector interface. This is critical for high-current EV applications where traditional crimps may degrade thermally over time."
