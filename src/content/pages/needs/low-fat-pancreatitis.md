      ## Navigating Low Fat Pancreatitis with Treats

      When your dog is dealing with low fat pancreatitis, picking the wrong treat can lead to immediate vet visits. Transparency isn't a luxury; it's a necessity.

      ### What Works Best
      Single-ingredient treats are the gold standard here. For example, [Beef Liver](/ingredients/beef-liver) or [Sweet Potato](/ingredients/sweet-potato) allow you to test one variable at a time without exposing your dog to hidden allergens.

      ### Recommended Approach
      1. Establish a baseline diet.
      2. Introduce one new single-ingredient treat at a time.
      3. Wait 48 hours for any reactions.
      4. Remember the 10% rule (use our [Treat Allowance Calculator](/tools/treat-allowance)).

      *Consult your veterinarian before making dietary changes for chronic conditions.*
