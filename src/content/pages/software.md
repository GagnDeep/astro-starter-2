    heading: "What makes catering software different from restaurant POS?"
    text_content: |
      Catering software differs from restaurant POS systems by focusing on event-based pipelines, future-dated inventory, and Banquet Event Orders (BEOs). While a restaurant POS processes immediate, anonymous transactions, catering platforms manage a long sales cycle: capturing a lead, sending a proposal, tracking deposits, and generating specialized prep lists for specific dates.

      Retail point-of-sale systems fail caterers because they cannot handle complex, multi-stage invoicing or associate a single order with multiple service locations and staffing requirements.
