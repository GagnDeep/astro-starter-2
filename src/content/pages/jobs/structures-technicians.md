      ## Structural Maintenance Capabilities

      ### What specific structural skills are employers seeking?
      Employers are actively seeking technicians with documented experience in reading complex engineering blueprints, performing precise sheet metal riveting, and executing approved structural repair manuals (SRM). Advanced skills in cold working, advanced fastener installation, and structural integrity assessments are highly prioritized by UAE MROs.

      ### Are composite repair skills required for these roles?
      Yes, modern aircraft require extensive composite repair capabilities. Many roles specify experience in carbon fiber layup, vacuum bagging, and localized heat curing, directly mirroring the manufacturing processes utilized by [composite manufacturing](/directory/composite-manufacturing/) subcontractors.
