      ## Precision Machining and CAM Software

      ### What CAM software proficiency is required?
      Proficiency in advanced CAM software is strictly required for these roles. Employers actively seek programmers with deep experience in platforms like Mastercam, Siemens NX, or CATIA, specifically for generating collision-free, optimized toolpaths for simultaneous 5-axis milling operations.

      ### Do these roles require experience with exotic alloys?
      Yes, experience with exotic aerospace alloys is a critical requirement. Programmers and setup machinists must understand the specific feeds, speeds, and tool wear characteristics associated with machining Inconel, Titanium, and high-nickel alloys to prevent work hardening and ensure surface integrity.
