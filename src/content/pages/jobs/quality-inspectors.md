      ## Regulatory Compliance and Auditing

      ### What certifications are employers looking for in auditors?
      Employers are looking for auditors who possess formal lead auditor certification (e.g., ISO 9001, AS9100) and hold specific regulatory approvals from authorities like the GCAA or EASA. Candidates must have a proven track record of conducting internal audits and managing corrective action procedures.

      ### What are the duties of a manufacturing quality inspector?
      A manufacturing quality inspector is responsible for verifying that fabricated parts meet exact engineering specifications. This involves using precision measurement tools like CMMs (Coordinate Measuring Machines) and ensuring that [CNC machining subcontractors](/directory/cnc-machining/) adhere to strict dimensional tolerances before parts enter the supply chain.
