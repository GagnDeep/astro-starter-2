      ## Avionics Systems and Troubleshooting

      ### What electronic systems experience is required?
      Employers require deep experience in troubleshooting and maintaining complex flight management systems, radar, navigation instruments, and communication suites. Specialists must demonstrate proficiency with automated test equipment and the ability to interpret intricate wiring diagrams for modern fly-by-wire aircraft.

      ### Do avionics roles involve software management?
      Yes, modern avionics maintenance heavily involves software management. Technicians are frequently responsible for executing field loadable software (FLS) updates, managing electronic flight bag (EFB) integration, and ensuring the secure configuration of aircraft network domains.
