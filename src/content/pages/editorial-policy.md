      At NRI Direct UK, our editorial mandate is strictly defined: we provide actionable, verified guidance on consular applications based on current, real-world enforcement.

      ### Independent Testing
      All document guides and checklists are regularly verified against current UK and Indian application requirements. We achieve this through independent testing, monitoring real application outcomes, and analysing the specific reasons for rejections at VFS centres. We do not rely solely on official published FAQs, as these are often incomplete or outdated.

      ### Strictly Independent
      We are an independent commercial entity. Our content must never imply official accreditation or government affiliation. We explicitly state our independence on every page and maintain a clear boundary between our paid services and official government processes. We do not publish news, editorial commentary, or political opinions; our content is purely instructional and advisory.

      ### Corrections
      If a reader or client identifies a factual error in our guidance, or if application requirements change, we update our documentation promptly. Every commercial guide displays a visible 'last updated' date. We omit unverified statistics, credentials, or claims rather than fabricating them.
