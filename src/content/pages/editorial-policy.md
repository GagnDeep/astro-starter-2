      Our mission is to provide Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs) with independent, actionable technology analysis that is completely free of vendor influence.

      ## Independence and Integrity
      We do not accept payment to review or rank any software product. Every technology stack and integration discussed on this site is rigorously tested in realistic wealth-management environments before any recommendation is made. Our assessments of vendor risk management, data security, and system interoperability are based entirely on technical merit and our firsthand experience in the field.

      ## Corrections and Updates
      The wealth tech landscape moves quickly, and facts change. Corrections to methodology, service definitions, or product capabilities are issued promptly on the relevant pages, with transparent update logs noting the date of the change and the nature of the correction.

      ## Citation and Sources
      We rely exclusively on primary sources — regulators, standards bodies, and official vendor documentation — for all technical specifications and compliance requirements. Every editorial citation to a primary source is a followed link. We never invent statistics, awards, or test results.
