      Our core mandate is to provide objective, technically rigorous analysis for managing partners navigating legal technology. Our credibility is our product. We do not invent statistics, fabricate credentials, or manipulate technical capabilities.

      ## How do we test legal technology?
      We implement and review AI tools within standard law firm workflows, ensuring they meet data privacy and compliance standards before recommending them. We evaluate underlying security models and verify claims of zero data retention through technical documentation and independent audit reports, such as SOC2. We never publish self-reviews or unverified vendor claims.

      If our analysis of a technology or governance requirement is incorrect, we will issue a public correction and update the relevant policy framework immediately. Our reviews are focused on the practical realities of legal operations, prioritizing client confidentiality above all else.
