      Aerospace UAE operates deliberately outside UAE media-licensing law. We are strictly a directory, jobs board, and reference data repository. We do not engage in journalism, news reporting, or editorial commentary.

      Our content is restricted to verifiable facts: equipment lists, certifications, active job postings, and organizational capabilities. We never invent or fabricate statistics, credentials, awards, testimonials, review counts, case studies, or customer names. If a capability or certification cannot be independently verified against direct supplier documentation or public registries, it is omitted.

      Errors in directory listings or reference data are corrected immediately upon verification.

      ## Data Boundaries

      ### What defines our non-journalism constraint?
      We do not interview executives, publish press releases, cover industry events, or write opinion pieces. Our platform acts solely as a technical lookup tool for procurement professionals and recruiters.

      ### How do we handle corrections?
      Since our data is entirely fact-based, any identified inaccuracies (such as an incorrectly transcribed machine tolerance or an expired certificate) are updated immediately by our Data Team upon receipt of verifying documentation.
