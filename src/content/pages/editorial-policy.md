      ## Editorial Independence

      We enforce strict editorial independence. Our research and content are built by aggregating regulatory filings, vendor API documentation, and primary enforcement actions. Our editorial team does not accept payment for favorable reviews or altered rankings.

      ## Verification and Methodology

      Any performance claims regarding technology platforms, transition timelines, or structural economics are back-tested against published, verifiable firm data. We do not rely on vendor marketing projections. We publish our methodology alongside our claims.

      ## Corrections

      We are committed to accuracy. Corrections to factual errors will be published promptly on the relevant page, with a note detailing the change and the date it was made. If you identify a factual error, please contact us immediately.
