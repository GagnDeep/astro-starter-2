      ## How do you evaluate a portfolio management system when alternative investments are a primary asset class?

      The Portfolio Management System (PMS) is the operational heart of an RIA. It dictates how you trade, how you report performance to clients, and how you bill for your services. The primary failure point in technology selection is purchasing a PMS based on marketing dashboards rather than data architecture and integration depth.

      Our evaluation focuses on the critical differentiation point for modern RIAs: the ability to seamlessly handle alternative investments (private equity, real estate, direct lending) alongside traditional public market securities.

      We have developed our own criteria for evaluating these systems, prioritizing data normalization, multi-custodial reconciliation, and the flexibility of the billing engine.

      ### What are the three non-negotiable criteria for a multi-custodial reconciliation engine?

      1.  **Alternative Investment Handling:** Does the system natively support IRR reporting, capital call tracking, and complex ownership structures, or does it rely on manual spreadsheet uploads?
      2.  **Reconciliation Speed:** How quickly and accurately does the system process daily custodial data feeds?
      3.  **Billing Flexibility:** Can the engine handle complex tiered fee structures, householding exclusions, and automated fee debits across multiple custodians?

      ## Why do promised future integrations pose a systemic operational risk to an independent RIA?

      A PMS is only as good as its integrations. We strongly recommend testing the API endpoints or native integrations between your shortlisted PMS and your chosen CRM (e.g., Salesforce, Wealthbox). Data must flow bidirectionally to ensure that client updates in the CRM automatically reflect in the billing and reporting engine, eliminating dual data entry.

      Avoid vendors that promise "future roadmap" integrations. Base your decision entirely on currently functioning, verifiable data flows.
