      ### Common Mistakes & Edge Cases
      - **Overfeeding:** Remember the 10% rule. Too much of a rich organ or dense protein can cause diarrhea. Check out our [Calorie Calculator](/tools/calorie-calculator) and [Treat Allowance](/tools/treat-allowance) tools.
      - **Sourcing:** Always ensure it's sourced and made in the USA. Avoid chemically treated versions. Read more on [how to read labels](/blog/reading-treat-labels).

      ### FAQs
      **Can puppies eat this?**
      Yes, but in extreme moderation due to developing digestive tracts.

      **How should I store it?**
      Keep in a cool, dry place. Dehydrated forms last longer. Learn [how to store treats properly](/blog/how-to-store-treats).
