      The EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542) phases in requirements over a ten-year period. For engineering and compliance teams, missing these dates means immediate market exclusion. This guide provides the definitive timeline.

      ## Core DPP Implementation

      The Digital Product Passport (DPP) itself has a hard enforcement date. By this date, a battery must have a physical QR code linked to a live data system.

      *   **18 February 2027:** Mandatory for all LMT (Light Means of Transport) batteries, EV (Electric Vehicle) batteries, and Industrial batteries with a capacity greater than 2 kWh.

      ## Carbon Footprint Declarations

      Carbon footprint requirements phase in before the full DPP is mandated. Manufacturers must provide a verifiable declaration.

      | Battery Type | Declaration Required | Performance Classes Defined | Maximum Threshold Enforced |
      | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
      | **EV Batteries** | 18 February 2025 | 18 August 2026 | 18 February 2028 |
      | **Industrial (>2 kWh)** | 18 February 2026 | 18 August 2027 | 18 February 2029 |
      | **LMT Batteries** | 18 August 2028 | 18 February 2030 | 18 August 2031 |

      *Note: The calculation methodology is complex. See our [Carbon Footprint Guide](/blog/how-to-calculate-carbon-footprint/) for a technical breakdown.*

      ## Recycled Content Targets

      The regulation forces a shift toward circular supply chains by mandating minimum thresholds of recycled cobalt, lead, lithium, and nickel.

      ### Declaration of Recycled Content
      *   **18 August 2028:** Mandatory documentation of the share of cobalt, lead, lithium, or nickel recovered from waste present in active materials.

      ### Mandatory Minimums (Phase 1: August 2031)
      *   **Cobalt:** 16%
      |   **Lead:** 85%
      |   **Lithium:** 6%
      |   **Nickel:** 6%

      ### Mandatory Minimums (Phase 2: August 2036)
      *   **Cobalt:** 26%
      |   **Lead:** 85%
      |   **Lithium:** 12%
      |   **Nickel:** 15%

      ## Supply Chain Due Diligence
      *   **18 August 2025:** Economic operators must establish and implement a supply chain due diligence policy regarding social and environmental risks.
