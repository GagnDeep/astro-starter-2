## The Core Debate

When optimizing for weight loss, the primary battle is against hunger. Both protein and fiber are critical tools for satiety, but they work through fundamentally different mechanisms.

Protein drives the **Thermic Effect of Food (TEF)** and preserves lean body mass, while fiber provides **physical bulk** and slows gastric emptying.

## Mythbusting

1. **"High protein damages kidneys"**: Only true if you have pre-existing kidney disease. For healthy individuals, it's safe. [NEEDS CONFIRMATION]
2. **"Fiber isn't digested, so it doesn't matter"**: Soluble fiber feeds gut microbiome, producing SCFAs that regulate appetite hormones. [NEEDS CONFIRMATION]
