      - question: "How much does it cost to start an RIA?"
        answer: "Starting a bare-bones state-registered RIA requires approximately $15,000 to $25,000 in year-one capital. This budget covers legal entity formation, initial compliance consulting, regulatory filing fees, and foundational technology like a CRM and billing software. Institutional setups will cost significantly more."
      - question: "Do I need to take the Series 65 exam?"
        answer: "You must pass the Series 65 exam unless you hold a qualifying professional designation such as the CFP, CFA, CIC, ChFC, or PFS. Most state regulators grant immediate waivers for these designations, allowing you to register as an investment adviser representative without the exam."
      - question: "Should I register with the SEC or my state?"
        answer: "You must register with state regulators if your regulatory assets under management (RAUM) are below $100 million. Once your RAUM exceeds $100 million, you are generally required to transition your registration to the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) within 90 days."
