      - title: What does SB 253 require for emissions reporting?
        description: >
          The Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act (SB 253) requires companies to report Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions annually. This mandate specifically targets direct emissions and purchased electricity for covered entities meeting the revenue threshold.
      - title: What does SB 261 require for financial risk assessment?
        description: >
          The Climate-Related Financial Risk Act (SB 261) requires companies to publish a biennial climate-related financial risk report. This assessment must align with the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) framework.
      - title: Are third-party audits required for compliance?
        description: >
          Yes, SB 253 mandates assurance engagements performed by an independent third-party auditor. The requirement phases in, starting with limited assurance for Scope 1 and 2, and eventually moving to reasonable assurance.

        answer: >
          The initial reporting deadlines are determined by the specific statute and subsequent CARB rulemaking. Generally, SB 253 requires Scope 1 and 2 reporting in 2026 on 2025 data, while SB 261 requires the first financial risk report by January 1, 2026.
