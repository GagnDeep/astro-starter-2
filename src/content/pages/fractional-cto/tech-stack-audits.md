      Over time, law firms accumulate layers of software subscriptions—many of which are redundant, underutilized, or actively introducing security risks. A comprehensive technology audit cuts through the bloat, providing an objective assessment of your current architecture and actionable steps for consolidation.

      ## How do you conduct a legal tech audit?
      We conduct a legal tech audit by mapping every software subscription against actual firm usage, identifying redundant capabilities, and assessing the security posture of each vendor. We review SSO integrations, data flow diagrams, and contract terms to build a streamlined, cost-effective architecture.

      This independent review is crucial before making major software purchases or negotiating renewals. Because we operate as a [Fractional Legal CTO](/fractional-cto/), our loyalty is to your firm's profitability, not a vendor's quota.
