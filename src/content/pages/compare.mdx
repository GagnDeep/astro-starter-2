## Which is best for you?
There is no single "best" strategy. It depends entirely on your financial situation and goals.
- If you are constrained by borrowing capacity, a high yield strategy might be necessary. Check your capacity with our [Borrowing Power Calculator](/tools/borrowing-power-calculator).
- If you have high taxable income, you might prefer the tax benefits of a high growth, negatively geared property. Learn more about [Negative Gearing](/glossary/negative-gearing).
