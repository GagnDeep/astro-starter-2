# About Us

The **Institute of Property** is an education hub designed for the modern, data-driven Australian investor.

We believe that property investing shouldn't rely on "gut feeling" or the latest hype from self-proclaimed gurus. It requires objective analysis, a solid understanding of the numbers, and a clear strategy.

## Our Mission
To empower everyday Australians to build wealth through property by providing transparent, actionable, and mathematically sound education and tools.

## What we offer
- **Free Tools:** Like our [Rental Yield Calculator](/tools/rental-yield-calculator) and [Borrowing Power Calculator](/tools/borrowing-power-calculator).
- **Reference Library:** Deep dives into [Strategy](/library/property-investment-strategy), [Tax](/library/investment-property-tax), and [Finance](/library/financing-investment-property).
- **Glossary:** Clear definitions of industry jargon, from [LVR](/glossary/lvr) to [Negative Gearing](/glossary/negative-gearing).
