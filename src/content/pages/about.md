      ## Our Mission

      The Institute of RIA provides B2B media and research for principals, COOs, and compliance leads at registered investment adviser firms. We map the exact operational, legal, and technological workflows required to launch and operate a firm without conflicting vendor spin.

      We recognize that the transition to independence is a structural engineering problem. We provide the blueprints.

      ## Important Disclosures

      The Institute of RIA is a media and research organization. **We are not an accreditation body, a regulatory agency, or a professional institute.**

      We do not provide legal, compliance, or investment advice. Our content is for informational purposes only and must be independently verified against primary regulatory sources before implementation.
