    text_content: "The Institute of Retirement was founded to combat the overwhelming amount of thinly veiled sales pitches dominating the international retirement space. We are not a professional academic institute, nor are we a real estate brokerage masquerading as one. We are an independent editorial team dedicated to publishing a fully disclosed, honestly scored comparison methodology for popular retirement destinations.\n\n### How do we evaluate retirement destinations?\n\nWe evaluate retirement destinations based on a four-pillar methodology that strictly analyzes cost of living, visa accessibility, healthcare quality, and tax liability using verified governmental and international data. We refuse to use crowdsourced anecdotes or promotional materials, relying instead on official consumer price indices, consular requirements, and bilateral tax treaties.\n\nOur data is updated constantly. When visa requirements shift—such as changes to the Spanish IPREM or the end of the Portuguese NHR tax regime—our guides reflect those changes immediately with clear, dated citations.\n\n### Why transparency matters in international relocation\n\nTransparency matters in international relocation because the financial and emotional costs of making the wrong choice are devastating for retirees on a fixed income. A failure to understand the nuances of dual taxation or the specific underwriting terms of expat health insurance can result in thousands of dollars in unexpected liabilities, which is why we strictly separate verifiable facts from subjective lifestyle advice.\n\nWe monetize this site modestly through affiliate partnerships and lead generation for trusted services like specialized tax advisors and international movers. However, these relationships never influence our destination scores or the facts we present.
