      ## Our Fractional CTO
      As the founder of AdvisorCTO, I serve as a Fractional CTO exclusively for wealth management firms. My background involves years of hands-on experience scaling technology infrastructure for financial services.

      ### Track Record
      Led technology integration for a large RIA, merging disparate CRM and portfolio management systems while architecting robust cybersecurity governance policies. We never fabricate credentials, clients, logos, or case studies — this space is reserved for verifiable achievements.

      ### Our Philosophy
      We believe in assessing the real risks, aligning technology with business objectives, architecting robust integrations (like connecting Redtail or Wealthbox with Orion and eMoney), and acting decisively. We sit on your side of the table when negotiating with vendors, ensuring your firm's data remains secure and compliant.
