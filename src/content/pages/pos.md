  - _name: "TextBlock"
    heading: "How to choose between proprietary and off-the-shelf hardware?"
    text_content: |
      Proprietary hardware often withstands heat, grease, and spills better than consumer iPads. Terminals built specifically for restaurants usually have hardwired internet connections and integrated card readers, which can reduce points of failure.

      However, iPads offer lower upfront costs and easier replacement if a screen breaks. You can walk into an Apple Store and buy a replacement immediately, whereas proprietary hardware requires shipping time from the vendor. Your decision should weigh the harshness of your environment against your need for immediate hardware replacement.
