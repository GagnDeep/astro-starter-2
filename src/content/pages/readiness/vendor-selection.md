      Choosing the right software provider is critical for ensuring compliance with the EU Battery Regulation. The market is crowded, and capabilities vary significantly.

      ### Key Evaluation Criteria

      When assessing vendors, focus on these core areas:

      1.  **Data Integration capabilities:** Can the platform integrate with your existing ERP and PLM systems?
      2.  **Supply Chain Traceability:** Does it support deep multi-tier supply chain mapping?
      3.  **Verification Support:** How does the platform facilitate third-party audits by notified bodies?
      4.  **Standards Compliance:** Is the solution built on open standards (e.g., W3C Verifiable Credentials) as required by the EU?
