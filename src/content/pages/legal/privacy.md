# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how the Institute of Property collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

## 1. Information We Collect
We only collect information you voluntarily provide through our forms (such as newsletter signups or course waitlists). This may include your name and email address.

## 2. How We Use Information
Your information is used solely to provide the services you requested, such as sending the data pack or notifying you about course openings.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
We use a secure, privacy-first capture system. Form submissions are securely stored, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

## 4. Analytics
We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how our tools and library are used so we can improve them. We honour "Do Not Track" signals.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have questions about this policy, please [Contact Us](/contact).
