      ## Listing and Job Post Costs

      ### How much does it cost to list a company in the directory?
      It costs a flat rate of 500 AED per month to maintain a verified listing in the supplier directory. This fee covers the initial capability verification audit, ongoing compliance checks for certifications like AS9100, and placement in the relevant technical categories.

      ### What is the pricing structure for the hiring board?
      The pricing structure for the hiring board is a simple flat rate of 250 AED per individual job post for a 30-day duration. There are no volume discounts or hidden resume access fees; you pay only to list verified, highly specialized engineering and MRO roles.
