    heading: Our Approach to Research
    text_content: >
      The Washington DC Review editorial team consists of researchers dedicated to parsing federal acquisition regulations, agency guidelines, and official procurement portals. Our goal is to translate dense bureaucratic requirements into actionable, linear roadmaps for small businesses.


      We rely exclusively on primary sources, including SAM.gov, the Small Business Administration (SBA), the General Services Administration (GSA), and the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR). We synthesize this information to provide clear guidance on registrations, certifications, and compliance mandates.


      We are not a government agency, nor do we offer legal, financial, or compliance advice. Our content is designed to be a starting point for businesses entering the federal marketplace, equipping them with the vocabulary and foundational knowledge needed to engage effectively with contracting officers or specialized consultants.
