    text_content: |
      Public Protection Classification (PPC) codes directly influence an underwriter's ability to deploy maximum line capacity. A classification of 1 indicates superior water supply and fire department response, allowing for maximum discretionary credits. Conversely, a PPC of 9 or 10 denotes unprotected areas, requiring severe capacity restrictions and mandatory percentage deductibles.
