      In Tier 1 coastal zones or severe convective storm corridors, flat deductibles expose carriers to unsustainable frequency and severity aggregation. Percentage deductibles, typically ranging from 1% to 5% of the total insured value at the time of loss, enforce risk-sharing and ensure the insured maintains a vested interest in storm mitigation.

      Evaluating this risk profile requires a systematic review of the underlying exposures, loss history, and implemented controls. The underwriter must look past initial representations to verify the operational reality and model potential severities accurately.

      This section details the critical path for assessing applications in this category, ensuring that risk selection aligns with broader portfolio objectives.
