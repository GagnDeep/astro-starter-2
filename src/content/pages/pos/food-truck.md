  - _name: "LeftRight"
    heading: "How does offline mode really work?"
    text_content: |
      True offline mode is critical. When you are parked at a festival with ten thousand people congesting the cellular network, your POS must still capture credit card data securely.

      Some systems, like <a href="/pos/toast-vs-square/" rel="sponsored nofollow">Square</a>, handle offline payments by storing encrypted card data locally and processing the batch when connectivity returns. You assume the risk of declined cards, but the line keeps moving. Other systems require a constant connection and will grind your service to a halt if the signal drops. Always test a system's offline queue limits before buying.
