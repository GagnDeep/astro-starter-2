      Our editorial policy is governed by a single principle: **Regulatory accuracy is our entire product.**

      ### Verification Standards

      All regulatory requirements, dates, and obligations published on this site are cross-referenced directly with EUR-Lex and official publications from the European Commission. We do not rely on secondary sources, press releases, or vendor interpretations.

      ### Handling Unverified Information

      The regulatory landscape is still evolving, particularly concerning the detailed delegated acts. If a specific requirement or technical standard is proposed but not yet finalized, it is strictly labeled as "unverified" or "proposed."

      ### Corrections Policy

      Corrections to regulatory data are made immediately upon verification against official EU sources. All pages carry a visible last-verified date in the byline to ensure readers know how current the information is.

      ### Independence

      Our editorial content is entirely independent of our monetization strategy. We do not alter our analysis, omit facts, or endorse specific solutions based on vendor sponsorships.
