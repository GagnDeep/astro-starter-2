    text_content: >
      Our editorial process is grounded in primary source verification. When detailing federal contracting processes, we consult the official documentation provided by the relevant government agencies. This includes the System for Award Management (SAM.gov) user guides, the Small Business Administration (SBA) standard operating procedures, and the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR).


      We synthesize these complex regulations into actionable steps for small businesses. However, we do not conduct independent audits or provide legal analysis. We explicitly state that we are not a government agency and do not offer legal or compliance advice.


      ### Corrections and Updates

      Federal regulations and procurement systems are subject to frequent changes. If we misstate a regulation, process, or requirement, we are committed to correcting the error promptly. We update the affected page and clearly note the date of the correction to ensure transparency with our readers.


      We encourage all readers to independently verify critical information by consulting the primary government sources linked in our articles or by seeking counsel from qualified legal or compliance professionals before making business decisions.
