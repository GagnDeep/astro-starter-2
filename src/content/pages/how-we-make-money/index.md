    text_content: >
      Washington DC Review operates as a free reference hub for small businesses navigating the federal contracting landscape. We do not charge our readers for access to our guides, articles, or resources. To sustain our operations and maintain the quality of our content, we rely on a referral-based revenue model.


      We generate revenue through lead generation and referral bounties to specialized GovCon service providers. This means that if you choose to engage a service provider, consultant, or software platform through specific links or forms on our site, we may earn a commission or referral fee.


      ### Disclosure and Independence

      Our editorial integrity is paramount. While we maintain financial relationships with certain service providers, these relationships do not dictate our editorial content or the objective facts we present regarding federal regulations.


      We are committed to transparency. Every commission link on our site carries a clear, plain-language disclosure above the first such link and above the fold, ensuring you are fully aware of the relationship before you click. We encourage you to evaluate any service provider independently to ensure they meet your specific business needs.
