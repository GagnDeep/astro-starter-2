      Battery Digital Passport provides its core informational content entirely for free. To support the ongoing research and maintenance required to keep this information accurate, we rely on two revenue streams.

      ### 1. The Readiness Toolkit

      We sell a comprehensive, paid Readiness Toolkit. This toolkit includes detailed gap analysis templates, project management roadmaps, and technical evaluation matrices designed for compliance teams. This is our primary source of revenue. **We do not sell compliance software.**

      ### 2. Vendor Sponsorships and Commissions

      We occasionally partner with software vendors who offer solutions relevant to the battery passport ecosystem. These partnerships may take the form of sponsored placements or affiliate commissions.

      **Our strict rules for sponsorships:**
      *   Any link that results in a commission is clearly marked with `rel="sponsored nofollow"`.
      *   We provide a plain-language disclosure above the fold and before any sponsored link appears on a page.
      *   Sponsorships never influence our independent analysis or the verified pricing data we publish.
