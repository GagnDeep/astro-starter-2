      ## Our Revenue Model

      The Institute of RIA provides its content freely to readers. To support our research and editorial operations, we generate revenue through vendor referrals and sponsorships from organizations serving the RIA market.

      ## Affiliate and Referral Links

      Some of the links on our site are affiliate or referral links. This means we may earn a commission if you click on the link and make a purchase or engage a vendor's services.

      We adhere strictly to FTC guidelines and SEO best practices. Every commission link carries a `rel="sponsored nofollow"` attribute, and we provide a plain-language disclosure above the fold and above the first such link on any page where they appear.

      Our editorial recommendations are never dictated by affiliate relationships. We review vendors based on our objective criteria and data architecture requirements.
