      Transparency is a core requirement for establishing trust as your strategic technology leader. Our revenue model is designed to align our incentives entirely with your firm's success.

      ## Direct Engagements
      We operate exclusively on monthly retainers and fixed-scope engagements for our Fractional CTO services. When a wealth management firm hires us to conduct a vendor risk assessment, architect a system integration, or build a cybersecurity governance plan, they are paying for our unbiased expertise.

      ## Zero Vendor Commissions
      We do not accept referral fees, kickbacks, or affiliate commissions from software vendors (e.g., Orion, Redtail, Wealthbox, eMoney, or any other platform). If we recommend a product, it is because we have technically verified that it is the best fit for your specific operational and compliance requirements.

      If we ever provide a commission link (e.g., to a generic service), it will carry a `rel="sponsored nofollow"` attribute, and a plain-language disclosure will be prominently displayed above the fold and before the first such link.
