      NRI Direct UK is a private, independent consultancy. We are completely transparent about how we fund our operations and the services we provide.

      ### Professional Fees
      Our primary source of revenue is charging a flat fee for our professional document checking and application assistance services. When a client engages us to audit their OCI, passport, or visa application pack, they pay us directly for that expert review.

      This fee is entirely separate from, and in addition to, any mandatory government, consular, or VFS Global processing fees. We do not handle the payment of official government fees on your behalf.

      ### Independence Guaranteed
      Because we are paid directly by our clients, we work exclusively in their best interest. We receive no funding, kickbacks, or commissions from the Indian government, any consulate, or VFS Global. This absolute independence allows us to provide objective, critical advice on how to successfully navigate the application process and avoid common pitfalls.

      If we ever use commission-generating links for ancillary services (such as specialized courier or photography services), a clear, plain-language disclosure will be prominently displayed above the fold and immediately preceding the first such link. All such links will carry the `rel="sponsored nofollow"` attribute in compliance with our editorial standards.
