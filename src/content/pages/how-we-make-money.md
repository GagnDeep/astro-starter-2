      Aerospace UAE is a subscription-based platform. We generate revenue exclusively through the following channels:

      1.  **Paid Supplier Listings:** Subcontractors in the UAE aerospace and MRO supply chain pay a monthly or annual subscription fee to be listed in our verified directory.
      2.  **Recruiter Job Postings:** Employers and recruiters pay to list specialized engineering and MRO roles on our hiring board.
      3.  **Subcontractor-Matching Newsletter:** A premium subscription service that matches primes with capable, verified local subcontractors.

      We do not accept payment for editorial content, news coverage, or unverified claims. We do not participate in affiliate marketing for generic B2B services, and any links to such services (if they existed) would carry `rel="sponsored nofollow"` with a plain-language disclosure. Our focus remains solely on connecting primes with verified UAE suppliers and facilitating specialized aerospace hiring.

      ## Financial Integrity

      ### Why is there no advertising on the site?
      Advertising models incentivize click-generation and unverified marketing copy, which degrades the utility of a technical directory. By relying solely on listing fees, we align our financial incentives with the accuracy and utility of our supply chain data.

      ### Are verified capabilities influenced by payment?
      No. While suppliers must pay for a listing, the verification standards are identical for every tier. If a supplier fails our compliance verification, their payment is refunded and the listing is denied.
