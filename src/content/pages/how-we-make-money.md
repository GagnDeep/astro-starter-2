    text_content: "## A transparent, performance-based business model\n\nAI Wire Harness exists to streamline the procurement of custom wire harnesses. To maintain the quality of our matching engine and support our continuous supplier verification processes, we operate a specialized monetization model that aligns our incentives with both buyers and manufacturers.\n\n### How does the pay-per-lead model work?\n\nOur service is completely free for engineers, buyers, and procurement staff submitting RFQs. When an RFQ enters our system, our engine matches the technical requirements with verified manufacturers. These manufacturers pay a set fee to access the contact information and full technical data package associated with that specific, highly-qualified lead.\n\n### Do manufacturers pay for preferential ranking?\n\nNo. We do not sell premium directory placements, \"sponsored\" search results, or boosted rankings. Manufacturers receive RFQs strictly based on their verified technical capabilities matching the buyer's stated requirements. A shop cannot pay to receive an aerospace RFQ if they do not possess verified AS9100 certification.\n\n### Are there affiliate or commission links on this site?\n\nWhile our primary model is pay-per-lead, we may occasionally include links to specific tooling, raw materials, or industry services. Any link that results in a commission or referral fee is strictly marked with a `rel=\"sponsored nofollow\"` tag. Furthermore, a plain-language disclosure is always placed above the first such link, above the fold, to ensure complete transparency."
