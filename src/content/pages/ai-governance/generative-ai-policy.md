      ## What must a law firm AI policy include?
      A law firm AI policy must include explicit prohibitions on inputting client data into public models, mandate human-in-the-loop verification for all generated outputs, and list explicitly authorized tools. It establishes a hard line between experimental usage and client-facing deliverables.
