    text_content: >
      Washington DC Review is built on a single premise: the barrier to entry in federal contracting should be capability, not information. For too long, the complexities of government procurement—from SAM.gov registration to maintaining DCAA compliance—have discouraged capable small businesses from serving the federal government.


      Our mission is to dismantle that barrier by providing clear, authoritative, and actionable reference material. We translate dense bureaucratic requirements into linear roadmaps, empowering small business owners to navigate the federal marketplace with confidence.


      ### Who We Serve

      Our resources are designed specifically for small business owners who are attempting to win their first federal contract or are looking to expand their footprint through GSA Schedules and socio-economic set-asides. We strip away the jargon and focus on the practical steps required to become an eligible, competitive vendor.


      While our name suggests a news outlet, we are fundamentally a reference hub. We do not chase the daily news cycle of government appropriations; instead, we focus on the enduring foundational knowledge required to succeed in government contracting. Please note that we are not a government agency and do not offer legal or compliance advice.
