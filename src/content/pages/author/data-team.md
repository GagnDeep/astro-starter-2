      The Data Team at Aerospace UAE is responsible for independently verifying all subcontractor capabilities, certifications, and active job postings across the platform.

      Operating strictly as a data research unit, the team ensures that all platform data remains factual, verified, and free from editorial commentary. Every AS9100, EASA Part-145, or Nadcap certification claimed on this site is cross-referenced by the Data Team against official accreditation bodies before publication.

      ## Verification Process

      ### What are the core verification duties of the team?
      The primary duty is validating the documentation submitted by aerospace suppliers. If a CNC machining facility requests a listing, the Data Team requires a copy of their active AS9100 certification and an equipment list, which is then verified against their operational output.

      ### Does the Data Team produce journalism?
      No. The Data Team operates strictly as data validators. They do not write editorial pieces, news, or opinion columns, ensuring the platform remains an objective procurement index.
