    text_content: "### How much income do you need for a Spanish Non-Lucrative Visa?\n\nTo qualify for a Spanish Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV) in 2024, the primary applicant must demonstrate a stable, passive monthly income of exactly 400% of the Spanish IPREM, which equates to roughly €2,400 per month. You must also prove an additional 100% of the IPREM (about €600) for each dependent family member accompanying you on the application.\n\nThe *Indicador Público de Renta de Efectos Múltiples* (IPREM) is the reference index used in Spain for granting aid, subsidies, and determining visa requirements. For the Non-Lucrative Visa, the government requires proof that you will not become a burden on the state.\n\n### Can I use savings instead of a monthly pension for the NLV?\n\nYes, you can use substantial liquid savings instead of a monthly pension for the NLV, provided the total amount covers the required IPREM multiples for the entire duration of the visa (initially one year). For a single applicant in 2024, this means demonstrating a minimum balance of approximately €28,800 in an accessible bank account, though consulates often prefer a combination of savings and passive income.\n\n### What are the health insurance requirements for the Spanish NLV?\n\nThe health insurance requirements for the Spanish NLV mandate that you purchase a comprehensive private policy from a provider authorized to operate in Spain. The policy must offer coverage equivalent to the Spanish national health system, featuring zero copayments, zero deductibles, and no clauses for repatriation, effectively ensuring you have full coverage from day one.\n\nThis insurance requirement is one of the most strictly enforced aspects of the application.
