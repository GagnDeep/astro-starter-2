      An insured's operational resilience is inextricably linked to their third-party IT service providers. Contingent business interruption (CBI) underwriting models the systemic aggregation risk of shared cloud infrastructure outages, requiring detailed vendor mapping and the enforcement of extended waiting period deductibles to mitigate portfolio-wide shock losses.

      Evaluating this risk profile requires a systematic review of the underlying exposures, loss history, and implemented controls. The underwriter must look past initial representations to verify the operational reality and model potential severities accurately.

      This section details the critical path for assessing applications in this category, ensuring that risk selection aligns with broader portfolio objectives.

      The primary pricing mechanisms involve base rate derivations adjusted by specific modifiers for exposure quality. Actuarial baseline models are supplemented by judgmental credits or debits, ensuring that the final premium adequately reflects the true marginal risk added to the portfolio.
