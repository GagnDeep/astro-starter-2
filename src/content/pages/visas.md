Our directory focuses strictly on passive income visas, retirement specific visas, and investment-based residency options that do not require you to seek local employment.\n\n### How much income do you need for a Spanish Non-Lucrative Visa?\n\nTo qualify for a Spanish Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV) in 2024, the primary applicant must demonstrate a stable, passive monthly income of exactly 400% of the Spanish IPREM, which equates to roughly €2,400 per month. You must also prove an additional 100% of the IPREM (about €600) for each dependent family member accompanying you on the application.\n\nUnderstanding these income multipliers is crucial. Many countries tie their financial requirements to a fluctuating economic index (like Spain's IPREM). We actively monitor these indices to ensure you have the correct, up-to-date targets before you begin the costly application process.\n\n### Can I work remotely on a retirement visa?\n\nNo, you generally cannot work remotely on a traditional retirement or passive income visa, as these are specifically designed for individuals who will not participate in any form of employment, local or foreign. If you intend to continue working, you must apply for a specific digital nomad visa or an entrepreneur visa, which carry entirely different tax implications and application criteria.\n\nMake sure your income sources align with the visa's legal definitions.
