      ## What is Hydrolyzed Protein?

      **Definition:** Protein broken down into tiny pieces so the immune system doesn't recognize it. Often used for severe allergies.

      ### Why It Matters
      Understanding terms like hydrolyzed protein is crucial for seeing through marketing jargon. The pet food industry relies on complex labels to obscure true nutritional value.
