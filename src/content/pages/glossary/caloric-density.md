      ## What is Caloric Density?

      **Definition:** How many calories are packed into a specific weight. Crucial for the 10% treat rule.

      Understanding terms like caloric density is crucial for seeing through marketing jargon. The pet food industry relies on complex labels to obscure true nutritional value.
