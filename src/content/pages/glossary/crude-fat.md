      ## What is Crude Fat?

      **Definition:** The minimum fat content. High crude fat can be problematic for dogs prone to pancreatitis.

      ### Why It Matters
      Understanding terms like crude fat is crucial for seeing through marketing jargon. The pet food industry relies on complex labels to obscure true nutritional value.
