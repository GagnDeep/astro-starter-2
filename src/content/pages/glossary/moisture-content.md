      ## What is Moisture Content?

      **Definition:** The maximum amount of water in the product. Higher moisture means you are paying for water.

      ### Why It Matters
      Understanding terms like moisture content is crucial for seeing through marketing jargon. The pet food industry relies on complex labels to obscure true nutritional value.
