      ## What is Crude Protein?

      **Definition:** The guaranteed minimum amount of protein based on nitrogen content. Note: This does not account for digestibility.

      ### Why It Matters
      Understanding terms like crude protein is crucial for seeing through marketing jargon. The pet food industry relies on complex labels to obscure true nutritional value.
