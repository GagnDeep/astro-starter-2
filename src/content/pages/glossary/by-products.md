      ## What is By Products?

      **Definition:** Non-rendered, clean parts, other than meat, derived from slaughtered mammals. Often a sign of lower quality.

      ### Why It Matters
      Understanding terms like by products is crucial for seeing through marketing jargon. The pet food industry relies on complex labels to obscure true nutritional value.
