      ## What is Novel Protein?

      **Definition:** A protein source the dog has never eaten before (like venison or rabbit), used to bypass existing allergies.

      ### Why It Matters
      Understanding terms like novel protein is crucial for seeing through marketing jargon. The pet food industry relies on complex labels to obscure true nutritional value.
