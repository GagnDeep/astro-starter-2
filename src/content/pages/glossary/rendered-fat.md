      ## What is Rendered Fat?

      **Definition:** Fat that has been cooked down and purified. Can be vague (e.g., 'animal fat') which is a red flag.

      ### Why It Matters
      Understanding terms like rendered fat is crucial for seeing through marketing jargon. The pet food industry relies on complex labels to obscure true nutritional value.
