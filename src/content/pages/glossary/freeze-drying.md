      ## What is Freeze Drying?

      **Definition:** A process of freezing then applying a vacuum to remove ice as vapor. Maintains structure and raw nutritional profile.

      Understanding terms like freeze drying is crucial for seeing through marketing jargon. The pet food industry relies on complex labels to obscure true nutritional value.
