## The Form 1099-K Threshold Confusion

If you accept payments via Stripe, PayPal, Square, Venmo for Business, or Shopify, you've likely heard about Form 1099-K.

### The Congressional Phase-In
After delaying the planned $600 threshold implementation, the IRS announced a phased approach for Form 1099-K reporting:

- **2024 / 2025 Phase-In:** Payment processors issue Form 1099-K to payees who receive over **$5,000** in gross payments (and state-specific lower thresholds in CA, MA, VT, VA, MD, IL).

### The Golden Rule of 1099 Reporting
> **Whether you receive a 1099-K, 1099-NEC, or ZERO tax forms at all, you are legally required to report ALL taxable business income earned on Schedule C.**

The IRS matches 1099-K totals reported by Stripe with your Schedule C Line 1. If your Schedule C gross income is lower than the total on your 1099-K forms, an automated IRS mismatch notice (CP2000) will be triggered.
