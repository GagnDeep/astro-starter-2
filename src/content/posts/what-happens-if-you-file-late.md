## Don't Panic: How IRS Penalties Actually Work

If April 15 is approaching and you haven't finished your tax numbers or don't have enough cash in your bank account to pay your bill, rule #1 is: **DO NOT IGNORE IT.**

The IRS penalizes people for not filing far more harshly than for not paying on time.

---

## Penalty #1: Failure-to-File vs. Penalty #2: Failure-to-Pay

- **Failure-to-File Penalty (IRC § 6651(a)(1)):** **5% per month** of the unpaid tax amount, up to a maximum cap of 25%.
- **Failure-to-Pay Penalty (IRC § 6651(a)(2)):** **0.5% per month** of the unpaid tax amount.

*Notice:* The penalty for not filing on time is **10 TIMES BIGGER** than the penalty for filing on time but paying late!

---

## The Free IRS Extension Solution (Form 4868)

Filing an extension via **IRS Form 4868** is **100% free** and gives you 6 additional months (until **October 15**) to file your tax return.

> **Important Fact:** Form 4868 gives you an extension of time to FILE your return, NOT an extension of time to PAY. Pay whatever estimated amount you can by April 15 to minimize interest.
