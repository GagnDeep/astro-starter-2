## What Changed for the 2025 Tax Year?

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) released inflation adjustments in Revenue Procedure 2024-40 affecting the 2025 tax year. Here is the fast, jargon-free summary of key changes for self-employed contractors.

### 1. Increased Standard Deductions
- **Single Filers:** Increased to **$15,000** (up from $14,600 in 2024).
- **Married Filing Jointly:** Increased to **$30,000** (up from $29,200 in 2024).
- **Head of Household:** Increased to **$22,500** (up from $21,900 in 2024).

### 2. Social Security Wage Base Increase
The maximum earnings subject to the 12.4% Social Security tax cap rose to **$176,100** for 2025 (up from $168,600 in 2024). Earnings above $176,100 only pay the 2.9% Medicare tax portion of self-employment tax.

### 3. Federal Income Tax Brackets Shifted
Tax brackets shifted upward by roughly 2.8% to combat inflation, widening lower tax brackets so less of your income gets pushed into higher brackets.
