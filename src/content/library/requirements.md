# Battery Passport Requirements 2027

By February 18, 2027, economic operators placing LMT (Light Means of Transport) or industrial batteries > 2kWh on the market must ensure each unit has an active digital product passport.

## Core Data Requirements

1. **General Information:** Battery model, manufacturer, manufacturing date.
2. **Material Composition:** Chemistry, critical raw materials (Cobalt, Lithium, Nickel, Lead).
3. **Carbon Footprint:** Total carbon footprint, share per life cycle stage.
4. **Recycled Content:** Share of recycled cobalt, lithium, and nickel.
