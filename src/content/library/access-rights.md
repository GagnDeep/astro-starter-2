# Access Rights Requirements

The EU Battery Regulation introduces strict mandates concerning access rights.

## Key Provisions

1. Data must be verifiable and linked to a unique identifier.
2. The economic operator is responsible for accuracy.
3. Specific methodologies will be defined in upcoming Delegated Acts.

## What to do next

Ensure your data architecture can support the Access Rights requirements by mapping your internal systems against the JRC guidelines.
