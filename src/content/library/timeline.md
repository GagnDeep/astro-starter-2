# EU Battery Regulation Timeline

The EU Battery Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2023/1542) entered into force on August 17, 2023, and applies from February 18, 2024. However, the requirements are phased in over a decade.

Understanding this timeline is critical. The "Battery Passport" mandate in 2027 is the endpoint of a process that requires data gathering years in advance.

## Key Deadlines by Year

### 2024
- **February 18, 2024:** Regulation generally applies. Basic obligations for economic operators begin.
- **August 18, 2024:** Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) obligations begin for portable batteries.

### 2025
- **February 18, 2025:** Mandatory **Carbon Footprint Declaration** for **EV batteries**. This is the first major data hurdle. Economic operators must declare the carbon intensity of their supply chain.
- **August 18, 2025:** Supply chain due diligence policies must be implemented and third-party verified.

### 2026
- **February 18, 2026:** Mandatory **Carbon Footprint Declaration** expands to **LMT (Light Means of Transport) batteries** and **Industrial batteries > 2kWh**.
- **August 18, 2026:** Minimum performance and durability requirements apply to portable batteries.

### 2027
- **February 18, 2027:** The **Digital Battery Passport** becomes mandatory for **EV batteries** and **LMT batteries**. The QR code must be physically present on the battery, resolving to the decentralized database.

### 2028
- **February 18, 2028:** Maximum carbon footprint thresholds are enforced for EV batteries. Batteries exceeding the limit cannot be sold.
- **August 18, 2028:** Mandatory **Recycled Content Declaration** for EV, LMT, and Industrial batteries.

### 2033
- **August 18, 2033:** Mandatory minimum **Recycled Content Targets** apply (16% Cobalt, 85% Lead, 6% Lithium, 6% Nickel).

## Strategy for Economic Operators

Do not wait for 2027. The 2025 carbon footprint declaration for EV batteries requires primary data from your supply chain.

If you are starting your compliance project in late 2025, you will likely fail the 2027 passport mandate because you will not have the historical supplier data required to populate it.
