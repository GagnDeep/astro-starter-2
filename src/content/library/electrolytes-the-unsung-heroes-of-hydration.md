# Electrolytes: The Unsung Heroes of Hydration

While water is crucial, [Electrolytes](/glossary/electrolytes) like sodium and potassium dictate where that water goes.

## The Role of Sodium
Sodium is the primary cation in the extracellular fluid. When you sweat, you lose significant amounts of sodium, which must be replaced to maintain blood volume.

## Clinical Context
Ignoring electrolytes during heavy endurance events can lead to [Hyponatremia](/glossary/hyponatremia), a dangerous condition where blood sodium levels plummet.
