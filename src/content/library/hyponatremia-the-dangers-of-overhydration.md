# Hyponatremia: The Dangers of Overhydration

Drinking too much water without electrolytes is incredibly dangerous. This condition, known as [Hyponatremia](/glossary/hyponatremia), causes cells to swell.

## The Mechanism
When blood sodium levels drop below 135 mEq/L, water shifts into the [Intracellular Fluid](/glossary/intracellular-fluid) via osmosis.

## Edge Cases
Endurance athletes who drink plain water excessively are at the highest risk.
