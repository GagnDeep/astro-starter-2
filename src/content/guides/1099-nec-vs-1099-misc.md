# 1099-NEC vs 1099-MISC

If you are a [Freelancer](/types/freelancers/) or [Gig Worker](/types/gig-workers/), you will likely receive a 1099 form. Knowing the difference between the 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC is the difference between a free tax return and a $120+ software upsell.

## Overview
Historically, independent contractor income was reported on Form 1099-MISC (Box 7). In 2020, the IRS reintroduced Form 1099-NEC specifically for Nonemployee Compensation.

### Comparison Table

| Feature | 1099-NEC | 1099-MISC |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stands For** | Nonemployee Compensation | Miscellaneous Information |
| **Common Uses** | Freelance work, gig economy (Uber, Doordash), consulting fees. | Rent, prizes, awards, medical payments, royalties. |
| **Filing Requirement** | If paid $600 or more during the year. | If paid $600 or more (for most categories) or $10 in royalties. |
| **Self-Employment Tax?** | **Yes.** Income here triggers Schedule SE. | **Usually No.** Unless it's tied to a trade or business. |

### Worked Example: The Software Upsell
David works a day job and receives a [W-2](/guides/w2-form-guide/). He also drives for a rideshare app on weekends, earning $4,000, reported on a 1099-NEC.
- He goes to a commercial tax website offering a "Free Edition."
- He enters his W-2 fine.
- When he enters his 1099-NEC, the software blocks him. It states that to file **Schedule C** (Business Income) and **Schedule SE** (Self-Employment Tax), he must upgrade to the "Self-Employed" tier for $119.

**The Solution:** David qualifies for [IRS Free File](/guides/irs-free-file/) because his total income is under the $79k limit. He can file both his W-2 and 1099-NEC for absolutely free using a Free File Alliance partner or an alternative like FreeTaxUSA.

### Edge Cases
- **Receiving both:** It is entirely possible to receive a W-2 and a 1099-NEC from the *same* company, but it usually triggers IRS scrutiny (you generally can't be both an employee and a contractor for the exact same duties).
- **Under $600:** Even if you didn't earn enough to receive the physical 1099 form, you are still legally required to report the income on your taxes.
