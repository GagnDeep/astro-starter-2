# Tax Penalty Rules

When filing your taxes, especially as an [Independent Contractor](/types/independent-contractors/) or [Freelancer](/types/freelancers/), underpayment penalties are a common concern.

## Overview
The IRS generally requires you to pay taxes as you earn income. If you do not pay enough through withholding or estimated tax payments, you may face a penalty.

### Key Facts
- You must pay at least 90% of the tax for the current year, or 100% of the tax shown on the return for the prior year, whichever is smaller.
- If your prior year Adjusted Gross Income (AGI) was more than $150,000, the safe harbor jumps to 110% of the prior year's tax.
