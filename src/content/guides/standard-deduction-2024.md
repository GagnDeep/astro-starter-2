# Standard Deduction 2024

When considering whether **free tax filing software** is best for you, understanding the Standard Deduction for the 2024 tax year is critical.

## Overview
The standard deduction is a specific dollar amount that reduces the amount of income on which you're taxed. For many, it eliminates the need to [Itemize Deductions](/guides/itemized-deductions/) and makes filing simple enough to qualify for truly free software.

### Key Facts and Figures (2024 tax year, filed in 2025)
*Source: IRS Rev. Proc. 2023-34*

| Filing Status | 2024 Standard Deduction | Increase from 2023 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Single | $14,600 | +$750 |
| Married Filing Jointly | $29,200 | +$1,500 |
| Head of Household | $21,900 | +$1,100 |
| Married Filing Separately | $14,600 | +$750 |

*Additional Deduction for 65+ or Blind: $1,950 per qualifying individual (if Married) or $1,550 (if Single/Head of Household).*

### Worked Example: The Power of the Standard Deduction
Sarah is a single filer who earned $50,000 in 2024.
- **Without Deduction:** Taxed on $50,000.
- **With Standard Deduction:** She subtracts $14,600. Her new *Taxable Income* is $35,400.
Because she takes the standard deduction, she doesn't need to hunt for receipts for charitable giving or medical expenses. This simplicity means she can almost certainly use [IRS Free File](/guides/irs-free-file/) or [FreeTaxUSA](/blog/freetaxusa-review) without paying an upsell fee.

### Edge Cases
- **Dependents:** If you can be claimed as a dependent on someone else's return, your standard deduction is limited to either $1,300 or your earned income plus $450 (up to the regular standard deduction of $14,600).
- **Dual-Status Aliens:** Nonresident aliens cannot typically take the standard deduction.

### Common Mistakes
- **Paying to Claim It:** Many commercial tax prep tools push users to upgrade to "Deluxe" versions just because they have a question about the standard deduction. Don't fall for it.
- **Itemizing unnecessarily:** If your itemized expenses are less than $14,600 (Single), it is mathematically worse for you to itemize.
