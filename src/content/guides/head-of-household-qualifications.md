# Head of Household Qualifications

When considering whether **free tax filing software** is best for you, understanding Head of Household Qualifications is critical.

## Overview
Head of Household Qualifications is an essential part of the tax code. According to the IRS, millions of taxpayers qualify for free filing options if their income is below the threshold ($79,000 for 2023).

### Key Facts and Figures (2024)
- **Eligibility:** Always verify limits against primary IRS sources.
- **Impact:** Misunderstanding this can lead to missed refunds or penalties.

If John makes $50,000 and uses the standard deduction, his taxable income is significantly lower. Using tools like the Tax Refund Calculator can help verify these numbers.
