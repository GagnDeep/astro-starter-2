## What Is Schedule C?

If you earned income as an independent contractor, sole proprietor, or single-member LLC, **Schedule C (Profit or Loss From Business)** is the tax form where you report your business income and deduct your business expenses.

Your net profit calculated at the bottom of Schedule C transfers directly to your main Form 1040 tax return and Schedule SE (Self-Employment Tax).

---

## Key Schedule C Sections Explained

### Part I: Income
- **Line 1 (Gross receipts or sales):** Total money collected from clients, 1099-NEC forms, 1099-K forms, Stripe, CashApp, and cash payments before any fees.
- **Line 2 (Returns and allowances):** Refunds given back to unhappy clients or canceled orders.
- **Line 7 (Gross income):** Net income remaining after cost of goods sold.

### Part II: Expenses (Where Write-Offs Live)
- **Line 8 (Advertising):** Facebook ads, Google ads, domain names, promotional stickers.
- **Line 10 (Commissions and fees):** Stripe processing fees, PayPal merchant charges, platforms fees.
- **Line 11 (Contract labor):** Payments to 1099 freelancers, editors, virtual assistants (if paid $600+, issue a 1099-NEC).
- **Line 13 (Depreciation / Sec 179):** Writing off laptops, camera bodies, monitors, and gear.
- **Line 18 (Office expense):** Software subscriptions (Figma, Adobe, Notion, Google Workspace), office supplies.
- **Line 20b (Rent on other business property):** Coworking space memberships (WeWork, local desks).
- **Line 24a (Travel):** Flights, hotel stays, Ubers for out-of-town client trips.
- **Line 24b (Deductible meals):** 50% of client business meals.
- **Line 30 (Expenses for business use of your home):** Home office deduction calculated via Form 8829 or simplified method ($5/sqft).
- **Line 31 (Net profit or loss):** The golden number! Gross income minus total expenses.

---

## What Happens After Line 31?

1. **Self-Employment Tax:** Your Line 31 profit goes to Schedule SE to compute your 15.3% Social Security and Medicare taxes.
2. **Income Tax:** Your Line 31 profit minus 50% SE tax deduction and Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction flows into your Form 1040 income tax calculation.
