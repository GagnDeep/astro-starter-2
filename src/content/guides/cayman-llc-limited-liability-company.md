Introduced in 2016, the **Cayman Islands Limited Liability Company (LLC)** was created in direct response to demands from the US investment funds industry for an offshore vehicle that perfectly mirrored the Delaware LLC.

It is a hybrid entity that combines the separate legal personality of an Exempted Company with the internal flexibility and tax transparency of an Exempted Limited Partnership.

## Key Features of the Cayman LLC

1.  **Separate Legal Personality:** Unlike a partnership, an LLC is a distinct legal entity. It can own assets, sue, and be sued in its own name. The liability of its members is limited to their capital contributions.
2.  **Members, Not Shareholders:** An LLC has "Members" who hold "LLC Interests" (rather than shareholders holding shares).
3.  **No Share Capital:** An LLC does not issue shares with a nominal or par value. A member's interest is based on their capital account and their agreement with the other members.
4.  **Management:** An LLC can be managed by its members (Member-Managed) or by appointed managers (Manager-Managed). The managers do not need to be members.
5.  **Fiduciary Duties:** One of the most attractive features of the LLC Act is that it allows the LLC Agreement to expand, restrict, or completely eliminate the traditional fiduciary duties owed by members or managers to the LLC (save for the irreducible core duty to act in good faith).

## Formation and The LLC Agreement

Forming an LLC requires at least one member.
1.  **Registration Statement:** A short registration statement is filed with the Registrar of LLCs, containing the LLC's name and registered office.
2.  **LLC Agreement:** This is the private internal governing document (equivalent to an Operating Agreement in the US). It dictates how profits are allocated, how the LLC is managed, and the voting rights of members. It provides maximum contractual freedom.

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. General Partner (GP) Entities
The vast majority of Cayman LLCs are used to act as the corporate General Partner for a Cayman Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) in a private equity structure. The LLC provides limited liability for the fund managers running the GP, while allowing for flexible allocation of "carry" (carried interest) among the principals via the LLC Agreement.

### 2. Joint Ventures
Because the LLC Agreement can be heavily customized to reflect complex commercial arrangements (including bespoke voting mechanisms and profit allocations that don't rely on issuing different classes of shares), the LLC is the preferred vehicle for offshore joint ventures.

### 3. Holding Companies and SPVs
LLCs are frequently used as Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) to hold specific assets within a broader fund structure, particularly where US tax transparency is desired.

## Comparison: LLC vs. Exempted Company

| Feature | Limited Liability Company (LLC) | Exempted Company |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ownership** | Members holding LLC Interests | Shareholders holding Shares |
| **Governance Document** | LLC Agreement (Private) | Memorandum & Articles (M&A) |
| **Management** | Members or Managers | Board of Directors |
| **Fiduciary Duties** | Can be modified/eliminated by contract | Strict common law duties apply |
| **Capital Maintenance** | Highly flexible | Strict rules on capital reduction |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is a Cayman LLC subject to US taxes?**
Like a Delaware LLC, a Cayman LLC can usually "check the box" to be treated as a disregarded entity or a partnership for US federal tax purposes, meaning it is tax-transparent.

**Does a Cayman LLC need to hold an annual general meeting?**
No. There is no statutory requirement for an LLC to hold an annual general meeting of its members.

**Can an Exempted Company convert to an LLC?**
Yes. The LLC Act provides a streamlined statutory process for a Cayman Exempted Company to seamlessly convert into a Cayman LLC without affecting its existing contracts or liabilities.
