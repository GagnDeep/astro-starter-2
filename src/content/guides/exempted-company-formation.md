The Exempted Company is the principal vehicle used by international businesses and investors in the Cayman Islands. It is highly flexible and primarily used for activities carried out outside the Cayman Islands.

## Key Features of an Exempted Company

*   **Tax Neutrality:** No direct taxes in the Cayman Islands (no income, corporate, capital gains, or withholding taxes).
*   **Tax Exemption Certificate:** An exempted company can obtain an undertaking from the Cayman Islands Government that it will remain free from any future taxes for up to 20 years (extendable to 30 years).
*   **Confidentiality:** The register of shareholders is not open to public inspection.
*   **Flexibility:** No requirement for directors or shareholders to be resident in the Cayman Islands (subject to Economic Substance rules).
*   **Capital:** No minimum capital requirements.

## The Incorporation Process

1.  **Name Selection:** Choose a unique name. It does not need to include "Limited" or "Ltd."
2.  **Registered Office:** The company must have a registered office in the Cayman Islands, provided by a licensed service provider.
3.  **Memorandum and Articles of Association (M&A):** Draft and file the M&A with the Registrar of Companies. These outline the company's rules and powers.
4.  **Due Diligence (KYC):** Service providers must collect Know Your Customer (KYC) documentation on directors, shareholders, and ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs) to comply with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations.
5.  **Incorporation Fees:** Pay the initial government registration fee and the registered office fee.

## Timeline and Costs

Incorporation is usually swift. Standard incorporation takes 3-5 business days. Express services (within 24 hours) are available for a premium fee. Setup costs vary depending on the service provider but typically range between US$3,000 and US$5,000, including first-year government fees.

## Post-Incorporation Requirements

*   **Registers:** Must maintain a Register of Members, Register of Directors and Officers, and a Register of Mortgages and Charges.
*   **Annual Return:** File an annual return in January declaring that the company's operations are primarily outside the Cayman Islands.
*   **Beneficial Ownership Register:** Maintain a UBO register, which is filed confidentially with the competent authority (not publicly accessible).
