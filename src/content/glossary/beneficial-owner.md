A **Beneficial Owner** (often referred to as the Ultimate Beneficial Owner or UBO) is defined as the natural person(s) who ultimately owns or controls a legal entity.

In the Cayman Islands, the Beneficial Ownership Transparency Act requires corporate service providers to maintain an up-to-date register of beneficial owners for in-scope entities. A person is typically considered a beneficial owner if they hold, directly or indirectly, 25% or more of the shares or voting rights in the company, or if they exercise ultimate effective control over the entity.
