# Term 15 of Hydration

Understanding the specific terminology around hydration is crucial for making informed decisions about your health.

## Definition and Mechanism

Term 15 refers to the complex interplay of fluid dynamics within the human body. It is often misunderstood in popular media. When this mechanism is activated, the body shifts fluids from the intracellular space to the extracellular space.

## Edge Cases

- **Athletes:** High-intensity exercise significantly alters how this mechanism functions.
- **Older Adults:** As we age, the body's ability to regulate this process diminishes, increasing the risk of dehydration.
