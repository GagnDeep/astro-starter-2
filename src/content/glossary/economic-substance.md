**Economic Substance (ES)** refers to the legislative requirement (under the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act) that entities registered in the Cayman Islands conducting certain "relevant activities" must demonstrate they have actual economic substance in the jurisdiction.

This involves conducting core income-generating activities (CIGA) on-island, having an adequate physical presence, and incurring adequate operating expenditure in the Cayman Islands.
