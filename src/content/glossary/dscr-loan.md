## Definition

**DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) Loan** is a key term in US real estate and tax law. For Gulf-based investors, understanding this concept is critical to navigating cross-border investments effectively.

### Relevance to UAE Investors

Understanding the mechanics of DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) Loan can mean the difference between a tax-efficient, high-yielding investment and one burdened by unexpected US tax liabilities. Always consult with a cross-border tax specialist when dealing with US assets.
