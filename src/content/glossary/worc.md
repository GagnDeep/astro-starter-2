**WORC (Workforce Opportunities & Residency Cayman)** is the government department responsible for labor and immigration in the Cayman Islands. It processes work permits, permanent residency applications, and enforces the requirement that employers prioritize Caymanian labor via the JobsCayman portal before hiring expatriates.
