---
title: "Natural Language Processing (NLP)"
definition: "The branch of AI focused on the interaction between computers and human language, enabling machines to understand, interpret, and generate text."
---

Understanding **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** is essential for any professional looking to apply practical AI in their daily workflows.

By grasping this concept, you move beyond surface-level usage of chat interfaces and begin to understand the underlying mechanics of how these tools operate. This knowledge is critical for troubleshooting, optimizing prompts, and designing automated systems.
