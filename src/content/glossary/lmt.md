# Light Means of Transport (LMT) Battery

Under the EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542), a **Light Means of Transport (LMT) battery** is defined as any battery that is sealed, weighs 25 kg or less, and is designed specifically to provide electric power for the traction of wheeled vehicles that can be powered by the electric motor alone or by a combination of motor and human power.

Crucially, this category is distinct from EV batteries and includes devices not type-approved as motor vehicles under existing EU vehicle frameworks.

## Common Examples

*   E-bikes (Pedelecs)
*   Electric scooters (E-scooters)
*   Electric mopeds (weighing under 25kg)
*   Hoverboards

*Note: Wheelchairs and mobility mobility scooters are generally excluded from the LMT classification if they fall under specific medical device regulations, often being classed instead as portable batteries depending on weight.*

## Compliance Obligations for LMT Batteries

The creation of the LMT category was a major addition in the final text of the regulation, recognizing the massive growth of micromobility. LMT batteries face almost identical obligations to heavy EV batteries, meaning micromobility manufacturers face a steep compliance curve.

Key deadlines include:
*   **February 18, 2026:** Mandatory Carbon Footprint Declaration.
*   **February 18, 2027:** Mandatory Digital Battery Passport. The battery must carry a QR code linking to a decentralized data record containing the 90+ required attributes.
*   **August 18, 2028:** Mandatory Recycled Content Declaration.

## Design for Removal and Replacement

A critical mandate specifically targeting LMT (and portable) batteries is the "removability and replaceability" requirement.

Starting in February 2027, LMT batteries must be designed so that they can be easily removed and replaced by an **independent professional** using commonly available tools. The manufacturer cannot use proprietary glues or software locks that force the consumer to return to the original manufacturer for a battery swap.

The Digital Product Passport plays a key role here: the restricted tier of the passport must contain the detailed dismantling and replacement manuals required by those independent professionals.

## What to do next

If you manufacture or import e-bikes or e-scooters into the EU, you are the economic operator. You must immediately begin mapping your cell suppliers' carbon emissions to meet the 2026 declaration deadline, and select a software vendor to host your required Digital Product Passports for the 2027 deadline.
