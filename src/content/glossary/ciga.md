**CIGA (Core Income Generating Activities)** is a foundational concept within the Cayman Islands Economic Substance regime.

If an entity conducts a "relevant activity" (e.g., fund management, banking, or headquarters business), it must demonstrate that the central, profit-generating activities of that business—its CIGA—are actually being performed within the Cayman Islands, rather than being outsourced offshore.
