**Net Asset Value (NAV)** represents the total value of an investment fund's assets minus its liabilities. In the context of Cayman Islands Mutual Funds, the accurate calculation and reporting of NAV to investors is a critical regulatory focus, often delegated to independent Fund Administrators.
