**FATCA (Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act)** is a United States federal law that requires Foreign Financial Institutions (FFIs), including Cayman Islands mutual funds and banks, to identify US account holders and report their financial account information to the US Internal Revenue Service (IRS).

The Cayman Islands implements FATCA via a Model 1 Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) with the US. Cayman FIs report the data to the Cayman Tax Information Authority (TIA), which then automatically exchanges it with the IRS.
