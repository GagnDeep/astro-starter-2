**Know Your Customer (KYC)** is the mandatory process by which financial institutions, corporate service providers, and professionals identify and verify the identity of their clients.

In the Cayman Islands, KYC is a critical component of the Anti-Money Laundering Regulations. It involves collecting certified passports, proof of address, source of wealth declarations, and conducting background checks to ensure clients are not involved in financial crime.
