---
title: "Fine-tuning"
definition: "Taking a pre-trained AI model and training it further on a smaller, specific dataset to specialize it for a particular task or domain."
---

Understanding **Fine-tuning** is essential for any professional looking to apply practical AI in their daily workflows.

By grasping this concept, you move beyond surface-level usage of chat interfaces and begin to understand the underlying mechanics of how these tools operate. This knowledge is critical for troubleshooting, optimizing prompts, and designing automated systems.
