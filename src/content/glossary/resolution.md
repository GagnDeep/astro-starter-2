# Resolution

**Definition:** The detail an image holds, commonly measured in dimensions (e.g., 1920x1080) or density (DPI).

## Technical Context

Understanding resolution is essential for proper document conversion and handling. It directly impacts how data is processed, stored, and displayed on screen versus in print.
