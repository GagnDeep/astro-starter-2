The **Financial Reporting Authority (FRA)** is the central financial intelligence unit of the Cayman Islands. Its primary role under the Proceeds of Crime Act is to receive, request, analyze, and disseminate disclosures of financial information concerning the proceeds of criminal conduct (Suspicious Activity Reports, or SARs) to local law enforcement and overseas authorities.
