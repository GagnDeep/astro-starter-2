## What is Metabolic Water?

In the study of hydration and human physiology, **metabolic water** plays a crucial role. It is interconnected with how our cells communicate, how organs function, and how we respond to environmental stressors.

### Clinical Significance

Understanding this concept is essential for preventing conditions like [Dehydration](/glossary/dehydration) or [Hyponatremia](/glossary/hyponatremia). It informs how we should approach rehydration, particularly after intense exercise or in extreme climates.
