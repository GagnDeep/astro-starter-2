The **DTR** usually refers to the register maintained by CIMA under the Directors Registration and Licensing Act (DRLA). However, in Cayman Islands corporate practice, the primary document is the **Register of Directors and Officers (ROD)**, which every Exempted Company must maintain at its registered office and file confidentially with the Registrar of Companies.
