# What is a Change Order?

A Change Order is an amendment to a contract outlining modifications to the scope of work, timeline, or budget.

In the context of electronic signatures and document management, understanding the exact terminology is crucial for ensuring legal compliance and security.

### Why it matters for Small Businesses

Understanding this concept helps you:
1. Ensure your contracts hold up in a dispute.
2. Avoid overpaying for enterprise features you don't need.
3. Communicate clearly with clients about how their data is secured.
