---
title: "Chain-of-Thought Prompting"
definition: "A prompting technique where the AI is instructed to explain its reasoning step-by-step before arriving at a final answer."
---

Understanding **Chain-of-Thought Prompting** is essential for any professional looking to apply practical AI in their daily workflows.

By grasping this concept, you move beyond surface-level usage of chat interfaces and begin to understand the underlying mechanics of how these tools operate. This knowledge is critical for troubleshooting, optimizing prompts, and designing automated systems.
