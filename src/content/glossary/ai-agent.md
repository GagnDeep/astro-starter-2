---
title: "AI Agent"
definition: "An AI system capable of autonomous action, able to use tools (like web browsers or APIs) and make decisions to achieve a specified goal."
---

Understanding **AI Agent** is essential for any professional looking to apply practical AI in their daily workflows.

By grasping this concept, you move beyond surface-level usage of chat interfaces and begin to understand the underlying mechanics of how these tools operate. This knowledge is critical for troubleshooting, optimizing prompts, and designing automated systems.
