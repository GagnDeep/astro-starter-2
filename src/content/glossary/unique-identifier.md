# Unique Identifier

Under the EU Battery Regulation, a **Unique Identifier** is defined with specific technical and legal parameters. Understanding this definition is critical for determining which compliance obligations apply.

## Context

The classification impacts timelines, data requirements, and whether a Digital Product Passport is mandatory.
