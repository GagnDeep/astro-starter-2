The **Local Companies (Control) Act (LCCA)** is the key piece of legislation designed to protect the domestic Cayman Islands economy. It dictates the "60/40 rule," requiring that any ordinary company trading locally must be at least 60% beneficially owned and directed by Caymanians, unless they secure a specific Local Companies Control License.
