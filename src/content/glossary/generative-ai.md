---
title: "Generative AI"
definition: "A category of AI systems designed to create new content—such as text, images, code, or audio—based on patterns learned from training data."
---

Understanding **Generative AI** is essential for any professional looking to apply practical AI in their daily workflows.

By grasping this concept, you move beyond surface-level usage of chat interfaces and begin to understand the underlying mechanics of how these tools operate. This knowledge is critical for troubleshooting, optimizing prompts, and designing automated systems.
