An **Exempted Company** is the principal corporate vehicle used by international businesses and investors in the Cayman Islands.

It is called "exempted" because it is exempt from certain requirements of the Companies Act (such as holding an annual general meeting and keeping a public register of members) provided that its operations are conducted mainly *outside* of the Cayman Islands. It can also obtain an undertaking from the government guaranteeing exemption from any future taxes for up to 20 years.
