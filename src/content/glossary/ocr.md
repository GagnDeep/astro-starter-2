# Ocr

**Definition:** Optical Character Recognition. The electronic conversion of images of typed or handwritten text into machine-encoded text.

## Technical Context

Understanding ocr is essential for proper document conversion and handling. It directly impacts how data is processed, stored, and displayed on screen versus in print.
