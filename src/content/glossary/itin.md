## Definition

**ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number)** is a key term in US real estate and tax law. For Gulf-based investors, understanding this concept is critical to navigating cross-border investments effectively.

### Relevance to UAE Investors

Understanding the mechanics of ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) can mean the difference between a tax-efficient, high-yielding investment and one burdened by unexpected US tax liabilities. Always consult with a cross-border tax specialist when dealing with US assets.
