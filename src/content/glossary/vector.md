# Vector

**Definition:** Images defined by 2D points connected by lines and curves to form polygons and other shapes, allowing infinite scaling.

## Technical Context

Understanding vector is essential for proper document conversion and handling. It directly impacts how data is processed, stored, and displayed on screen versus in print.
