A **Zone Employment Certificate (ZEC)** is a specialized type of work permit issued exclusively to employees of companies registered in Cayman Enterprise City (the Special Economic Zone). ZECs are highly desirable because they are processed within 5 working days and bypass the local job advertising requirements of standard WORC permits.
