# Potassium

In the context of hydration, **Potassium** is a critical concept that dictates how fluids function within the body.

## Detailed Definition
Potassium refers to a state or mechanism involved in maintaining fluid homeostasis. When the body's internal environment shifts—due to sweating or metabolic processes—this factor plays a pivotal role.

## Clinical Context
In clinical settings, understanding Potassium helps diagnose conditions like [Dehydration](/library/hydration-science-reference-topic-4) or overhydration.
