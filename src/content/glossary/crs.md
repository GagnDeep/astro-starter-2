The **Common Reporting Standard (CRS)**, developed by the OECD, is an information standard for the Automatic Exchange of Information (AEOI) regarding financial accounts on a global level.

The Cayman Islands is an early adopter of CRS. Cayman Financial Institutions (FIs) are required to identify the tax residency of their account holders and report specified financial account information to the Cayman Islands Tax Information Authority (TIA). The TIA then automatically exchanges this information with the tax authorities of the jurisdictions in which the account holders are tax resident.
