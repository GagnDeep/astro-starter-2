The **Limited Partnership Agreement (LPA)** is the private, internal contract that governs the relationship between the General Partner and the Limited Partners of a Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership. It outlines the economic terms of the fund, including profit allocation waterfalls, management fees, and voting rights.
