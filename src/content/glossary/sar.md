A **Suspicious Activity Report (SAR)** is a confidential document filed by the MLRO of a financial institution with the Cayman Islands Financial Reporting Authority (FRA). A SAR is mandatory whenever the institution knows, suspects, or has reasonable grounds to suspect that funds are the proceeds of money laundering or terrorist financing.
