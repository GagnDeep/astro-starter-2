**REEFS (Regulatory Enhanced Electronic Forms Submission)** is the online portal operated by the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA). Financial institutions and their service providers use REEFS to submit fund registrations, file FARs, and apply for VASP or SIBA licenses securely.
