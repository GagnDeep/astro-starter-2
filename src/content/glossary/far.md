The **Fund Annual Return (FAR)** is a prescribed form that all CIMA-regulated Mutual Funds and Private Funds must file annually, along with their audited financial statements, within six months of their financial year-end. The FAR captures high-level operational and financial data, which CIMA uses for macro-prudential supervision and statistical reporting.
