---
title: "Foundation Model"
definition: "A large, generalized AI model trained on vast amounts of unlabeled data, serving as a base that can be adapted (fine-tuned) for a wide range of specific tasks."
---

Understanding **Foundation Model** is essential for any professional looking to apply practical AI in their daily workflows.

By grasping this concept, you move beyond surface-level usage of chat interfaces and begin to understand the underlying mechanics of how these tools operate. This knowledge is critical for troubleshooting, optimizing prompts, and designing automated systems.
