The **Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA)** is the primary financial services regulator in the jurisdiction. It is responsible for the regulation and supervision of the financial services industry, including banking, insurance, investment funds, trust companies, and corporate service providers.

CIMA is also responsible for managing the Cayman Islands' currency (the Cayman Islands Dollar, CI$), managing the country's foreign exchange reserves, and providing assistance to overseas regulatory authorities.
