# Watermark

**Definition:** A recognizable image or pattern in paper or digital files that appears as various shades of lightness/darkness.

## Technical Context

Understanding watermark is essential for proper document conversion and handling. It directly impacts how data is processed, stored, and displayed on screen versus in print.
