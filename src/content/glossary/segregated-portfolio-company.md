A **Segregated Portfolio Company (SPC)** is a type of exempted company that allows for the creation of separate "portfolios" within one legal entity.

The crucial feature of an SPC is statutory ring-fencing: the assets and liabilities of one portfolio are legally segregated from the assets and liabilities of the other portfolios and from the general assets of the company itself. This structure is heavily used in the investment funds and captive insurance sectors.
