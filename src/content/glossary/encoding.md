# Encoding

**Definition:** The process of converting data from one form to another according to a specific standard (e.g., UTF-8 for text).

## Technical Context

Understanding encoding is essential for proper document conversion and handling. It directly impacts how data is processed, stored, and displayed on screen versus in print.
