---
title: Compound Interest Definition
term: Compound Interest
definition: Interest calculated on the initial principal, which also includes all of the accumulated interest from previous periods.
seo:
  page_description: Definition of compound interest in investing.
---
In simple terms, compound interest is "interest on interest."

If you invest \$100 and earn a 10% return, you make \$10 in year one, for a total of \$110. In year two, you earn 10% on the **\$110**, meaning you earn \$11. Your money grows exponentially over long periods. It is the primary engine of wealth building.
