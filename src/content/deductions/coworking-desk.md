## Writing Off Coworking Memberships

If working from home gives you cabin fever or lacks a quiet place for client Zoom calls, renting a hot desk or private office at a local coworking space is **100% deductible** as business rent on Schedule C Line 20b (Rent on Other Business Property).

### No "Exclusive Use" Rule Needed!
Unlike the home office deduction—which requires strict, exclusive business square footage in your home—coworking membership fees are pure commercial rent expenses. You don't need to measure square footage or worry about home utility calculations.

- **Example:** You pay $350/month for a hot desk membership at a local workspace ($4,200/year). You deduct the entire **$4,200** as business rent.
