## Deducting Money Paid to Other Freelancers

When your workload expands and you hire a contract designer, video editor, developer, or virtual assistant, those payments are **100% deductible business expenses** reported on Schedule C Line 11 (Contract Labor).

### The $600 Form 1099-NEC Filing Rule
If you pay an unincorporated independent contractor **$600 or more** in a tax year for services rendered in your trade or business:

1. You **must** request a signed **Form W-9** from them before issuing payment.
2. You **must** file **Form 1099-NEC** with the IRS and send a copy to the contractor by **January 31** following the tax year.

> **Crucial Exception:** Payments made through credit cards or third-party payment processors (like Stripe, Upwork, or Fiverr) are reported by those processors on Form 1099-K. You do NOT issue a 1099-NEC for credit card or payment platform transactions.
