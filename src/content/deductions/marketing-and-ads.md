## Writing Off Every Dollar Spent Acquiring Clients

Advertising and marketing costs are 100% deductible on Schedule C Line 8. The IRS encourages businesses to spend money attracting clients and building brand awareness.

### What Counts as Business Advertising?
- **Digital Ad Campaigns:** Meta (Instagram/Facebook) ads, Google Search ads, Twitter/X ads, Reddit ads, sponsored podcast reads.
- **Web Infrastructure:** Custom domain registrations (e.g. buying `yourbrand.ai` for $60/yr), SSL certificates, web hosting on Vercel/Netlify.
- **Physical Branding:** Business cards, stickers handed out at meetups, branded t-shirts or hoodies used for promotion.

> **Example:** You spend $1,500 on Meta ads testing a new digital product launch. That $1,500 is written off in full, lowering your net profit dollar-for-dollar.
