## Writing Off Your Smartphone & Wi-Fi

You cannot run a freelance business or gig hustle without high-speed Wi-Fi and a smartphone. The IRS recognizes this, allowing independent workers to deduct the business portion of their phone and internet bills.

### How to Calculate Your Business Percentage
Be honest and reasonable. Most full-time freelancers use their phone and home internet between **50% and 80%** for client calls, email, research, Slack, and uploading work assets.

- **Example Calculation:**
  - Annual Cell Phone Bill: $1,200 ($100/mo)
  - Annual Home Internet Bill: $960 ($80/mo)
  - Business Use Percentage: 70%
  - **Total Deduction:** 70% of ($1,200 + $960) = **$1,512 write-off on Schedule C**.

> **Pro Tip:** If you buy a dedicated second phone line (like Google Voice, OpenPhone, or a second SIM) strictly for business, that second line is **100% deductible**.
