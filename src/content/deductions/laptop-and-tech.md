## Writing Off Tech Hardware Without 5-Year Depreciation Horrors

In the old days, accountants made you depreciate a $2,000 computer over 5 painful years ($400/year). Nobody has time for that.

### The De Minimis Safe Harbor ($2,500 Instant Expense Rule)
Under IRS Treasury Regulations § 1.263(a)-1(f), any business equipment item costing **$2,500 or less per invoice** can be written off 100% in the year you buy it.

- **Example:** You buy a $2,100 MacBook Pro on Apple.com. Because $2,100 is less than $2,500, you write off the full $2,100 on Schedule C Line 13 or Line 22 as an instant business expense.

### Section 179 Deduction (For High-End Tech > $2,500)
Bought a $4,500 RED camera setup or $3,500 studio workstation? IRC Section 179 allows you to write off up to $1,220,000 (2024/2025 limit) of equipment in full immediately, bypassing depreciation schedules completely.

> **Factual Rule:** If you use a computer 80% for client work and 20% for gaming, you deduct 80% of its cost. Keep a copy of your receipt saved as a PDF or image in your tax folder.
