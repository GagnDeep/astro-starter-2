## The Hidden Deductions Sucked Out of Your Client Invoices

When a client pays you a $5,000 invoice via Stripe, Stripe automatically deducts $145.30 in processing fees (2.9% + 30¢) and deposits $4,854.70 into your bank account.

If you only report the $4,854.70 deposited, your gross revenue numbers won't match the 1099-K issued by Stripe (which reports the gross $5,000).

### The Correct Accounting Treatment
1. Report **$5,000** as Gross Revenue on Schedule C Line 1.
2. Report **$145.30** as a Payment Processing Fee deduction on Schedule C Line 10 (Commissions and Fees) or Line 27a (Other Expenses).
3. **Net taxable amount:** **$4,854.70**.

Every dollar paid in merchant transaction fees, wire charges, and business banking service fees reduces your taxable earnings.
