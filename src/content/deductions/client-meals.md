## The 50% Client Meal Rule Demystified

Under current IRS Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA) rules, business meals with clients or business collaborators are **50% deductible**.

### The 4 Mandatory IRS Criteria for Meal Write-Offs
1. The meal is an **ordinary and necessary** expense in pursuing your business.
2. The meal is **not lavish or extravagant** under the circumstances.
3. The taxpayer (you) or an employee is **physically present** at the meal.
4. The meal is provided to a **current or potential business customer, client, consultant, or professional contact**, and business is actively discussed.

### The Coffee Shop Myth
Can you write off your daily $7 iced oat latte when working alone at Starbucks?
**No.** The IRS considers solo daily meals personal living expenses under IRC § 262. However, if you buy a coffee or lunch for a client or potential sub-contractor while pitching a project, 50% of that total bill is deductible.

> **Audit Proof Requirement:** Write the name of the client and the topic discussed on the back of the receipt or in your digital expense app (e.g., "Lunch with Sarah - discussed Q3 website redesign pitch").
