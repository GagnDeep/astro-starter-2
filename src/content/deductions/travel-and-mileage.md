## Driving & Flying for Freelance Work

When you travel away from your general tax home for business, the IRS allows you to deduct transportation and lodging expenses.

### Standard Mileage Rate vs. Actual Expenses
For vehicles you drive for business:
- **Standard Mileage Rate:** **67.0 cents per mile** (IRS standard rate). If you drive 5,000 miles to meet clients or deliver work in 2024/2025, your deduction is **$3,350** ($0.67 × 5,000 miles).
- **Actual Expense Method:** Track actual gas, oil changes, tires, lease payments, and insurance, multiplied by business use percentage.

> **Rule:** If you choose standard mileage, you must elect it in the first year you use the car for business.

### Overnight Business Trips
If client work requires staying overnight away from home:
- 100% of flights, train tickets, rental cars, and hotel stays are deductible.
- 50% of your meals while traveling away from home are deductible.
