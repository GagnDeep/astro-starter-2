## Writing Off Upgrades to Your Mind & Skills

Investing in your skills as a creator, developer, designer, or marketer directly increases your earning power. Under IRS rules, education expenses are **100% deductible** on Schedule C as long as they meet specific criteria.

### The IRS Rule: Existing Skills vs. New Trade
To be deductible under Treasury Regulations § 1.162-5:
1. The education must **maintain or improve skills** required in your current business.
2. The education **must not** be required to meet the minimum educational requirements of your current job.
3. The education **must not** qualify you for a completely new trade or business.

### Examples
- **Deductible:** A Webflow developer buying an advanced Three.js WebGL animation course ($400) -> **100% Write-Off**.
- **Deductible:** A freelance copywriter buying a $1,200 conversion copywriting bootcamp -> **100% Write-Off**.
- **Non-Deductible:** A freelance graphic designer taking medical school night classes to become a doctor -> Not deductible on Schedule C.
