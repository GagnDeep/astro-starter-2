## The Human-in-the-Loop Reality

One of the biggest misconceptions about AI in the workplace is that it replaces an entire process perfectly. In reality, practical AI workflows usually follow a "Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL) model.

The AI does the heavy lifting (drafting, extracting, summarizing), but a human must review, edit, and approve the final output.

### Using the Calculator

This calculator accounts for the HITL reality:
-   **Automation Success Rate:** AI isn't perfect. If a task requires complex reasoning, the AI might fail 20% of the time, requiring you to do the task from scratch anyway.
-   **Human Review Time:** Even when the AI succeeds, you still spend time reading and verifying its work.

By factoring in these variables, you get a much more accurate picture of the true time savings of a proposed automation.
