## Understanding the Numbers

The AI ROI Calculator uses a straightforward formula: it compares the **monthly cost of AI software licenses** against the **gross value of the time saved** by your employees.

### How to use this tool

1.  **Number of Employees:** Enter the number of team members who will receive an AI license (e.g., ChatGPT Plus, GitHub Copilot).
2.  **Average Hourly Rate:** Estimate the fully loaded hourly cost of these employees.
3.  **Hours Saved:** Be realistic here. A good baseline for knowledge workers using AI daily is 2-5 hours per week.
4.  **AI Tool Cost:** Enter the per-user monthly subscription cost. (e.g., $20 for ChatGPT Plus, $30 for Copilot Enterprise).

### Why this matters

Intuition isn't enough to justify a business expense. When proposing an AI rollout to management, presenting a clear, mathematical estimate of the Net Monthly Savings transforms the conversation from a "cool tech request" to a strategic business decision.
