## Asset Class Overview

Investing in **Single-Family Rentals (SFR)** presents unique characteristics regarding yield, management intensity, and tax treatment. For Gulf-based investors, understanding these nuances is critical for portfolio alignment.

### Why Invest in Single-Family Rentals (SFR)?

- **Yield Profile:** Historical data shows this asset class provides robust cash-on-cash returns, particularly when leveraged with US non-recourse debt.
- **Inflation Hedge:** Rents and property values in this sector typically outpace core inflation.
- **Management:** We recommend utilizing top-tier third-party property management or investing passively through syndications to minimize day-to-day involvement.

### Tax Considerations for UAE Residents

Income derived from Single-Family Rentals (SFR) is subject to US federal income tax. However, through depreciation (including bonus depreciation where applicable) and proper expense deductions, the taxable income is often significantly lower than the actual cash flow.

It is imperative that this asset is held within an LLC or LP structure to mitigate US Estate Tax exposure and simplify tax filings.

### Geographic Focus

For Single-Family Rentals (SFR), we currently favor the Sun Belt markets (e.g., Texas, Florida, Arizona) due to strong population growth, business-friendly environments, and lack of state income taxes in key states.
