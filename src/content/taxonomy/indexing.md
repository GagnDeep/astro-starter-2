---
title: Indexing
description: A passive investment strategy that attempts to generate similar returns as a broad market index.
seo:
  page_description: The indexing strategy explained.
---
Indexing involves buying a representative sample of the stock market, rather than trying to pick individual winning stocks.

Because over 90% of professional active managers fail to beat the market over a 15-year period (according to SPIVA data), indexing is widely considered the most reliable strategy for retail investors.
