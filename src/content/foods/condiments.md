# Condiments

Properly categorizing and tracking the foods you eat is essential for accurate macro counting. This guide breaks down the nutritional profile and common tracking pitfalls for **Condiments**.

## Nutritional Profile

Foods in this category generally have a distinct macronutrient profile that makes them suitable for specific dietary goals.

*All caloric values referenced should be verified against the USDA FoodData Central database.*

### Tracking Considerations

When logging these foods, pay close attention to:

1. **State:** Is it raw, cooked, dried, or reconstituted?
2. **Additives:** Are there hidden oils, sugars, or sauces?
3. **Volume vs. Weight:** Always weigh in grams using a digital food scale rather than relying on volume measurements like cups.
