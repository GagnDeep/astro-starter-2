# What is MOBI?

A legacy ebook format originally used by Amazon Kindle devices before the shift to AZW3 and KF8 formats.

## Technical Architecture

The MOBI format was engineered to solve specific data structuring and delivery problems in computing. Unlike plain text, complex formats require parser software to interpret the byte stream or XML structure.

## Primary Use Cases

*   **Delivery vs Editing:** Formats are generally split between final delivery (like PDF) and active editing (like DOCX).
*   **Web vs Print:** Image formats are divided into lossy (web-optimized) and lossless (archival or print-ready).
