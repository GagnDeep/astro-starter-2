# Most Private Phone Brief

## The Domain and Business
**Domain:** mostprivatephone.com
**Business Model:** We provide de-Googled phone guidance. We monetize through a paid setup guide and a capped configured-hardware line. Affiliate commissions (1-4%) are secondary and not relied upon to carry the site.

## The Buyer
**Target Audience:** Privacy-conscious buyers considering GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, or a pre-configured de-Googled device.

## Positioning Constraints
*   **Factual Claims:** "Most private" is a factual claim that must be substantiated.
*   **Evaluation Criteria:** We must publish the evaluation criteria and the evidence behind every ranking.
*   **Tested Security Properties:** Never state a security property we have not tested.
*   **Disclosures:** Every commission link carries `rel="sponsored nofollow"` with a plain-language disclosure above the first one and above the fold.
*   **Primary Sources:** Editorial citations to primary sources are followed, never nofollowed.
