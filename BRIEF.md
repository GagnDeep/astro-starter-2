# NRI Direct UK - Project Brief

**Domain**: nridirect.uk
**Business**: OCI card, Indian passport renewal and visa document assistance.
**Target Buyer**: Indian-origin UK residents navigating OCI applications, passport renewals and visa paperwork.
**Business Model (Money)**: Paid document-check and application-assistance services.

## Positioning Constraints (CRITICAL)
- The site **MUST NEVER** look like an official government channel.
- No page may imply affiliation with VFS Global, the Indian government, a consulate, or any high commission.
- Every page **needs a clear independence disclaimer**.
