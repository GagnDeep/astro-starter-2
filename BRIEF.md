# Institute of RIA - Project Brief

## The Business & Domain
- **Site:** instituteofria.com
- **Type:** B2B media for registered investment adviser (RIA) firms.
- **Coverage:** Firm formation, compliance, technology stacks, and succession.

## Target Buyer
- RIA principals, COOs, and compliance leads.

## Positioning Constraints
- **Never target consumers** looking for an adviser (SmartAsset owns consumer-facing head terms).
- This is **not** an accreditation body or a professional institute. Disclaimers must explicitly state this.
- Financial promotion regulations apply: every page needs a "not investment advice" disclaimer and published methodology for untested claims.
- Never invent statistics, credentials, testimonials, awards, or customer names. Date every verifiable claim.
- **Revenue Model:** Vendor referrals and sponsorship from RIA-serving vendors. Commission links require `rel="sponsored nofollow"` and a plain-language disclosure above the fold/first link.
