# Institute of Humanoids

**Domain:** instituteofhumanoids.com
**Business:** A humanoid-robotics supply-chain cross-reference and hardware job board.
**Target Buyer/Audience:** Component suppliers, investors, and hardware engineers in humanoid robotics.
**Monetization:** Site sponsorship and paid job postings for hardware engineering roles. We do not accept payment for inclusion in the supply-chain database.

## Positioning Constraints
- This is deliberately not another humanoid news feed (humanoid.press already owns that lane).
- Focus is strictly on structured supplier data (which suppliers provide actuators, harmonic drives, batteries, sensors, compute to which humanoid company) and specialist hardware jobs.
- Never invent a credential, award, or profile URL — omit the field instead.
- Content must not have fabricated statistics, case studies, or customer names. All verifiable claims must be dated.
