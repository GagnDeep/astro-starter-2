# Handpano - Project Brief

## Domain & Business
Handpano.com is a buyer's resource for first-time handpan buyers. Handpans are relatively expensive instruments (often $1,500 - $3,000+), and there are many makers, scales, and materials to choose from. The business generates revenue by selling its own buyer's decision guide (roughly 29 USD) and direct lessons, and by offering marketplace referrals.

## Target Buyer
First-time handpan buyers who need help choosing a scale, finding a reputable maker, and setting a realistic budget. They are likely overwhelmed by the options and wary of cheap knock-offs.

## Revenue & Positioning Constraints
- **NO affiliate links.** Handpan makers do not run affiliate programs. Every monetized path must be our own product (the buyer's decision guide, lessons) or a real referral relationship. Never fabricate an affiliate link or commission setup.
- The content must be highly authoritative. We buy or borrow every handpan we test; we do not accept free instruments for coverage.
- Focus on practical decision-making: scale selection, maker reputation, and budgeting.
