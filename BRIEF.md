# BRIEF

## Domain
instituteofsustainability.com

## Business
A compliance workflow service focused explicitly on California SB 253 and SB 261 climate disclosure statutes. It provides essential tools and readiness guides.

## Buyer
Sustainability and finance leads at companies in scope of California SB 253 and SB 261.

## Monetization
Corporate sponsorships and a paid readiness toolkit.

## Positioning Constraints
- Scope narrowly: strictly focus on California SB 253 and SB 261.
- Date everything: this regulation shifts fast. Every page must carry a last-verified date and cite the statute or CARB guidance directly.
- Identity: Not a professional institute or accrediting body. The 'last-verified' date reflects the latest regulatory review.
