# Institute of Underwriting Brief

## The Domain & Business
**Institute of Underwriting** (instituteofunderwriting.com) is a sponsorship-funded resource and newsletter designed for working insurance underwriters.
It functions primarily as a trade publication offering technical briefings and insights.

## The Buyer / Target Audience
The target audience consists of **commercial and specialty underwriters**.

## How It Makes Money
The business is fully funded by **insurtech and industry sponsorships**.

## Positioning Constraints
- **Crucial Limitation:** The Institute of Underwriting must NOT be mistaken for a chartered professional body.
- The public brand should read exclusively as a trade publication.
- An explicit **'not a professional body, not an accrediting institute'** statement must appear in the footer and on the about page.
