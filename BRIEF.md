# Aerospace UAE: Project Brief

## Domain & Business
**aerospaceuae.com** is a subscription directory and hiring board for the UAE's Tier 2/3 aerospace and MRO (Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul) supply chain.

## The Buyer
The target audience consists of:
1. Primes and buyers sourcing UAE subcontractors and ICV-registered vendors.
2. Recruiters posting specialist engineering roles.

## Revenue Model
The site generates revenue exclusively through:
1. Paid supplier listings in the directory.
2. Recruiter job postings on the hiring board.
3. A subcontractor-matching newsletter.

## Positioning Constraints
- **Strict Independence & Scope:** The site must operate deliberately outside UAE media-licensing law.
- **No Journalism:** No news reporting, and no editorial commentary whatsoever.
- **Content Limitations:** The site is strictly a directory, jobs board, and reference data repository.
- **No Fabrication:** We never invent a statistic, credential, award, sameAs URL, testimonial, review count, case study, or customer name. We omit fields rather than fabricate.
