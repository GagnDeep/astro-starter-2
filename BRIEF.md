# BRIEF

**Domain**: instituteofretirement.com
**Business**: A disclosed-methodology comparison of countries to retire abroad in, scored on cost of living, visa route, healthcare, and tax treatment. Not a professional institute.
**Buyer**: Near-retirees comparing destination countries to retire abroad.
**Monetization**: Modest affiliate commissions and lead generation fees from selected partners.
**Positioning Constraints**: NerdWallet, Fidelity, and Bankrate own generic retirement terms. The only winnable term is retire-abroad country comparison. The scoring methodology must be published in full and honestly. Every claim must be verified against official sources. No fabricated statistics, credentials, or testimonials. No "click here" or "read more" filler text.
