# Institute of Retirement Brief

**Domain**: instituteofretirement.com
**Business**: A disclosed-methodology comparison of countries for near-retirees to retire abroad, scored honestly on cost of living, visa routes, healthcare, and tax treatment. Not a professional institute.
**Buyer**: Near-retirees comparing potential destination countries for retirement abroad.
**Monetization**: Modest affiliate commissions and lead generation. This does not influence the scoring methodology, which must be fully and honestly published.
**Positioning Constraint**: Generic retirement terms are dominated by large entities like NerdWallet, Fidelity, and Bankrate. The winnable niche is strictly the "retire-abroad country comparison".
