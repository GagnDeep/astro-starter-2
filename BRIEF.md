# Institute of Restaurants - Project Brief

## The Business & Domain

**Domain:** instituteofrestaurants.com
**Business:** A restaurant POS and technology comparison site.
**Target Buyer:** First-time independent restaurant owners choosing POS, payroll, online ordering, and reservation systems within the 90-day pre-opening window.

## Monetization

The site earns revenue through vendor referral bounties (commissions) when visitors choose a vendor via outbound links:

- Toast: ~$500-1500 USD per referred restaurant
- TouchBistro: up to ~$1000 USD
- Square: ~$200 USD

## Positioning Constraints

- **Accreditation Disclaimer:** The name "Institute of Restaurants" implies an accreditation body, which it is NOT. This must be stated plainly upfront.
- **Methodology:** A dedicated methodology page is required to explain how evaluations are conducted.
- **FTC Disclosures:** An honest, plain-language FTC affiliate disclosure must appear beside _every_ commission-earning link (above the first link/fold).
- **Integrity:** We do not fabricate credentials, statistics, awards, review counts, case studies, or profile URLs. Omit the field if real data is unavailable.
- **Outbound Links:** Every commission-earning link must carry `rel="sponsored nofollow"`. Editorial citations to primary sources are followed, never nofollowed.
