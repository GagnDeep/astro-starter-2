# DogSnacker Brief

**Domain & Identity:**
dogsnacker.com - Vet-reviewed homemade dog-treat recipes organised by food allergen. The brand is invented, so growth relies entirely on targeting the allergen long tail (e.g., "chicken-free dog treat recipes").

**Buyer:**
Dog owners who are actively managing a food-sensitivity problem or elimination diet for their pet.

**Monetization:**
Affiliate links (for recommended ingredients and kitchen tools) and a digital recipe product. This is *not* a direct-to-consumer treat brand.

**Positioning Constraints:**
Pet health is YMYL-adjacent (Your Money or Your Life). Every recipe MUST carry:
1. Allergen tagging.
2. A vet-reviewed byline placeholder.
3. A clear 'talk to your vet' note.
