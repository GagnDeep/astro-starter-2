# Project Brief: Institute of Neurofeedback

## Domain
`instituteofneurofeedback.com`

## The Business
An authoritative, evidence-based portal providing an honest, citation-backed review of neurofeedback research. The business models transparency and rigorous academic review, separating established clinical findings from unsubstantiated claims.

## The Buyer
1. **Patients and Parents:** Researching the validity, efficacy, and application of neurofeedback as a potential intervention.
2. **Practising Clinicians:** Seeking B2B content, clinical resources, and making purchasing decisions (e.g., equipment budgets).

## Revenue Model
The platform generates revenue through B2B content and comprehensive equipment comparisons. (Note: BCIA provides free practitioner directory listings, so this site intentionally does NOT build a directory).

## Positioning Constraints (Strict YMYL)
- **Citations Required:** Every single clinical claim must be accompanied by a citation.
- **Credentialed Byline:** Every piece of clinical content needs a credentialed-reviewer byline placeholder.
- **No Promised Outcomes:** Absolutely no page on this site may promise a treatment outcome.
- **Honesty & Integrity:** The site strictly reviews what neurofeedback research does and does NOT support, never inventing statistics, credentials, or awards.
