# Business Brief: handpano.com

## Domain and Business
Handpano is a buyer's resource for first-time handpan buyers. It helps users navigate the complex decision process of choosing a handpan by focusing on three main areas: scale, maker, and budget.

## The Buyer
Target audience is first-time buyers who are looking to purchase a handpan but are overwhelmed by the choices of scales, makers, and prices.

## How it Makes Money
The site monetizes through:
- A $29 USD buyer's decision guide.
- Handpan lessons.
- Real marketplace referrals.

## Positioning Constraints
- Handpan makers do not run affiliate programs, so affiliate revenue does not exist.
- Every monetised path must be the site's own product or a real referral relationship.
- Never use a fabricated affiliate link.
- Never invent a credential, award, or profile URL. Omit the field instead if no real data is available.
- Editorial independence is strictly maintained — no free instruments accepted in exchange for reviews, and corrections are prominently logged.
