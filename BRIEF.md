# Project Brief: Fish Scanner

## The Domain
fishscanner.com

## The Business & Target Buyer
Fish Scanner is an aquarium stocking and compatibility calculator focused explicitly on the marine and reef gap. Incumbent tools predominantly target freshwater tanks. The target buyer/user is a reef and marine aquarium hobbyist who is planning a new tank setup or expanding an existing one.

## Monetization
The primary income stream is aquarium-supply affiliate commissions.

## Positioning Constraints
- Do NOT include consumer fish identification.
- Do NOT include seafood-fraud detection.
- Do NOT include aquaculture computer vision.
- Build ONLY a marine stocking and compatibility tool.
- MUST state the data source behind every single compatibility claim (e.g., verifying against primary aquaculture and scientific literature, not anecdotal forum posts).
