# Institute of Contractors Brief

- **Domain**: instituteofcontractors.com
- **Business**: A state-by-state reference on contractor licensing requirements, exam-prep comparison, and bonding and insurance.
- **Buyer**: Tradespeople getting licensed (not homeowners hiring contractors).
- **Monetization**: Exam-prep and bonding/insurance referrals.
- **Positioning Constraint**: This site has a genuine legal duty not to look like a licensing authority. An explicit 'we are not a licensing board and cannot issue or verify a licence' statement must appear on every state page. This is not a homeowner lead-generation play.
