# Institute of RIA

**Domain:** instituteofria.com

**Business:** B2B media and insights for registered investment adviser (RIA) firms.

**Target Buyer:** RIA principals, Chief Operating Officers (COOs), and compliance leads. This site does *not* target consumers looking for an adviser.

**Revenue Model:** The business makes money through vendor referrals and sponsorships from vendors that serve RIAs.

**Positioning Constraints:**
- **No Consumers:** Do not target consumers looking for financial advice.
- **Not an Accreditation Body:** This is *not* an accreditation body or a professional institute. Disclaim this clearly.
- **No Financial Advice:** This site does not provide financial or investment advice.
- **Competitor Constraints:** SmartAsset owns consumer-facing adviser-matching head terms. Do not try to compete for consumer matching.
- **Claims:** Never invent statistics, credentials, awards, sameAs URLs, testimonials, review counts, case studies, or customer names. Date all verifiable claims.
