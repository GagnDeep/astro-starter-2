# Institute of Restaurants

**Domain:** instituteofrestaurants.com

**The Business:** A restaurant POS and technology comparison site aimed at the 90-day pre-opening window.

**The Buyer:** First-time independent restaurant owners choosing POS, payroll, online ordering, and reservation systems.

**How it Makes Money:** Vendor referral bounties (Toast roughly 500-1500 USD per referred restaurant, TouchBistro up to 1000, Square around 200).

**Positioning Constraints:**
- The name implies an accreditation body, but it is not. This must be stated plainly up front.
- A methodology page must be published.
- An honest FTC affiliate disclosure must be placed beside every commission-earning link.
