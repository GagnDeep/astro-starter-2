# Institute of Retirement Brief

**Domain:** instituteofretirement.com

**Business:** A disclosed-methodology comparison of countries to retire abroad in. We score countries based on cost of living, visa routes, healthcare, and tax treatment.

**Buyer:** Near-retirees comparing destination countries for retiring abroad.

**How it makes money:** Modest affiliate commissions and lead generation.

**Positioning Constraints:**
- Generic retirement terms are dominated by NerdWallet, Fidelity, and Bankrate, so only the "retire-abroad country comparison" niche is winnable.
- The scoring methodology must be published in full and honestly.
- We are *not* a professional institute, so we do not pretend to have credentials, awards, or false authority. We present data honestly.
