# Project Brief: They Are Tracking

- **Domain**: theyaretracking.com
- **Business**: Providing guidance on data-broker removal and comparing privacy tools.
- **Buyer/Audience**: Individuals trying to get their personal information off data-broker sites.
- **Monetization**: Affiliate commissions on paid removal services and privacy tools.
- **Positioning Constraints**:
  - Prominently disclose affiliate relationships due to the credibility tension in earning commission from privacy products.
  - Publish the comparison methodology transparently.
  - Provide a free, manual opt-out route for *every* data broker alongside the paid, automated option on the same page.
