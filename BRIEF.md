# NRI Direct UK - Brief

**Domain:** nridirect.uk
**Business:** Paid document-check and application-assistance services for OCI card, Indian passport renewal, and visa document assistance.
**Target Buyer:** Indian-origin UK residents navigating OCI applications, passport renewals, and visa paperwork.
**Monetisation Strategy:** Charging a fee for professional document checking and application assistance services.

## Positioning Constraints
- **CRITICAL CONSTRAINT:** The site must never look like an official government channel.
- No page may imply affiliation with VFS Global, the Indian government, any consulate, or any high commission.
- Every page needs a clear, prominent independence disclaimer.
- The tone should be professional, accurate, and helpful, but distinctly independent from official bodies.
