# Institute of Neurofeedback

## Domain
instituteofneurofeedback.com

## The Business
An honest, citation-backed review platform of what neurofeedback research does and does not support. We provide B2B content for clinicians who have equipment budget, as well as educational content for patients and parents.

## The Buyer
Patients and parents researching neurofeedback, and practicing clinicians looking for equipment comparisons.

## How it Makes Money
B2B content and equipment comparison (affiliate/commission relationships for clinical equipment).

## Positioning Constraints (Strict YMYL)
- Every clinical claim needs a citation and a credentialed-reviewer byline placeholder.
- No page may promise a treatment outcome.
- No directory listings (BCIA gives practitioner directory listings away for free).
