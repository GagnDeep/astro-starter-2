# Institute of DNA: Brief

## Domain & Business
**Domain:** instituteofdna.com
**Business:** A privacy-led DNA-test comparison site. The site serves as an authoritative guide on what happens to customer genomes when using direct-to-consumer DNA tests, pivoting on the 23andMe bankruptcy case as a wedge.

## Target Buyer
People choosing a consumer DNA test who care fundamentally about where their genetic data ends up and what companies are doing with their biological information.

## Monetization
**Money:** Affiliate commissions. Links to DNA testing companies are sponsored and nofollow, with a clear disclaimer.

## Positioning Constraints
- **Primary Angle:** Must win on the data-privacy angle alone.
- **Strict Avoidance:** Never compete on generic 'best DNA test' terms.
- **Content Standard:** Health content is YMYL (Your Money or Your Life). All claims must cite sources and never provide medical advice.
