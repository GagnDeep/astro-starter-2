# Battery Digital Passport

- **Domain**: batterydigitalpassport.com
- **Business**: The authoritative free information layer on the EU battery passport mandatory from 18 February 2027.
- **Buyer**: Compliance and product managers at battery, EV, and manufacturing companies.
- **Monetization**: Vendor sponsorship plus a paid readiness toolkit. This site does NOT sell compliance software.
- **Positioning Constraints**: Regulatory accuracy is the entire product. All dates, obligations, and data fields must cite EUR-Lex and official sources. Every page must carry a last-verified date, and anything unverified must be clearly labeled as such.
