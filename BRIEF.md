# Institute of Catering - Project Brief

## The Business & Domain
- **Domain:** instituteofcatering.com
- **Purpose:** A practical operator resource for people who RUN catering businesses.
- **Focus Areas:** Licensing, pricing, contracts, software, and certifications.

## The Buyer
- **Target Audience:** Catering business owners and operators.
- **Strict Exclusion:** Not for students.

## Positioning Constraints
- Deliberately **not** a cooking school.
- Never target course, diploma, or qualification intent (accredited colleges own the education SERP).
- State clearly that the site does not teach or accredit.

## Monetization
- **Primary Revenue:** Catering software referrals and certification affiliate programs.
