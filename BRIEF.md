# Fish Scanner - Project Brief

**Domain:** fishscanner.com
**Business:** An aquarium stocking and compatibility calculator focused exclusively on the marine and reef gap.
**Buyer:** Reef and marine aquarium hobbyists planning or expanding a tank.
**Monetization:** Aquarium-supply affiliate links.

**Positioning Constraints:**
- Consumer fish identification, seafood-fraud detection, and aquaculture computer vision are **ruled out**.
- Build the marine stocking and compatibility tool specifically.
- State the data source behind every compatibility claim.

**Testing and Revenue Model:**
- We combine aquarium husbandry literature with experienced marine aquarist reports to source our compatibility logic. Every claim is cited to its primary data source.
- Revenue comes from affiliate commissions on aquarium supplies purchased through our links. These are always marked clearly with 'sponsored'.
- If we get stocking logic wrong, we fix it and update our algorithms based on reports and new citations.
