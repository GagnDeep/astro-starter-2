# Institute of Underwriting: Brand and Positioning Brief

## The Domain
`instituteofunderwriting.com`

## The Business
A sponsorship-funded resource and newsletter designed for working insurance underwriters. It serves as a practical reference hub, providing technical insight rather than generic advice.

## The Buyer (Audience)
Target audience: commercial and specialty underwriters.

## Monetization
The platform is funded entirely through insurtech sponsorships. Sponsor content is clearly marked to ensure transparency and separation between editorial resources and sponsored material.

## Positioning Constraints
- **Crucial Constraint:** The brand must not be mistaken for a chartered professional body.
- The public brand reads as a trade publication.
- An explicit disclaimer: "not a professional body, not an accrediting institute" must be present in the footer and on the about page.
- Never invent a credential, award, or profile URL. Omit the field instead.
