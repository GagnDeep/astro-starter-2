# Institute of Sustainability Brief

## Domain
instituteofsustainability.com

## The Business
A compliance workflow site strictly focused on California SB 253 and SB 261 climate disclosure. It is **not** a professional institute or accrediting body.

## The Buyer
Sustainability and finance leads at companies in scope of SB 253 and SB 261.

## Monetization
- Site sponsorship
- Paid readiness toolkit

## Positioning Constraints
- Scope narrowly: strictly compliance workflow for California SB 253 and SB 261.
- Date everything: this regulation shifts fast enough to strand content, so every page must carry a last-verified date.
- Cite the statute or CARB (California Air Resources Board) guidance directly.
- Never invent a credential, award, or profile URL. Omit rather than fabricate.
