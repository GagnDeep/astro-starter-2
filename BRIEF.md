# Institute of Sustainability

## Domain
instituteofsustainability.com

## Business Model
This site provides resources for one specific compliance workflow: California SB 253 and SB 261 climate disclosure.
The site generates revenue via sponsorships and a paid "Readiness Toolkit".
It is NOT a professional institute or accrediting body.

## Target Buyer
Sustainability and finance leads at companies within the scope of the California SB 253 and SB 261 statutes.

## Positioning Constraints
- Content must be scoped narrowly.
- This regulation shifts rapidly, so every page must carry a last-verified date.
- Content must directly cite the statute or CARB guidance.
- Never invent a credential, award, or profile URL.
