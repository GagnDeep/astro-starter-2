# Most Private Phone Brief

## The Domain and Business
**Domain:** mostprivatephone.com
**Business:** De-Googled phone guidance, a paid setup guide, and a capped line of pre-configured hardware.

## The Buyer
**Target Buyer:** Privacy-conscious individuals considering GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, or a pre-configured de-Googled device.

## Monetization
**Money:** Revenue is driven by the paid setup guide and configured hardware. Affiliate commissions (1-4%) are secondary and cannot carry the site.

## Positioning Constraints
- **Factual Claims Only:** 'Most private' is treated as a factual claim that must be substantiated.
- **Evidence-Based:** Must publish the evaluation criteria and the evidence behind every ranking.
- **No Unverified Claims:** Never state a security property that has not been tested.
