# Battery Digital Passport

- **Domain**: batterydigitalpassport.com
- **Business**: The authoritative free information layer on the EU battery passport that becomes mandatory on 18 February 2027.
- **Buyer**: Compliance and product managers at battery, EV and manufacturing companies.
- **Monetization**: Vendor sponsorship plus a paid readiness toolkit. The site does not sell compliance software.
- **Positioning constraint**: Regulatory accuracy is the entire product. Cite EUR-Lex and official sources for every date, obligation, and data field. Carry a last-verified date on every page, and label anything not yet verified strictly as unverified.
