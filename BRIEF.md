# AI and Investing — Brief

**Domain:** aiandinvesting.com

**Business:** Reproducible, published back-testing of AI stock-picking tools.

**Target Buyer:** Retail investors evaluating AI investing products.

**Monetization:** Affiliate commissions on the tools reviewed.

**Positioning Constraints:**
- Financial-promotion regulation applies.
- Every page needs a "not investment advice" disclaimer.
- Every tool review must have a published and repeatable methodology.
- No performance claim can be made without the reproducible test behind it.
- Head terms are locked out for 12-24 months; target the long tail only.
