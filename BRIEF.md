# Institute of Humanoids

**Domain:** instituteofhumanoids.com

**Business:** A humanoid-robotics supply-chain cross-reference recording which suppliers provide components (actuators, harmonic drives, batteries, sensors, and compute) to which humanoid company. It also includes a specialist hardware job board.

**Target Buyer/Audience:** Component suppliers, investors, and hardware engineers in humanoid robotics.

**Monetization:** Corporate sponsorships and paid job postings.

**Positioning Constraints:**
- This is strictly a supply-chain cross-reference and job board, NOT another humanoid news feed (humanoid.press already owns that space).
- Contains structured supplier data and jobs only.
- No aggregated press releases or rumours; data is backed by verifiable sources, public disclosures, and teardowns.
