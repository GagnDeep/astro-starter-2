# Battery Digital Passport

## Domain & Positioning
- **Domain:** batterydigitalpassport.com
- **Product:** The authoritative free information layer on the EU battery passport, which becomes mandatory on 18 February 2027.
- **Positioning constraint:** Regulatory accuracy is the *entire* product.
  - Every date, obligation, and data field must be cited from EUR-Lex and official EU sources.
  - Every page must carry a last-verified date.
  - Anything not yet verified must be explicitly labelled as unverified.

## Target Buyer
- Compliance and product managers at battery, EV, and manufacturing companies.

## Business Model
- Vendor sponsorship.
- A paid readiness toolkit.
- **Constraint:** The site *does not* sell compliance software.
