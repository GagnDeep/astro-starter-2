# BRIEF

**Domain:** instituteofsustainability.com
**Business:** A compliance workflow and readiness resource focusing strictly on California SB 253 and SB 261 climate disclosure.
**Target Buyer:** Sustainability and finance leads at companies scoped by those statutes.
**Monetization:** Site sponsorship and a paid readiness toolkit.

**Positioning Constraints:**
- Scope narrowly: this site only covers SB 253 and SB 261. No broad ESG, no other states, no EU CSRD.
- Date everything: this regulation shifts fast enough to strand content, so every page must carry a last-verified date.
- Primary sources: every claim must cite the statute or CARB guidance directly.
- Identity: Not a professional institute or accrediting body. Do not invent credentials, awards, or profile URLs. Omit instead.
