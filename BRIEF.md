# aiandinvesting.com Brief

**Domain:** aiandinvesting.com
**Business:** Published, reproducible back-testing of AI stock-picking tools.
**Buyer:** Retail investors evaluating AI investing products.
**Revenue Model:** Affiliate commissions on the tools reviewed.

**Positioning Constraints:**
- Financial-promotion regulation applies.
- Every page needs a "not investment advice" disclaimer.
- A published and repeatable methodology must be present.
- No performance claim can be made without the reproducible test behind it.
- Target the long tail only (head terms are locked out for 12-24 months).
