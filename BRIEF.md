# Business Brief: Washington DC Review

## Domain and Business
- **Domain:** washingtondcreview.com
- **Business:** A federal-contracting resource and reference hub for small businesses.

## Buyer/Audience
- **Target Buyer:** Small business owners trying to win their first federal contract.

## Revenue Model
- **Money:** Lead generation and referral bounties/commissions to specialized GovCon service providers (e.g., consultants who help with SAM.gov, GSA Schedules, etc.).

## Positioning Constraints
- **Category:** Federal-contracting reference hub (not a news outlet, despite the masthead-style name).
- **Affiliation:** MUST explicitly state it is not a government agency and NEVER imply any government affiliation or endorsement.
- **Tone:** Technical, authoritative, yet accessible reference material.
