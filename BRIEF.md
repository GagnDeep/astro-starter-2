# Washington DC Review: Site Brief

## Domain
washingtondcreview.com

## The Business
A federal-contracting resource hub focusing on SAM.gov registration, set-aside certifications (8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, HUBZone), and GSA Schedule entry.

## The Buyer
Small business owners attempting to win their first federal contract. They need actionable, clear, and accurate guidelines without overwhelming bureaucratic jargon.

## Revenue Model
Lead generation and referrals to GovCon service providers. Referral links must be marked transparently.

## Positioning Constraints
- Must be positioned as a **reference hub**, not a news outlet (due to the masthead-style name).
- **Never imply any government affiliation or endorsement.**
