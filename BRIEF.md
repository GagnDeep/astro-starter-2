# Aerospace UAE - Brief

## Domain and Business
**aerospaceuae.com** is a subscription directory and hiring board tailored exclusively for the UAE's Tier 2/3 aerospace and MRO (Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul) supply chain.

## The Buyer
The target audience consists of primes and buyers who are sourcing UAE subcontractors and ICV-registered (In-Country Value) vendors, as well as recruiters posting specialist engineering roles in the region.

## Revenue Model
The business generates revenue through:
- Paid supplier directory listings.
- Paid recruiter job postings.
- A premium, subcontractor-matching newsletter.

## Positioning Constraints
- **Strictly Data and Directory:** The site functions as a directory, jobs board, and reference data repository.
- **No Journalism/Editorial Scope:** The site must remain deliberately outside UAE media-licensing law. It will feature no journalism, no news reporting, and no editorial commentary.
