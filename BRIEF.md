# DogSnacker Brief

**Domain**: dogsnacker.com
**Business**: Vet-reviewed homemade dog-treat recipes organised by food allergen.
**Buyer**: Dog owners managing a sensitivity or elimination diet.
**Monetization**: Affiliate commissions and digital recipe products. This is not a direct-to-consumer treat brand.
**Positioning Constraints**: Pet health is YMYL-adjacent. Every recipe carries allergen tagging, a vet-reviewed byline placeholder, and a clear 'talk to your vet' note. The brand is invented and carries no keyword, so it leans entirely on the allergen long tail.
