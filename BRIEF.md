# NRI Direct UK — Project Brief

**Domain**: nridirect.uk
**Business**: Paid document-check and application-assistance services for OCI cards, Indian passport renewals, and Indian visas.
**Target Buyer**: Indian-origin UK residents navigating complex bureaucratic application paperwork and document requirements.
**Monetisation**: Paid service offerings for checking documents and guiding applicants through procedures.

## Critical Positioning Constraints
- **Absolute Independence**: The site must never look like or imply being an official government channel.
- **No Affiliations**: No page may imply affiliation with VFS Global, the Indian Government, any consulate, or any high commission.
- **Disclaimer**: Every page needs a clear, unambiguous independence disclaimer stating that we are an independent assistance service, not a government body.
