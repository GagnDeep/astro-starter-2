# Institute of Sustainability

**Domain:** instituteofsustainability.com
**Target Buyer:** Sustainability and finance leads at companies in scope of California SB 253 and SB 261 statutes.
**Core Offering / Workflow:** Compliance workflow for California SB 253 and SB 261 climate disclosure.
**Monetization:** Sponsorships and a paid readiness toolkit.

**Positioning Constraints:**

- Scope is narrow: solely focused on the California SB 253 and SB 261 climate disclosure workflow.
- Date everything: the regulation shifts rapidly; content must carry a last-verified date.
- Cite the statute or CARB guidance directly.
- **NOT** a professional institute or accrediting body.
