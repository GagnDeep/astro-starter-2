# Institute of Catering

**Domain:** `instituteofcatering.com`

**Business:** A practical operator resource for people who run catering businesses.

**Buyer/Audience:** Catering business owners. This is **not** a cooking school, and the audience is not students.

**Positioning Constraints:**

- Do **not** target course, diploma, or qualification intent.
- Clearly state that the site does not teach or accredit.
- Never invent a credential, award, or profile URL. Omit the field instead.
- Every claim that can be verified must be dated.

**Monetization:**

- Catering software referrals (affiliate).
- Certification affiliate.
- Any commission link must carry `rel="sponsored nofollow"`, with a plain-language disclosure above the first one and above the fold.
