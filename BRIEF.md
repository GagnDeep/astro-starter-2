# BRIEF

## Domain
advisorcto.com

## Business
A fractional CTO practice serving Registered Investment Advisor (RIA) and wealth-management firms.

## Buyer
RIA principals who run technology stacks like Orion, Redtail, Wealthbox, or eMoney, but lack a dedicated leader accountable for security, integration, or vendor risk.

## Monetization
Monthly retainers and fixed-scope consulting engagements. The firm remains strictly independent, accepting no referral fees, kickbacks, or affiliate commissions.

## Positioning Constraints
- This is a founder-credibility site.
- The page set will include: positioning, service definition, methodology, proof of track record, and a booking flow.
- All credentials, clients, logos, and case studies must be real. Do not fabricate anything—if information is unavailable, use clearly marked placeholders.
