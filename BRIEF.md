# nridirect.uk Brief

**Domain**: nridirect.uk

**Business**: Independent document assistance and application review for OCI cards, Indian passport renewals, and visa paperwork for non-resident Indians (NRIs) living in the UK.

**Buyer**: Indian-origin UK residents navigating OCI applications, passport renewals, and visa paperwork. They are likely confused or overwhelmed by official documentation requirements and seek clarity and assurance.

**Monetization**: Paid document-check and application-assistance services.

**Positioning Constraints**:
- The site must **never** look like an official government channel.
- No page may imply affiliation with VFS Global, the Indian government, a consulate, or any high commission.
- Every page needs a clear independence disclaimer.
- No payment is accepted to "expedite" official government processing.
