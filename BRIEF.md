# Fish Scanner - Project Brief

**Domain**: fishscanner.com
**Business**: Aquarium stocking and compatibility calculator focused on marine and reef tanks.
**Target Buyer**: Reef and marine aquarium hobbyists planning or expanding a tank.
**Monetization**: Aquarium-supply affiliate commissions.

**Positioning Constraints**:
- Consumer fish identification, seafood-fraud detection, and aquaculture computer vision are strictly ruled out.
- The tool is exclusively a marine stocking and compatibility calculator.
- The data source behind every compatibility claim must be stated clearly.
