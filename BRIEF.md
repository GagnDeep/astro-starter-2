# Aerospace UAE - Brief

**Domain:** aerospaceuae.com
**Business:** A subscription directory and hiring board for the UAE's Tier 2/3 aerospace and MRO (Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul) supply chain.
**Target Buyer:**
- Primes and buyers sourcing UAE subcontractors and ICV-registered vendors.
- Recruiters posting specialist engineering roles.
**Revenue Model:**
- Paid supplier listings.
- Recruiter job postings.
- A subcontractor-matching newsletter.

**Positioning Constraint (CRITICAL):**
The site must sit deliberately outside UAE media-licensing law. There must be no journalism, no news reporting, and no editorial commentary. The site is strictly limited to directory listings, job postings, and reference data.
