# AI Wire Harness Brief

## Domain
`aiwireharness.com`

## The Business
A pay-per-lead RFQ matching platform connecting buyers sourcing custom, EV, and aerospace wire harnesses with capable manufacturers.

## The Buyer
Engineers and procurement staff looking to source custom harnesses.

## How it Makes Money
We charge harness manufacturers a pay-per-lead fee when they receive RFQs from qualified buyers.

## Positioning Constraint
The phrase "AI wire harness" has near-zero search volume. Therefore, we must build for the sourcing queries that buyers actually type (e.g., "custom wire harness manufacturer", "EV harness prototyping", "IPC/WHMA-A-620 supplier"). The RFQ form must be the structural center of the site to facilitate the matching process.
