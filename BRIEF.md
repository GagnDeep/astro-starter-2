# Advisor CTO Brief

## The Business & Domain

- **Domain:** advisorcto.com
- **Business:** Fractional CTO practice serving RIA (Registered Investment Advisor) and wealth-management firms.

## The Buyer

- RIA principals running tech stacks such as Orion, Redtail, Wealthbox, or eMoney.
- Typically, these firms have nobody accountable for security, integration, or vendor risk.

## Monetization

- **Revenue Model:** Monthly retainers and fixed-scope engagements.
- **Constraints:** No vendor referral fees.

## Positioning & Constraints

- **Goal:** Establish a founder-credibility site.
- **Pages Needed:** Positioning, service definition, methodology, proof of track record, and a booking flow.
- **Constraint 1:** Do not fabricate credentials, clients, logos, or case studies. Leave clearly marked placeholders instead.
- **Constraint 2:** Content must survive being quoted on its own (H2/H3s answered in first forty to sixty words).
