# Institute of Retirement

**Domain:** instituteofretirement.com
**Business:** Disclosed-methodology comparison of countries for retiring abroad.
**Buyer:** Near-retirees comparing destination countries.
**Monetization:** Modest affiliate and lead generation (e.g., international movers, tax advisors).
**Positioning constraints:** Not a professional institute. The scoring methodology (cost of living, visa route, healthcare, tax treatment) must be published in full and honestly.
