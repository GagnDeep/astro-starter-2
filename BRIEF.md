# BRIEF

## The Business
AI & Investing is a reproducible, published back-testing platform for AI stock-picking tools. We provide transparent, data-driven analysis of AI investing products to help users evaluate their effectiveness.

## The Buyer
Target audience: Retail investors evaluating AI investing products. They are looking for objective, data-backed reviews rather than marketing hype.

## How it makes money
Affiliate commissions on the tools reviewed and recommended on the platform. We only recommend tools that pass our rigorous testing methodology.

## Positioning Constraints
- **Financial-promotion regulation applies**: This site must adhere to strict regulations.
- Every page must have a **not-investment-advice disclaimer**.
- We must maintain a **published and repeatable methodology**.
- **No performance claim** can be made without the reproducible test behind it.
- Head terms are locked out for 12-24 months, so the SEO strategy focuses entirely on **long-tail keywords**.
