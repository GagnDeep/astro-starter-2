# Institute of Humanoids — Project Brief

## Domain and Business
**Institute of Humanoids** is a specialized platform (instituteofhumanoids.com) dedicated to documenting the humanoid hardware supply chain. It serves as a reliable cross-reference of which components (actuators, harmonic drives, batteries, sensors, and compute) are supplied to which leading humanoid platforms. Additionally, the platform hosts a specialist hardware engineering job board.

## The Buyer
The target audience consists of component suppliers, hardware engineers working in humanoid robotics, and investors seeking verifiable data on the humanoid supply chain.

## Monetization
The platform generates revenue through:
- Sponsored corporate listings from component suppliers.
- Paid job postings for hardware engineering and robotics roles.

## Positioning Constraints
- **Not a News Feed:** The site is strictly a structured database of supplier relationships and job postings, deliberately avoiding the humanoid news space already covered by entities like *humanoid.press*.
- **Verifiability:** Every relationship documented must be verified through primary sources, official press releases, tear-down videos, or direct confirmation.
- **Editorial Tone:** Content is professional, objective, and focuses on structured hardware specifications rather than hype or speculation. No invented credentials, statistics, or profiles.
