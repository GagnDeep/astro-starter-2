# Institute of Underwriting — Project Brief

## Domain and Business
**Domain:** instituteofunderwriting.com
**Business:** A sponsorship-funded resource and newsletter for working insurance underwriters.

## The Buyer
Target audience: Commercial and specialty insurance underwriters.

## Monetization
Funded entirely by insurtech sponsorships.

## Positioning Constraints
- Must not be mistaken for a chartered professional body.
- The public brand reads as a trade publication.
- An explicit 'not a professional body, not an accrediting institute' statement must appear in the footer and on the about page.
